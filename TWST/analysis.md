## What it is
Synthetic-DNA manufacturer on a proprietary silicon-chip synthesis platform, in two businesses: DNA Synthesis & Protein Solutions (clonal genes, oligo pools, variant libraries, antibody/protein solutions for drug discovery) and NGS Applications (target-enrichment panels and library-prep kits for sequencing). Customers are research labs, biotech/pharma drug developers, diagnostics, agriculture and industrial users — ~2,650 customers in Q3 FY2026, with demand increasingly tied to AI-enabled drug discovery (therapeutics revenue +49% YoY in Q3 FY2026; an antibody-characterization collaboration feeding Eli Lilly's TuneLab AI platform).

A 5 gives CAP = 8.5 years.
