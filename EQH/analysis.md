## What it is
New York-based financial services holding company operating across the retirement value chain in three franchises (FY2025 10-K): Equitable, the insurer providing retirement, income and protection strategies (a leading registered index-linked and variable annuity manufacturer); AllianceBernstein ("AB"), the global active asset manager in which Equitable holds an approximate 68% economic interest; and Equitable Advisors, the wealth-management / advice distribution platform. Assets under management and administration were $1.1 trillion at December 31, 2025 (10-K) and $1,175 billion at June 30, 2026 (Q2 2026 release, search results). Latest quarter Q2 2026 (10-Q filed 2026-08-06): GAAP net loss attributable to Holdings of $453 million, or $1.68 per share, against non-GAAP operating earnings of $488 million, or $1.70 per share ($501 million / $1.75 excluding notable items) — the gap is non-economic hedge / market-risk-benefit revaluation, the recurring story in this name. Segment highlights in the same release: Retirement net inflows of $1.7 billion with first-year premiums up 13% year over year, AB net inflows of $0.8 billion, Wealth Management advisory net inflows of $2.0 billion, and $449 million returned to shareholders in the quarter. Two post-FY events dominate the standalone story: (1) the all-stock merger with Corebridge Financial announced March 2026 — each EQH share converts into 1.55516 shares of the new parent, combined company valued at approximately $22 billion at announcement, shareholders of both companies approved on July 30, 2026, close expected by end-2026, management guides to more than 10% earnings-per-share accretion on a run-rate basis by end-2028 (search results); (2) the sale of the Employee Benefits business (approximately $500 million of premiums, approximately 300 employees) to The Hartford, which closed October 13, 2026 (search results). Q3 2026 reports November 2, 2026.
