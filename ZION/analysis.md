## What it is
A Western US regional bank holding company (SIC 6021, National Commercial Banks; fundamentals.json profile): seven local bank brands (Zions Bank, California Bank & Trust, Nevada State Bank, etc.) with local management across 11 western states, serving retail, small-business and middle-market commercial customers. Revenue is ~90% net interest income; total assets $88.99B, equity $7.18B at FY2025 (financials_history.csv). Market cap $9.505B at $64.59 (nasdaq_quote_api) — in scope under $40B.

Latest print (Q3 2026, reported 2026-10-19, per earnings coverage): net earnings to common $262M, diluted EPS $1.78 vs $1.48 a year earlier; revenue $893M, +2.4% YoY; taxable-equivalent NII $684M, +2% YoY; NIM 3.23%, down 4bp sequentially on higher deposit costs; net charge-offs 2bp of average loans; average customer deposits +8.8% annualized. Management guided Q3 2027 NII +8% to +12% versus Q3 2026, assuming three rate increases through June 2027.

## Moat: 4/10 — weak-to-moderate
Justification: relationship-driven Western regional bank — seven local brands with local management, multi-generational middle-market relationships and SBA / public-finance niches give real deposit stickiness, but there is no structural cost or scale advantage versus the national banks, NIM is hostage to the rate cycle and deposit competition, and returns are ordinary bank returns. Evidence: fundamentals.json's `roic_proxy` is not computable for this filer (operating income is not filed — the series is null), so the return evidence is ROE 12.5% (FY2025), 12.0–18.5% across FY2022–25, and ROA ~0.8–1.0% — respectable bank returns earned on a leveraged balance sheet, not evidence of a wide moat. Gross margin is not a meaningful bank metric and fundamentals.json does not file one.
- FOR: seven local bank brands with local management across 11 western states — deep, often multi-generational middle-market relationships and switching costs; stable core deposit franchise in faster-growing Western markets (average customer deposits +8.8% annualized in Q3 2026, funding reduced wholesale borrowings); specialist niches with reputation — SBA lending and public finance advisory; disciplined credit culture (Q3 2026 net charge-offs 2bp of average loans, criticized/classified balances falling).
- AGAINST: no scale or technology advantage versus the national banks, and deposit pricing competition compresses NIM (3.23% in Q3 2026, −4bp sequentially); earnings are rate-cycle and credit-cycle hostages — revenue CAGR only ~4.4% over five years and ~90% of revenue is net interest income; Western commercial real estate and regional-economy concentration; heavier post-2023 regional-bank regulatory/liquidity scrutiny, and a $50M fraudulent-loan charge-off in the California operations surfaced in 2025 disclosures.
- A 4 gives CAP = 7.2 years (key_numbers.md): growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 7.2 years, 15-year projection.

## Flags — every fragility flag resolved
- **STALE_FY** (latest FY ended 9.6 months ago): the TTM was built with ttm.py and quoted in full above — and rejected with the distorting line named with its number (H1 OCF $845M vs $117M, a $728M swing), with both bases' IRR and tier recorded side by side. The median-of-3-FY base is used.
- **OE_VOLATILE** (OE/share range over the last 5 years spans >3x — FY2021 $2.47 to FY2022 $8.32 in the CSV): real, and structural for a bank — it is balance-sheet flow noise, not an inflection. Handled by the normalized median base and by widening the bear weight (super bear 15%, bear 30%).
- **CASH_INCLUDES_RESTRICTED**: the only FY2025 cash tag is the combined cash + restricted-cash total, $683M (financials_history.csv), so the engine's net debt of $3.893B is understated by whatever restricted amount is inside it. For a bank this net-debt figure is not a leverage measure in any case — deposits, the real funding, are not in the debt tags; capital is (see HIGH_NET_DEBT).
- **SECTOR_MODEL_MISMATCH** (Finance sector): the controlling flag. A bank's operating cash flow includes deposit and loan flows, so OE/capex DCF fits poorly; confidence is low, and the final tier is sized one notch down under Andrew's standing rule (2026-10-15, universal per the 2026-10-19 Q2 ruling) — see the verdict. The quantified gap behind the notch: the TTM OCF base ($11.21/share) prints Tier A at 21.65% while the EPS record (FY2023–25: $4.35 / $4.95 / $6.01) and even FY2025 net income ($899M vs $917M OE) sit at or below the $6.23 median base.
- **WIDE_SCENARIO_SPREAD** (super-bull IV 4.17x super-bear IV in key_numbers.md): real — the outcome range for a rate- and credit-sensitive bank is wide. Confidence is low, and the verdict is sized below the bear end.
- **HIGH_NET_DEBT** (net debt 4.25x normalized OE): the ratio is a misfit artefact in form — it counts $4.576B of borrowings against the $683M combined cash tag and ignores the deposit base that actually funds the bank — but leverage is real in substance: equity is $7.18B against $88.99B of assets (~8%). The filing-based capital picture from Q3 2026 coverage: Tier 1 leverage ratio 9.2%, total risk-based capital ratio 13.9% — adequately capitalized, not fortress. No separate adjustment beyond the misfit notch.
- **TIER_BAND** (bear tier C at 10.54% vs bull tier B at 16.89%): sized to the bear end and below it — the final tier is D after the misfit notch, under the bear case's own C.

## My thoughts / overlay
- The honest headline is the method range, and for a bank it is unusually wide: the same filings print Tier A (TTM OCF base, 21.65%) or Tier C (median base, 13.34%) depending only on which OCF window is trusted — before the misfit notch takes the final answer to D. Any single IRR quoted for ZION without its base is hiding that.
- On earnings rather than cash flow, the bank is fine but not cheap: FY2025 EPS $6.01 and a Q3 2026 adjusted run-rate around $1.74–1.78 a quarter against a $64.59 price is a low-double-digit earnings multiple for ~12.5% ROE, mid-single-digit revenue growth and a 3.23% NIM that is currently compressing. Tangible book value per share was $43.50 in Q3 2026 coverage — the price is roughly 1.5x tangible book.
- Credit is the current bright spot (2bp net charge-offs, falling criticized balances) and the guided NII recovery (+8–12% by Q3 2027) is the bull case's engine; both are cyclical gifts, not franchise proof. The $50M California fraudulent-loan charge-off is a reminder that regional-bank credit surprises arrive unannounced.
- Data note: fundamentals.json's `roic_proxy` and gross margin are null for this filer (bank filings carry no operating-income or COGS lines) — stated here rather than estimated. Enrichment files were generated in step 9; any failure there is noted in the data section of the company page, not here.
