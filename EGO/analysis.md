# EGO — Eldorado Gold Corporation
Engine 5.5 pass-1 run, 2026-10-20. Canadian gold and copper producer, 40-F filer reporting under IFRS in USD (SIC 1040, Gold and Silver Ores); common shares listed directly on the TSX (ELD) and NYSE (EGO). In scope (market cap est. $7.220B at $35.15, nasdaq_quote_api).

All figures below are copied from key_numbers.md, dcf_numbers.json, financials_history.csv or fundamentals.json — none are recomputed. Figures marked "search-cited" are from company releases and reporting found in web search; they are narrative context only and were NOT used in the scoring, because they are not in this run's code outputs.

## What it is
Mid-tier gold producer with operating mines at Lamaque (Quebec, Canada), Olympias (Greece), and Kisladag and Efemcukuru (Türkiye), plus two growth projects in commissioning: Skouries, a copper-gold porphyry in northern Greece (energized September 28, 2026, commercial production targeted for Q4 2026 — search-cited), and the McIlvenna Bay polymetallic project in Saskatchewan, added with the Foran Mining acquisition that closed in 2026 (search-cited). It sells a commodity at a price it does not set; the investment case at today's price is a gold-price and project-delivery case, not a current-earnings case.

## Moat: 3/10 — weak
Justification: a gold miner is a pure price taker — the only durable advantages are cost-curve position, reserve life and jurisdiction, and Eldorado's are weak-to-moderate. Evidence first (fundamentals.json): the **ROIC proxy** series runs 4.72% (FY2021), 0.96% (FY2022), 3.27% (FY2023), 7.34% (FY2024) and 14.88% (FY2025) — below a plausible cost of capital in four of the last five years, clearing it only in FY2025 at a $3,505/oz realized gold price (search-cited, FY2025 results). **Gross margin** over the same five years moved 30.8%, 19.7%, 26.6%, 38.3%, 48.5% — the expansion tracks the gold price, not pricing power the company controls.
- FOR: Skouries copper-gold porphyry: long mine life with copper byproduct credits guiding AISC to -$100 to $200/oz — a genuinely low-cost asset once in commercial production (Q4 2026 targeted); Diversified producing base — Lamaque (Canada), Olympias (Greece), Kisladag and Efemcukuru (Türkiye) — no single-mine dependence; Reserve scale and growth path: 25.5M oz proven and probable gold-equivalent reserves (company investor materials); guided production growth of ~41% by 2028 as Skouries and McIlvenna Bay ramp
- AGAINST: Pure gold price taker — no pricing power; margins are set by the gold price, not the company; ROIC below the cost of capital in four of the last five years (roic_proxy 4.72% FY2021, 0.96% FY2022, 3.27% FY2023, 7.34% FY2024); FY2025's 14.88% is a peak-gold print; Mid-to-high current cost position: consolidated AISC $1,926/oz in Q2 2026 at the operating mines; Reserves deplete — every ounce must be replaced via capex or M&A; FY2025 capex of $866.4M exceeded operating cash flow of $742.5M; Jurisdiction and permitting risk in Greece and Türkiye; Skouries was delayed for years and first concentrate slipped again to Q3 2026 with ~$50M of added construction capital; Dilution: diluted share count compounding at 3.8%/yr (engine); SBC nearly doubled in FY2025 to $20.2M
- A 3 gives CAP = 5.9 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 5.9 years, 15-year projection. Moot here — with no positive base there is nothing to fade.

## DCF — engine 5.5 final run (2026-10-20)
**No scenario table exists, by design.** The engine's data gate refuses to project a DCF off a non-positive normalized base: tier_by_numbers is F, f_subtype `no_cash_generation` (dcf_numbers.json basis: the sum of the last 3 fiscal years — the median window — is not positive), implied IRR and IV are null. Probabilities passed were the defaults (10/25/30/25/10); they change nothing when the base is negative. Inventing scenario IVs off a single positive year (FY2024, $0.24/share) would be exactly the fake-precision failure the gate exists to prevent.
- Growth sanity check (mandatory): the engine's growth blend is 23.92% (components: rev CAGR 5y 12.12% · rev CAGR 3y 27.76% · latest rev YoY 37.52%, key_numbers.md). Those are gold-price prints, not volume prints: FY2025 revenue grew 37.52% while production was essentially flat at 488,268 oz (search-cited) — the realized price did the work ($3,505/oz in FY2025, $4,379/oz in Q2 2026, both search-cited). The blend was not used; under Tier F no growth input can change the verdict.
- The honest quantitative statement the numbers support: at $35.15, the market is paying for sustained ~$4,000+ gold and a successful Skouries/McIlvenna Bay ramp — guided production growth of ~41% by 2028 (search-cited three-year outlook: 640,000–740,000 oz in 2028 vs 488,268 oz produced in 2025). On the normalized base there is no price at which a DCF can be run at all. A buyer is long gold and execution, not long owner's earnings.

One definitional trap, corrected from an earlier draft of this section: the CSV's ΔNWC (+$97,449,000 in FY2024) is the engine's balance-sheet-derived change in (current assets ex-cash) − (current liabilities ex-current debt); it is not the statement's working-capital line (+$20,554,000) and does not bridge net income to OCF — it belongs only to the maintenance-OE construction, whose formula subtracts it. The divergence is therefore the expected signature of a capital-heavy miner — non-cash D&A, a tax charge well above cash taxes paid, and derivative marks — not a tax-deposit or impairment one-off, and it does not contaminate the base: the conservative OE starts from OCF, not net income. Note the divergence cuts the other way for valuation comfort — net income was positive in both flagged years while conservative OE was negative or barely positive (FY2023 -$29,168,000; FY2024 $50,013,000), because capex, not earnings quality, is the binding constraint.

## My thoughts / overlay
- The F is the method working as intended on a build-phase miner: in the latest filed year capex ($866,362,000) exceeded operating cash flow ($742,509,000) outright, and in FY2023 capex ($401,870,000) likewise exceeded OCF ($382,897,000) (financials_history.csv) — which is why the engine's f_subtype basis records that the sum of the last 3 fiscal years' OE is not positive. The operating business, at filed numbers, does not yet fund its own growth, let alone return cash. Normalizing that away because the spending is "growth" capex is exactly what the conservative definition refuses to do for a depleting asset.
- What the F does NOT capture, stated plainly: (1) FY2025 capex is a peak, not a run rate — Skouries construction capital was guided at $175–185M for 2026 with commercial production in Q4 2026 (search-cited), after which the project's guided AISC of -$100 to $200/oz (copper credits) would make it the portfolio's cash engine at ~140,000 oz of gold and 67M lb of copper a year on average over its life (search-cited); (2) the gold price the base was earned at ($3,505/oz realized in FY2025, search-cited) is well below Q2 2026's $4,379/oz — at sustained $4,000+ gold the producing mines throw off materially more OCF than the median window shows, as Q2 2026's ex-growth-project free cash flow of +$40.9M (search-cited) already hints; (3) McIlvenna Bay (via Foran, closed 2026) is entirely outside the filed base window. Anyone re-underwriting this name on post-Skouries numbers will get a higher base — but building that base requires interim OE legs this run's tools do not produce for an IFRS filer, so it is flagged here rather than faked.
- Watch items: Skouries commissioning (first concentrate slipped to Q3 2026 with ~$50M of added construction capital — the project's history is a decade of delays, so the Q4 2026 commercial-production date is a claim to verify, not a fact); consolidated AISC discipline at the existing mines ($1,926/oz in Q2 2026, search-cited, against 2026 guidance of $1,670–$1,870/oz excluding Skouries — the guide was already being pressed before Skouries costs land); Greece/Türkiye jurisdiction risk on permits and taxes; and whether the share count keeps compounding at ~3.8%/yr now that the build is supposedly ending.
- Method range: there isn't one to quote — every defensible base this run can construct (3-yr median -$0.15; latest FY -$0.70; best single year FY2024 +$0.24) fails to support a DCF at $35.15. The verdict is robust to the base choice even though the business's forward run-rate may not be.

## Final verdict

- One-liner: Price-taking gold miner in a heavy build phase, moat 3/10 — Tier F: no positive normalized owner's earnings (3-yr median OE/share is -$0.15; OE positive in only 2 of the last 5 fiscal years, and FY2025 OE was -$144.1M as Skouries capex peaked), so the OE DCF produces no IV and no price clears a 15% hurdle on normalized numbers; the $35.15 price is a bet on sustained ~$4,000+ gold and Skouries delivering from Q4 2026, not on through-cycle earnings power.
