## What it is
End-to-end warehouse automation for retail/wholesale distribution centers: proprietary AI software directing fleets of autonomous robots in a modular high-density storage structure, sold as multi-year system deployments, plus a growing recurring software and operations-services layer on the installed base. Anchor customer is Walmart (systems across its regional distribution centers, plus the Walmart Advanced Systems & Robotics business acquired in FY2025); the GreenBox joint venture with SoftBank sells the platform as warehouse-as-a-service. Backlog was $22.5B at Q3 FY2026 per earnings reports. Revenue: $1,176,891,000 (FY2023), $1,788,179,000 (FY2024), $2,246,922,000 (FY2025, +25.65% YoY); Q3 FY2026 revenue grew +21.7% YoY with Q4 guided to $760–780M per earnings reports — growth is decelerating from hypergrowth to the low-20s.

## OVERRIDE — capex (filing-sourced, bounded authority)
- **The gap:** the preliminary engine run exited 4 with `DATA_GAP:capex` — from FY2025 Symbotic reports capex only on a combined line (property-and-equipment purchases plus capitalization of internal-use software costs) that no engine candidate tag reads, so the FY2025 annual capex fact was absent and no tier was emitted.
- **The decision:** judgment.json carries `capex_override = {value: 79030000, kind: "filing", source: "Symbotic FY2025 Form 10-K (filed 2025-11-24), consolidated statements of cash flows / free-cash-flow reconciliation, year ended September 27, 2025: 'Purchases of property and equipment and capitalization of internal use software development costs' $79,030 thousand"}`. This is the filing's own capex definition — it is the line subtracted in the 10-K's free-cash-flow reconciliation. Its FY2024 comparative on the same combined line is $44,374 thousand vs the $42,237 thousand PPE-only figure the engine's tag captured, so the combined line is also the more complete measure for earlier years. The override fills only periods where the tag is absent (overrides_applied periods_filled: 2025-09-27 and a 2021 stub period); filed values are never replaced.
- **Measured impact (dcf_numbers.json override_impact):** on the engine-native median base the override moves nothing — base OE/share $0.95 zero-fill → $0.95 with override, weighted IRR 12.45% → 12.45% — because FY2025 is not the median year. On the figures that do use FY2025 it matters: latest FY OE/share $6.29 (zero-fill) → $5.56, and the TTM base $4.65 (pre-override ttm.py, which had also mis-timed its capex legs to FY2024) → $4.41. Its binding effect is binary: without it there is no tier at all (unblocks_tier).

## Moat: 5/10 — moderate / narrow
Justification: proprietary integrated warehouse-automation system with 25–30yr installed life and very high switching costs once embedded, anchored by Walmart across its regional distribution centers and a $22.5B backlog, but customer concentration is extreme, GAAP profitability is only just emerging, SBC is large, and AutoStore/Ocado/legacy integrators contest adjacent use cases.
- FOR: Proprietary end-to-end case-picking system (AI software + robot fleet + modular storage) with a clear lead in US retail distribution centers; installed systems designed for 25–30yr life and embedded in the distribution center itself — replacement means redesigning the facility, so switching costs are very high; Walmart anchor deployment under a long-term master agreement plus the GreenBox (SoftBank JV) warehouse-as-a-service channel; $22.5B contracted backlog at Q3 FY2026 giving multi-year revenue visibility; growing recurring software and operations-services revenue (Q3 FY2026 software +57% YoY, services +49% YoY per earnings reports).
- AGAINST: Extreme customer concentration — Walmart and GreenBox account for the vast majority of backlog and revenue; no proven through-cycle profitability (GAAP net income still negative in FY2025, only thinly positive in FY2026 quarters, negative historical ROIC); heavy SBC ($183,936,000 in FY2025, $234,533,000 TTM per ttm.py) is a real, recurring dilution cost; competition from AutoStore, Ocado and legacy integrators in adjacent fulfillment use cases — the technology lead must be re-earned; project business — revenue and cash flow are lumpy by deployment timing and customer capex cycles drive starts.
- A 5 gives CAP = 8.5 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 8.5 years, 15-year projection.

## Flags — every fragility flag resolved
- **DATA_GAP:capex**: resolved by the filing-sourced override — see OVERRIDE. The gap was a tagging change (combined PPE + capitalized-software line from FY2025), not missing economics.
- **OVERRIDE_APPLIED:capex**: see OVERRIDE — gap, source, and measured impact are recorded in judgment.json, dcf_numbers.json (overrides_applied / override_impact) and the registry.
- **STALE_FY** (FY ended 12.6 months ago): TTM was computed by ttm.py through Q3 FY2026 and is quoted in full above — and then deliberately NOT used as the base, because its OCF leg is the prepayment-inflated series (see BASE_DIVERGENCE) and its capex legs are override prorations rather than filed splits. The flag is resolved by examination, not by pinning.
- **BASE_DIVERGENCE** (latest FY $5.56 vs median $0.95): BOTH are distorted in opposite directions. The latest/TTM figures are inflated by deferred-revenue prepayments (FY2025 OCF $866,939,000 against net income of −$16,937,000); the median is a backward FY2023-level figure for a business that has since roughly doubled revenue. The median was kept as the headline base (conservative end) and the spread between the two is reported as the method range.
- **NI_OCF_DIVERGENCE** (2023, 2024, 2025): explained, not a one-off gain — non-cash SBC addbacks ($154,227,000 / $112,208,000 / $183,936,000 across FY2023–25, financials_history.csv) plus deferred-revenue and other working-capital swings drive OCF away from net income in both directions (FY2024 OCF was −$58,077,000 against a −$13,490,000 net loss). Consequence: the OCF-based conservative OE inherits the prepayment cycle, which is exactly why no single-year OE figure here is trustworthy as a base.
- **DILUTION** (share count +26.10%/yr): real and structural — SPAC/Up-C share-class evolution plus heavy SBC issuance; the current-YTD diluted count in ttm.py (131,666,538) already exceeds the FY2025 count (108,670,159). The blend already deducts 5pp for it; SBC is deducted in full in the OE definition; the residual risk is that per-share economics lag total-company economics, which the bearish probability skew covers. Note the per-share math throughout follows the filings' Class A diluted denominator, as the engine standardizes it.
- **BASIS_BREAK_SUSPECT**: no divestiture or restatement break in the operating series was found. The >60% revenue jumps are organic hypergrowth off a tiny base (revenue $92M in FY2020 on the filed history) plus the FY2025 acquisition of Walmart's Advanced Systems & Robotics business, which deepens rather than breaks the core deployment story. The CAGRs are still not trusted as forward growth — see the sanity check; forward starts were set as OE-leverage rates with the revenue prints stated alongside.
- **WIDE_SCENARIO_SPREAD** (4.21x): real — the outcome range IS wide because the OE base itself is unstable. Confidence is low, and the verdict is sized to the conservative end.

## My thoughts / overlay
- The honest headline is the method range, and it is unusually wide: the same filing data defensibly produces Tier D (median base with revenue-like OE growth starts of −5/0/10/18/25%: weighted IRR 6.27%, weighted IV@15% $10.55), the final Tier C (median base, default offsets: 12.45%, $28.44), and Tier S (TTM base, default offsets: weighted IRR 34.14%, weighted IV@15% $132.64). Anyone quoting one number for SYM without its base is hiding the ball — the entire disagreement is whether customer prepayments are earnings.
- On the economics, they mostly are not — yet. GAAP net income was negative in FY2023, FY2024 and FY2025, operating income was −$115,006,000 in FY2025 (financials_history.csv), and SBC alone ($234,533,000 TTM) exceeds any plausible current measure of true operating profit. What is real: revenue scale, the $22.5B backlog, gross-margin expansion, a growing recurring layer, and $1,244,993,000 of cash. The equity case is a margin-maturation story that has not yet shown up in GAAP earnings.
- Balance sheet: cash-rich but liability-heavy in a specific way — the dominant liability is deferred revenue (customer prepayments for undelivered systems), which is a delivery obligation, not free surplus. A starts slowdown would hit OCF first and hardest, which is the super-bear story.
- Valuation context from earnings coverage: the stock trades at very high multiples of current earnings (trailing P/E in the hundreds per Q3 FY2026 coverage), so the market is already paying for the bull path; the DCF disagrees only on how much of the prepayment cash counts today.
