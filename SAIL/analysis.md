## What it is
The category leader in identity governance and administration (IGA): SailPoint's platform answers "who — human, machine, or AI agent — should have access to what" for large enterprises: access certification campaigns, lifecycle provisioning, and policy enforcement, layered on top of whichever identity anchor (Microsoft Entra, Okta) the customer runs. Founded 2005, taken private by Thoma Bravo in 2022, re-IPO'd February 2025 (Thoma Bravo retains a majority — a controlled company). ~3,235 customers per company filings. The business is mid-transition from term licenses to SaaS: Q2 FY2027 (reported 2026-09-09) total ARR $1.231B, +25% YoY; SaaS ARR $847M, +36%, 97% of net new ARR; AI/agentic-identity ARR above $70M; dollar-based net retention 113%, gross retention high-90s; FY2027 ARR guidance raised to $1.38B with ~$200M free cash flow guided. In June 2026 it closed the Entro Security acquisition (non-human identity; ~$200M per press reports, terms undisclosed).

## Moat: 5/10 — moderate/narrow, leadership real, economics unproven
Justification: SailPoint is the recognized leader in identity governance and administration (IGA) — the governance layer enterprises buy on top of whichever identity anchor (Microsoft Entra, Okta) they run — with compliance-mandated switching costs: access-certification and lifecycle workflows are embedded in regulated-industry audit processes and implementations run for years. Retention confirms it: dollar-based net retention 113% and gross retention in the high 90s (Q2 FY2027 reporting), ARR $1.231B growing 25%, SaaS ARR +36% to $847M. But there is no demonstrated return on capital: operating income is negative in every filed year, conservative owner's earnings are negative 0 of 3 filed years and on TTM, and the roic_proxy series in fundamentals.json (+34.9% FY2024, +3.2% FY2025, -3.7% FY2026) is denominator noise from the Thoma Bravo LBO (negative book equity pre-IPO) and the February 2025 re-IPO recapitalization, with D&A of $211-264M/yr dominated by amortization of acquisition intangibles. Gross margin (60.5% FY2024 → 64.5% FY2025 → 64.5% FY2026 → 66.2% TTM) is improving but mid-pack for SaaS, not an elite pricing-power signature. Microsoft bundling Entra governance into M365 is the standing erosion force, with Okta, CyberArk (now Palo Alto Networks), Saviynt and AI-native entrants converging on the same agentic-identity ground. ## My thoughts / overlay
- There is no method range to hide behind: normalized-median base -$1.71/share, latest-FY-only -$0.35/share, TTM -$0.08/share, maintenance-definition FY2026 -$98,120,000. Every defensible variant is Tier F. Anyone quoting a DCF value for SAIL today is projecting SBC normalization, not measuring owner's earnings.
- The trajectory is genuinely close to the flip, and the flip condition is precise: TTM free cash flow is already +$194.9M and management guides ~$200M FCF for FY2027 — owner's earnings turn positive the moment SBC falls below OCF − capex. SBC is already drifting the right way ($254.9M FY2026 → $238.2M TTM, and H1 FY2027 SBC of $137,426,000 vs $154,061,000 in H1 FY2026 per ttm.json legs), but at ~20% of revenue it has a long way to fall, and the post-IPO share count is still rising ~4%/yr while it does.
- The franchise evidence is real: ARR +25%, SaaS ARR +36%, NRR 113%, gross margin expanding to 66.2% TTM, net cash balance sheet after the IPO repaid the LBO debt. What the $18.75 price ($10.203B market cap, ~8.8x TTM revenue of $1,165,544,000 per fundamentals.json) is paying for is the agentic-identity option — AI-driven ARR above $70M, a $200M+ pipeline, the Entro deal — in a category where Microsoft gives a competing answer away inside M365. That is a growth-equity bet, not an owner's-earnings discount; this screen prices the second and finds nothing to price.
- What would change the tier: one filed period — FY2027's 10-K (expected March 2027) or a TTM window before it — in which OCF − capex − SBC prints positive. On current legs the gap is only $43.3M TTM, so a refresh on the FY2027 10-K is the trigger; the 3-yr median base will lag the flip by years, so a positive latest-year print should prompt an immediate re-score rather than waiting for the queue.
