What it is

Newark-based holding company for PSE&G, New Jersey's largest regulated electric and gas utility (~2.4M electric, ~1.9M gas customers per search results), PSEG Power (merchant generation dominated by nuclear — Salem and Hope Creek in NJ plus a stake in Peach Bottom, PA, ~3,758 MW in total), and PSEG Long Island (operations provider for LIPA). The regulated business is the earnings anchor: a $22.5–$25.5B regulated capex plan through 2030, rate base guided to a 6–7.5% CAGR, and a planned base-rate filing by year-end 2026. Management reaffirmed 2026 non-GAAP operating EPS guidance of $4.28–$4.40 with Q2 2026 results and a long-term operating-EPS growth target of 6–8% through 2030 (search results).
