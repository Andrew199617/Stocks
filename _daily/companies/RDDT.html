What it is

The community platform: 100,000+ interest-based communities (subreddits) with volunteer moderation and user-generated content, 130.3M daily active uniques and 514.6M weekly active uniques in Q2 2026. Revenue is ~95% advertising ($762M of $805M in Q2 2026, +64% YoY); the rest is "other" — data licensing of its human-conversation corpus to AI labs (Google deal signed 2024 at a reported ~$60M/yr; OpenAI at a reported similar scale) plus premium. Asset-light: 91.3% gross margin in Q2 2026, capex ~0.2% of revenue. Public only since the March 2024 IPO ($34), so EDGAR history is 4 fiscal years (2022–2025) and OE-positive in exactly one of them — the entire analysis turns on whether the FY2025/TTM inflection is the new base. It is: Q1 2026 revenue +69% YoY, Q2 +61% (the 8th straight quarter above 60%), net income $253M in Q2 alone, TTM operating cash flow over $1B. Q3 2026 guided to $860–870M revenue (~+47–49% YoY) and $385–395M adjusted EBITDA.

MOAT — score 6/10 (moderate-strong, not wide)

FOR — (1) A genuinely unique corpus: two decades of authentic, organized human conversation across 100k+ self-moderated communities, now scarce enough that AI labs pay to license it; users append "reddit" to Google searches by name — demand pull few platforms have. (2) Community network effects with free labor: subreddits, norms, and volunteer moderators are expensive to replicate and the archive compounds. (3) Emerging pricing power: global ARPU +36% YoY in Q2 2026 (U.S. +51%) — monetization compounding far faster than users (DAUq +18%). (4) Structural economics: ~91% gross margin, near-zero capex, $1M+ revenue per employee. (5) International runway: machine translation into 30+ languages; intl revenue +84% YoY in Q2. AGAINST — (1) Distribution is rented, not owned: 77.7M of 130.3M DAUq are logged-out, much arriving via Google search; Google AI Overviews increasingly answer without a click, U.S. logged-in DAUq growth is nearly flat, the stock fell ~21% after the Q2 2026 beat on exactly this concern, and Reddit is reportedly weighing blocking Google's crawler as the 2024 licensing deal comes up for renewal — its biggest traffic source and a licensing customer are the same counterparty. (2) No lock-in: users multi-home across TikTok/YouTube/Discord/X; switching costs for readers are zero. (3) Ad business subscale vs Meta/Google; intl ARPU $2.26 vs U.S. $11.85 — the convergence is a hope, not a print. (4) Data-licensing revenue concentrated in a few AI counterparties with uncertain renewals. (5) SBC load: $338M TTM, ~33% of OCF. (6) Dependence on volunteer moderators (the 2023 API protests showed the community can revolt). CAP = clamp(2 + 1.3 × 6) = 9.8 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over the CAP, 15-year projection.
