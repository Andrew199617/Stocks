What it is

A specialty property & casualty insurance holding company (founded 1967, Greenwich, CT) operating through dozens of decentralized operating units in two segments — Insurance, and Reinsurance & Monoline Excess. It writes commercial specialty lines (liability, professional lines, workers' comp, commercial auto, private-client personal lines) where underwriting skill matters more than scale, collects premiums up front, and invests the float — $33,173,381,000 of net invested assets at FY2025 (Q4/FY2025 results release). Q2 2026: gross premiums written $4,144,000,000 (a record), combined ratio 90.0%, record net investment income $418,700,000, operating income $497,145,000 ($1.27/diluted share vs $1.05 a year earlier).
