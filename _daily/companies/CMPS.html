What it is

Pre-revenue, single-asset biotechnology company (London HQ, Nasdaq-listed ADSs) developing COMP360, a proprietary synthetic psilocybin formulation, as a supervised, in-clinic treatment for treatment-resistant depression (TRD). Two Phase 3 TRD trials (COMP005, COMP006) met their primary endpoints — 39% of COMP006 participants achieved a ≥25% MADRS reduction at Week 6, with benefit maintained on average through Week 26. A rolling NDA submission is underway with final modules expected Q4 2026 and a targeted U.S. launch in H1 2027 — subject to FDA approval and DEA/state rescheduling of a Schedule I substance. A late-stage PTSD program is the only other material pipeline leg. FDA Breakthrough Therapy designation and a Commissioner's National Priority Review voucher shorten the review clock but do not guarantee approval. There has never been any product revenue: revenue is $0 in every fiscal year in the EDGAR history.

My thoughts / overlay

  • This is the screen working as intended: CMPS is not an owner's-earnings investment at any price — with negative OE in every period, there is no price at which the OE DCF clears a 15% hurdle, and no margin-of-safety price to quote. Anyone buying at $11.42 is buying the probability-weighted pipeline: FDA decision on a rolling NDA completing Q4 2026, DEA rescheduling, payer coverage for a supervised multi-hour session, and uptake against Spravato's installed base and incoming Lilly/AbbVie-backed rivals.
