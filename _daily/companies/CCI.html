What it is

The largest US tower REIT, and — since May 1, 2026 — a pure-play one: ~40,000 US towers leased to the big wireless carriers under long-term contracts with ~3% annual escalators (weighted-average remaining tenant-contract term ~6 years; $23.7B of contracted amounts at Dec 31, 2025). On May 1, 2026 it closed the sale of its Fiber Solutions and Small Cell businesses for ~$8.4B net proceeds, then repurchased $1.0B of shares and repaid more than $7B of debt (Q2 2026 release, via search). What remains is a slow-growth, high-payout rental business: Q2 2026 site rental revenues $967M (−4% YoY), Adjusted EBITDA $675M (−4%), AFFO $488M / $1.13 per share (+11%). FY2026 outlook (continuing operations only): site rental revenues $3,855M (−5% vs 2025), Adjusted EBITDA $2,690M (−6%), AFFO $1,975M / $4.59 per share (+4% / +5%). Dividend reset to $4.25/yr (secondary reporting of the sale announcement).
