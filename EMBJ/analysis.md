## What it is
Brazilian aerospace manufacturer (20-F filer, CIK 1355444): Commercial Aviation (E175, E190-E2, E195-E2 regional jets), Executive Aviation (Phenom, Praetor), Defense & Security (C-390 Millennium, A-29 Super Tucano) and Services & Support, plus the Eve eVTOL subsidiary. It deliberately does not compete head-on with Airbus/Boeing in large narrowbodies; it dominates smaller regional, light/midsize executive and tactical airlift niches. Q2 2026 (company earnings release, via web search): revenue $2.24B, +22.9% YoY, 65 deliveries, firm order backlog a record $34.5B, +16% YoY (Commercial $15.1B, Executive $7.8B, Defense $6.1B, +42% YoY, Services $5.5B); management raised 2026 adjusted EBIT margin guidance to 10.0-10.6% and adjusted FCF (ex-Eve) guidance to at least $400M. Q3 2026 deliveries were 66 aircraft.

## Moat: 5/10 — moderate / narrow
Justification: Narrow-to-moderate aerospace niche moat: Embraer is the only in-production scope-compliant 76-seat regional jet maker (E175) and holds strong executive-jet (Phenom/Praetor) and growing C-390 defense franchises with a record $34.5B backlog and a large installed base feeding Services & Support, but gross margin has stayed thin at 17.3-18.0% in FY2023-2025 (15.7-20.1% over FY2021-2025, fundamentals.json) and returns have not stayed above the cost of capital through a cycle — roic_proxy was negative in 2017, 2019, 2020 and 2022, near zero in 2018, and only strong in FY2023-2025 (17.6% / 23.9% / 25.8%). Scale disadvantage versus Airbus/Boeing, A220 overlap at the top of the E2 range, and the US scope clause blocking the E175-E2 cap the score at 5. CAP 8.5y.
- FOR: Only in-production scope-compliant 76-seat regional jet (E175) — dominant in US regional fleets with no direct in-production rival in that class; diversified portfolio across Commercial, Executive, Defense & Security and Services & Support smoothing any single cycle, with firm backlog a record $34.5B in Q2 2026 (+16% YoY); Executive Aviation franchise (Phenom 300 family, Praetor) with Q2 2026 revenue +32% YoY and adj. EBIT margin 23.4%; installed base of 9,000+ aircraft feeding recurring Services & Support revenue (Q2 2026 +24% YoY, adj. EBIT margin 18.7%); C-390 Millennium defense momentum (Defense backlog +42% YoY to $6.1B in Q2 2026) and NATO/export adoption.
- AGAINST: Thin, cyclical economics (gross margin 17.5% in FY2025; roic_proxy negative or near-zero in 2017-2022 before recovering in 2023-2025 — no through-cycle pricing power proven); scale disadvantage versus Airbus and Boeing; Airbus A220 overlaps the E195-E2; US scope clauses (76 seats / 86,000 lb MTOW) block the more efficient E175-E2 from the largest regional market, development paused toward 2027-2029; customer/program concentration, supply-chain/GTF engine exposure, Brazilian political/FX overhang and Eve eVTOL cash burn.
- Evidence (fundamentals.json): gross margin FY2021 15.7%, FY2022 20.1%, FY2023 17.3%, FY2024 18.0%, FY2025 17.5%. roic_proxy FY2021 16.6% (on a small positive operating income against a depressed capital base — net income was still negative), FY2022 -8.7%, FY2023 17.6%, FY2024 23.9%, FY2025 25.8%; earlier years include -24.6% in FY2020. A moat of 7+ would require that return profile to have held through a cycle; it has not.
- A 5 gives CAP = 8.5 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 8.5 years, 15-year projection.
