## What it is
Specialty discount retailer running a treasure-hunt format aimed at tweens, teens and their parents ("the Kid and the Kid in all of us"): most items at $5 or less, plus a "Five Beyond" tier above $5. Ended fiscal 2026 (Jan 31, 2026) with 1,921 stores in 46 states after 150 net openings; 2,022 stores by Q2 fiscal 2027. Under CEO Winnie Park (in place since late 2024) the company executed a customer-centric reset — trend-right curated assortments, better in-stock, a peak-hours store labor model — and comps inflected from the fiscal 2024–25 trough to +12.8% in fiscal 2026 and +18.3% in H1 fiscal 2027, five straight double-digit comp quarters.

## Moat: 5/10 — moderate/narrow
Justification: a differentiated treasure-hunt value format with real brand resonance for its kid/tween niche, 2,022 stores and proven new-store economics — but no switching costs, a copyable trend-driven assortment, heavyweight competition, and imported-merchandise tariff exposure. Evidence first: return on invested capital (fundamentals.json `roic_proxy`: after-tax operating income over equity + debt − cash) ran 28.1% / 29.3% / 40.2% / 30.8% / 20.3% / 27.4% / 25.2% / 20.6% / 16.4% / 23.2% across FY2017–FY2026 — well above the cost of capital in every year including the trough — and gross margin held in a 34.9–36.5% band for the decade (FY2021 dip 33.2% aside). Caveat: the proxy excludes lease liabilities from invested capital, so it flatters the level; the durability through a margin trough is the part that is real. That evidence supports a genuine but narrow moat (5), not a 7+: returns are earned on merchandising execution that must be re-won each season, and comps round-tripped from negative to +18% within three years.
- FOR: differentiated treasure-hunt format and brand aimed at tweens/teens — customers come for discovery, not staples; decade of ROIC well above the cost of capital with gross margin stable at ~35–36%; scale and new-store machine (2,022 stores, ~150 net openings/yr, 8.5% unit growth in FY2026); traffic-led comps — five straight double-digit comp quarters (Q2 FY2027 +14.1% on +13.6% transactions).
- AGAINST: no switching costs, copyable assortment; competition for the same discretionary dollar (Dollar Tree/Dollar General, Walmart, Target, TJX, Amazon, Temu/Shein); tariff exposure on imported merchandise — tariff costs depressed FY2025 earnings and the Q2 FY2027 refund one-off shows the exposure cuts both ways; comp volatility (negative in the trough, +18.3% at the peak); "Five Beyond" price creep risks diluting the value promise.
- A 5 gives CAP = 8.5 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 8.5 years, 15-year projection.

## My thoughts / overlay
- The honest headline is the **method range**, and for once it is one-directional: on the engine's median base with default scenarios (preliminary run) weighted IRR was 5.81% with IV@15% $49.88 (Tier D); on the pinned latest-FY base, 9.28% / $110.97 (Tier D); on the raw TTM base with identical scenarios, 12.23% / $166.86 (Tier C). Every defensible base — including one that capitalizes a one-time tariff refund — leaves the stock short of the 15% hurdle at $222.72. The market has already paid for the turnaround; what remains is execution risk the buyer is not compensated for.
- **Override impact (measured, from the runs above):** the base_override moves the base from the engine-native $2.6376 to $6.8006 (+157.8%) and the weighted IRR from 5.81% (preliminary default-scenario run) to 9.28% on the final scenario set — large, and it still does not change the tier. Without the override the name would print a trough-company verdict; with it, a fairly-priced-recovery verdict. Tier D either way.
- Quality is not the debate: a debt-free balance sheet (leases aside), a decade of ROIC above the cost of capital, gross margin that never broke, SBC a modest $34.7M (5.9% of OCF in FY2026), buybacks resumed (Q2 fiscal 2027: ~311,000 shares for ~$60.0M per the Q2 release), share count essentially flat (−0.18%/yr).
- Risks: comps are running at unsustainable levels (+18.3% H1) against the anniversary of the reset — guidance already implies a hard H2 deceleration; tariff policy is a live, two-sided exposure on an imported assortment (costs hurt FY2025, a refund flattered Q2 FY2027 — neither is run-rate); new-store returns must hold as the base passes 2,000 stores; "Five Beyond" price creep vs the value promise; a consumer downturn hits discretionary treasure-hunt spending first.
- Margin of safety: there is none at this price on owner's earnings — the weighted IV@15% is half the price, and even the super-bull IV@15% ($212.61) is below it. A buyer at $222.72 needs the super-bull path merely to approach a 15% return (super-bull IRR 14.54%).
