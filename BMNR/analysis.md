## What it is
Formerly a small immersion-cooling Bitcoin mining and mining-equipment company. On June 30, 2025 it pivoted to an Ethereum treasury strategy under chairman Thomas "Tom" Lee: issue equity, buy ETH every week, and stake it. It is now the world's largest Ethereum treasury and the second-largest crypto treasury globally behind Strategy (search results): about 6,016,414 ETH at $2,726 per ETH as of October 4, 2026, 4.9% of the ETH supply (of 122.1 million ETH), plus 214 Bitcoin, stakes in Beast Industries and Eightco Holdings, and total cash & marketable securities of $643 million — total crypto, cash and marketable securities holdings of $17.4 billion (search results). 5,067,309 ETH were staked via its MAVAN (Made in America Validator Network) platform, with annualized staking revenues of about $358 million cited by the company (search results). Management's stated target is 5% of ETH supply — the "Alchemy of 5%" — and Lee has said accumulation stops at that cap (search results). The legacy operating business is immaterial by comparison: FY2025 revenue was $6.095M (financials_history.csv).

## My thoughts / overlay
- This is the model's known blind spot, flagged as such — with a twist: unlike a regulated utility, there is no hidden earnings engine the OE screen is missing. The operating business earned $6.095M of revenue in FY2025; everything else is a financed ETH position. An investor here owns ETH exposure assembled by continuous equity issuance: the bet pays only if ETH appreciates faster than the share count grows (weighted diluted shares 24,515,226 in FY2025 → 443,947,604 in the current YTD period) and if the shares hold a premium to NAV that lets issuance stay accretive per share. At about 1.0x mNAV (search results) that accretion engine is already switched off — issuance at NAV adds ETH and shares in proportion and compounds nothing per share.
- The staking build-out is the one path to real cash earnings: 5,067,309 ETH staked with about $358M of annualized staking revenue cited by the company (search results) against TTM revenue of $61,194,000 actually filed through 2026-05-31 (fundamentals.json) — the gap between the run-rate claim and the filed numbers is itself a flag to watch. Even at the claimed run rate, staking income would need to cover a cost and SBC base that produced TTM SBC of $39,463,000 (ttm.json) and deeply negative operating cash flow before it creates positive owner's earnings.
- Method range: on the v5 method there is one result, F — the normalized base, the latest FY, and TTM are all negative, so no defensible base choice spans tiers. An NAV model would price the stock off its $17.4B of holdings (search results) rather than earnings, but that is a different model, out of scope for this sweep, and it would still not be a 15%-hurdle owner's-earnings case.
- Hurdle-clearing price: not computable from this model — with no positive OE base there is no IV@15% at any price, and stating one would require an NAV/ETH-price model, which is out of scope for this sweep. No tier override: the numbers tier F is retained, with the mismatch documented, rather than inventing a tier the v5 method cannot support.
- Real risks for a holder (outside the model): a sustained ETH drawdown (TTM net income was -$8,754,771,609 on marks), loss of the NAV premium closing the accretive-issuance window, staking slashing/regulatory risk on MAVAN, and the sheer pace of dilution.
