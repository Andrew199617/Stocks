## What it is
The largest U.S. mortgage REIT: an internally managed, REIT-elected capital manager that borrows short (repo and other secured financing) and owns long mortgage assets across three groups — Annaly Agency Group (Agency MBS, $92.9B of the $104.7B portfolio at YE2025), Annaly Residential Credit Group (non-Agency whole loans and securitized products), and Annaly Mortgage Servicing Rights Group (MSR; long-term subservicing/MSR purchase agreement with PennyMac entered October 2025). The economics are a levered spread: asset yield minus funding and hedging cost, at GAAP leverage 7.2:1 / economic leverage 5.6:1 at YE2025 (10-K). Q2 2026 (latest quarter): earnings available for distribution (EAD) $0.79/share vs a raised $0.75 quarterly dividend, book value $20.15/share, economic return 5.5% for the quarter (company Q2 2026 release, via search).

## Moat: 3/10 — weak
Justification (judgment.json): scale ($135.6B assets, largest U.S. mortgage REIT), deep repo/swap market access and a diversified three-engine platform are real but replicable — the assets are commodity securities anyone with financing can buy, spreads are market-set, and there is no pricing power.
- FOR: scale and liquidity ($9.4B of total assets available for financing at YE2025, including $6.1B cash and unencumbered Agency MBS, per the 10-K MD&A); diversified Agency / Residential Credit / MSR platform whose engines partly offset each other across rate cycles (MSR values rise when Agency MBS falls); internally managed, no external-manager fee drag; 25+ years through multiple rate cycles.
- AGAINST: no pricing power — Agency spreads and repo costs are set by the market; structural leverage (GAAP 7.2:1, economic 5.6:1 at YE2025) makes book value hostage to rate/spread moves; persistent ATM dilution (share count +15.9%/yr in the engine history; diluted weighted count 494,541,323 in FY2023 → 641,042,741 in FY2025 → 732,317,721 current-YTD in ttm.json); dividend cut in 2023 and 5-yr DPS CAGR −5.1% (fundamentals.json).
- **ROIC evidence (required check):** fundamentals.json reports roic_proxy as null for every year (Annaly files no operating-income tag) and gross margin as null (no revenue tag) — the standardized ROIC/margin series does not exist for this filer. The return series that does exist is ROE: −14.6% (2023), +7.9% (2024), +12.6% (2025) (fundamentals.json annual) — sign-flipping and rate-driven, i.e. no demonstrated through-cycle return above the cost of capital. That is why the moat is 3 and cannot be argued higher on this evidence.
- A 3 gives CAP = 5.9 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 5.9 years, 15-year projection.
