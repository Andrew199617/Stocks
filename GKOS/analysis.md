## What it is
Ophthalmic medical technology and pharmaceuticals company (SIC 3841, 1,094 employees per profile.json) built around interventional glaucoma: the iStent MIGS family, iDose TR (sustained-release intracameral travoprost implant, launched February 2024), and Corneal Health — Photrexa transitioning to Epioxa (first FDA-approved epithelium-on corneal cross-linking therapy for keratoconus, launched 2026). Q2 2026 mix (company reporting): consolidated net sales $185.6M, +~50% YoY — U.S. glaucoma $118.5M (+64%, iDose TR ~$74M), international glaucoma $36.6M (+17%), Corneal Health $30.4M (+48%, Epioxa ~$11M in its first full quarter). Management raised FY2026 revenue guidance to $680M–$700M after Q2 (from $600M–$620M at the FY2025 release), with FY2026 operating expenses guided to ~$600M.

The franchise is hostage to Medicare coverage/reimbursement decisions, faces Alcon/Sight Sciences/AbbVie competition, and pays for growth with rising SBC ($63,218,000 in FY2025, $80,222,000 TTM).
- FOR: Interventional-glaucoma category leadership — iStent family is the largest MIGS franchise (over one million devices implanted worldwide, deep peer-reviewed evidence base), iStent infinite extends it to standalone use; iDose TR, first sustained-release intracameral drug implant, with re-administration creating a recurring-revenue refill model (~$74M of Q2 2026 sales, U.S. glaucoma +64% YoY); Epioxa, first FDA-approved epithelium-on cross-linking therapy (~$11M in its first full quarter, permanent J-code effective July 1, 2026); pricing power (GAAP gross margin 76.0%/75.5% in FY2023/24, non-GAAP ~84% FY2025, ~85% Q2 2026); pipeline breadth — five platforms / 13 programs plus a trained surgeon base that is costly for rivals to replicate.

## My thoughts / overlay
- There is no method range to hide behind: normalized median base -$2.23/share, latest-FY base -$1.50/share, TTM base -$1.58/share, maintenance base far lower at -$235,119,000 on the FY2025 working-capital build. Every defensible variant is Tier F. Anyone quoting a DCF value for GKOS today is projecting the inflection, not measuring owner's earnings.
- The inflection is real on the top line and in cash trajectory: OCF improved from -$61,318,000 (FY2024) to -$14,789,000 (FY2025) to -$950,000 TTM, FCF from -$67,618,000 to -$22,455,000 to -$12,196,000 TTM (fundamentals.json), on revenue +32% (FY2025), +41.55% TTM, +~50% in Q2 2026 with guidance raised to $680M–$700M. But the same TTM shows exactly what still eats the business: SBC of $80,222,000 against OCF of -$950,000. Even at OCF breakeven, SBC alone puts owner's earnings ~$80M underwater; opex guided to ~$600M for 2026 says management is still spending ahead of the ramp.
- What would change the tier: a filed period in which OCF − capex − SBC is positive. On the current trajectory that requires OCF to clear roughly $90M+ at the current SBC run-rate — plausible on the guided revenue path if gross margin holds ~85% and opex discipline arrives, but it is not in any filed period yet. The FY2026 10-K (expected February 2027) is the refresh trigger; an H2 2026 OCF surge would show in the Q3/Q4 10-Qs first.
- Quality note for the file: an unlevered balance sheet (~$282.6M of reported liquidity, no debt), ~84–85% non-GAAP gross margins, and two scarce assets (the only sustained-release glaucoma implant; the only approved epithelium-on cross-linking therapy) are genuine franchise evidence. At $166.96 and a $9,548,442,400 market cap the price already pays for the inflection completing — there is no margin of safety measurable in OE terms, which is what this screen measures.
