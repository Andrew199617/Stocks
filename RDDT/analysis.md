# RDDT — Reddit, Inc.
Sweep run, 2026-10-13 (engine v5 re-run; supersedes the 2026-10-12 v4 pass — same conclusion, price moved $155.66 → $159.93). In scope: market cap ~$32.3B < $40B. Price $159.93 (Nasdaq quote API).

## What it is
The community platform: 100,000+ interest-based communities (subreddits) with volunteer moderation and user-generated content, 130.3M daily active uniques and 514.6M weekly active uniques in Q2 2026. Revenue is ~95% advertising ($762M of $805M in Q2 2026, +64% YoY); the rest is "other" — data licensing of its human-conversation corpus to AI labs (Google deal signed 2024 at a reported ~$60M/yr; OpenAI at a reported similar scale) plus premium. Asset-light: 91.3% gross margin in Q2 2026, capex ~0.2% of revenue. Public only since the March 2024 IPO ($34), so EDGAR history is 4 fiscal years (2022–2025) and OE-positive in exactly one of them — the entire analysis turns on whether the FY2025/TTM inflection is the new base. It is: Q1 2026 revenue +69% YoY, Q2 +61% (the 8th straight quarter above 60%), net income $253M in Q2 alone, TTM operating cash flow over $1B. Q3 2026 guided to $860–870M revenue (~+47–49% YoY) and $385–395M adjusted EBITDA.

## MOAT — score 6/10 (moderate-strong, not wide)
FOR — (1) A genuinely unique corpus: two decades of authentic, organized human conversation across 100k+ self-moderated communities, now scarce enough that AI labs pay to license it; users append "reddit" to Google searches by name — demand pull few platforms have. (2) Community network effects with free labor: subreddits, norms, and volunteer moderators are expensive to replicate and the archive compounds. (3) Emerging pricing power: global ARPU +36% YoY in Q2 2026 (U.S. +51%) — monetization compounding far faster than users (DAUq +18%). (4) Structural economics: ~91% gross margin, near-zero capex, $1M+ revenue per employee. (5) International runway: machine translation into 30+ languages; intl revenue +84% YoY in Q2.
AGAINST — (1) **Distribution is rented, not owned:** 77.7M of 130.3M DAUq are logged-out, much arriving via Google search; Google AI Overviews increasingly answer without a click, U.S. logged-in DAUq growth is nearly flat, the stock fell ~21% after the Q2 2026 beat on exactly this concern, and Reddit is reportedly weighing blocking Google's crawler as the 2024 licensing deal comes up for renewal — its biggest traffic source and a licensing customer are the same counterparty. (2) No lock-in: users multi-home across TikTok/YouTube/Discord/X; switching costs for readers are zero. (3) Ad business subscale vs Meta/Google; intl ARPU $2.26 vs U.S. $11.85 — the convergence is a hope, not a print. (4) Data-licensing revenue concentrated in a few AI counterparties with uncertain renewals. (5) SBC load: $338M TTM, ~33% of OCF. (6) Dependence on volunteer moderators (the 2023 API protests showed the community can revolt).
CAP = clamp(2 + 1.3 × 6) = **9.8 years**: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over the CAP, 15-year projection.

## FLAGS — every fragility flag in the final dcf_numbers.json resolved
- **STALE_FY** (FY2025 ended 9 months ago): resolved by construction — the base is the `ttm.py` TTM override quoted above, computed in code from 10-Q YTD OCF/capex/SBC, not estimated.
- **DILUTION** (share count +52.3%/yr): an IPO artifact, not a run rate. The engine's window spans the pre-IPO weighted count (57–59M) → IPO/preferred conversion (145.5M in 2024; ~73M shares from preferred conversion plus 18.6M newly issued) → 202.1M (2025) → 202.3M (H1 2026 YTD, **+0.8% YoY** on the weighted diluted count). The engine's −5pp share-count adjustment is therefore too harsh against current reality, but it is kept (conservative direction, method-consistent) and noted here as a headwind the forward numbers don't deserve. The real continuing cost is SBC (33% of TTM OCF), deducted in full; the $1B buyback ($235M executed in Q2 2026) offsets issuance if sustained.
- **BASIS_BREAK_SUSPECT** (>60% revenue jump): no divestiture, big M&A, or restatement — acquisition cash in FY2024 was $17.1M. The jumps are organic post-IPO acceleration (FY2024 +61.7%, FY2025 +69.4%) from the rebuilt ad platform, machine translation, and search-referral growth. No basis break to straddle; the real caveat is that all CAGRs are measured over the acceleration window itself, which is why the capped 35% start (not the 48.9% blend) is used.
- **NI_OCF_DIVERGENCE** (2024): net income −$484.3M vs OCF +$222.1M, a $706M gap vs D&A of only $15.6M. The one-off is the IPO: $801.6M of SBC (dominated by the $534.7M double-trigger RSU recognition at IPO effectiveness) is non-cash, so it crushes net income while OCF adds it back. OCF is real cash; the divergence is why FY2024 conservative OE (−$586M, SBC charged as a real cost at the spike level) is a trough artifact, not a run rate. SBC has since normalized to ~12% of revenue.
- **WIDE_SCENARIO_SPREAD** (super-bull IV 4.6× super-bear): real. A company 30 months public with one profitable fiscal year has a genuinely wide outcome range. Confidence is capped at medium because of it.
- Substantive, though the mechanical flag did not fire: **BASE_DIVERGENCE** — latest FY $1.69 vs 3-yr median −$2.24 vs TTM $3.36 (the flag's test needs a positive median, so it surfaced as the preliminary Tier F instead). The median is the distorted one — it averages two pre-profit years and the $802M IPO-SBC year. Rejected; TTM used. This is the single biggest judgment in the run and it is documented, not silent.

## My thoughts / overlay
- The business is better than the stock at this price. Eight straight quarters of 60%+ revenue growth, EBITDA margins heading to ~45%, SBC leverage improving every quarter, $2.8B net cash — and the DCF still says C, because 47.5× TTM owner's earnings pre-pays the base case and most of the bull case. Even the super-bull scenario alone returns only 18.9% from $159.93: at this price no defensible weighting reaches Tier A, and only a bull-weighted one reaches B.
- **Method range (both ends are engine runs actually executed, not vibes):** 3-yr median base → **F** (no positive normalized OE); FY2025-only base ($1.687/share) with identical scenarios → weighted IV@15% $61.67, weighted IRR 8.4%, **Tier D**; TTM base (final) → **Tier C, 12.7%**. Every defensible method lands below the 15% hurdle — the disagreement is only about how far below. The TTM base is the filing-derived, current one, so the verdict sits at C, the optimistic end of the defensible range. If you believe the −5pp dilution penalty and the 35% cap are both too harsh (both arguable, see FLAGS), the number moves toward B, not A.
- What would change the answer: a Google-referral shock is the bear trigger (watch logged-out traffic and U.S. DAUq); licensing renewals (Google/OpenAI) repricing materially up is the bull swing item; SBC % of revenue is the cleanest quality gauge — if it re-accelerates, the OE story breaks.
- Margin-of-safety math: the stock clears Andrew's 15% hurdle at the weighted IV of **~$123**; with a real margin of safety against the bear case (bear IV $83), a buyer wants **~$100 or below**. At $159.93 you are paid for the bull case (bull IV $156) and insured against nothing.
