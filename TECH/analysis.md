## Moat: 7/10 — strong, but no longer compounding like one
Justification: R&D Systems is the reference standard for cytokines/growth factors — catalog products are cited by number in the scientific literature, and a failed assay costs a lab weeks, so reproducibility trust beats small price differences; that shows up as 65–68% gross margins held every year through the 2023–26 tools downturn. Instrument placements (ProteinSimple, COMET) pull proprietary consumables, and GMP-grade reagents are qualified into cell-therapy manufacturing processes where switching means re-validation. Merck KGaA paying $73 cash / ~$11.3B EV for the whole franchise is a market test that a sophisticated strategic buyer sees the same durability.
- FOR: Reference-standard citation lock-in for R&D Systems proteins/antibodies — displacement would take a competitor decades of parallel citation history; gross margin 68.0% / 68.4% / 67.7% / 66.4% / 64.8% / 65.8% (FY2021–26, fundamentals.json) — pricing power held through a downcycle; instrument + proprietary consumable pull-through (ProteinSimple, Lunaphore COMET, RNAscope as the spatial sensitivity standard); regulated GMP reagent positions embedded in customers' validated manufacturing workflows; net-cash balance sheet.
- AGAINST: Subscale against Thermo Fisher and Danaher, who can bundle across the whole lab wallet; revenue essentially flat for three years (FY2023 $1,136,702,000 → FY2026 $1,215,039,000) — the moat defends share and margin, it is not currently producing growth; ROIC (roic_proxy, fundamentals.json) down from 14.5% (FY2022) and 13.1% (FY2021) to 11.8% / 8.4% / 3.6% / 9.3% (FY2023–26) — the FY2025 print is depressed by the non-cash held-for-sale impairment and litigation/arbitration charges that hit operating income directly, but FY2024 and FY2026 also sit below the earlier level; Diagnostics and Spatial Biology is the weaker, lower-margin segment and shrank −2.9% in FY2026; demand rides the biotech funding cycle (the 2023–24 funding winter is what flattened revenue); balance sheet carries a large acquired goodwill/intangible base from the roll-up years.
- ROIC note (required for a 7): measured ROIC has NOT stayed well above the cost of capital through the recent cycle — it slid from the low-to-mid teens to high single digits. The 7 rests on the written reasons the recent series understates the franchise: FY2025's 3.6% is a one-off year (impairment + arbitration/litigation in operating income), the denominator carries the full acquired-intangible/goodwill stack from acquisitions whose consumable pull-through is still ramping, gross margins never broke, and a strategic acquirer just validated the franchise at $73 cash. It is capped at 7, not 8, because the ROIC downtrend and the three flat revenue years are real erosion signals, not noise.
- A 7 gives CAP = 11.1 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 11.1 years, 15-year projection.

## Flags — every fragility flag resolved
- **NI_OCF_DIVERGENCE** (net income vs OCF differ by >40% in 2024, 2025): resolved — structural plus one-offs, and it does not touch the base. Bio-Techne's OCF structurally exceeds net income because OCF adds back D&A ($111,711,000 in FY2024; $109,903,000 in FY2025, including heavy acquired-intangible amortization) and SBC ($38,042,000 / $40,833,000) while capex runs far smaller ($62,877,000 / $31,006,000). FY2025 widened the gap (NI $73,400,000 vs OCF $287,556,000) with non-cash / one-off charges that hit net income but not operating cash: an impairment of assets held for sale of $80,503,000 for the full year (the Exosome Diagnostics divestiture; $84.2M was the Q4 charge as reported at the time), certain litigation charges of $41,827,000 (arbitration-related, per the FY2025 release reconciliation), and restructuring of $28,231,000 — cutting FY2025 GAAP operating income to $102,255,000 (8.4% margin) against an adjusted operating margin of 31.6%. FY2026 confirms the one-off read: with the impairment gone, net income recovered to $181,862,000 and operating income to $251,890,000 while OCF was essentially flat at $292,073,000. The conservative OE used throughout is OCF-based and subtracts SBC and capex in full, so the divergence inflates nothing.
- **LOW_OE_YIELD** (base OE yield 1.86%): real and decisive. At $72.57 the buyer gets $1.35/share of owner's earnings — the price capitalizes the franchise's strategic/scarcity value (and, concretely, a signed cash takeover), not its current cash generation. The standalone tier therefore rests almost entirely on growth and duration, which is exactly why it is sized to the conservative end and why confidence is not high.
- **WIDE_SCENARIO_SPREAD** (super-bull IV 5.17x super-bear IV): real, but here it cuts against the stock, not for it — even the super-bull IV@15% of $27.37 sits 62% below the price. The spread reflects the low base yield (small base, long duration), not disagreement about the business. Confidence capped at medium.

## My thoughts / overlay
- The $73.00 Merck KGaA cash deal is the security now. If it closes, holders receive $73.00 — a +0.6% spread over $72.57, an arbitrage return whose annualized value depends entirely on closing timing and completion risk, neither of which this DCF measures. The company has stopped holding earnings calls in light of the transaction.
- What this DCF actually measures is the break value: if the deal fails, the standalone weighted IV@15% is $13.50 and even the super-bull path only reaches $27.37. That is the honest downside map — a break would not be a 10–20% correction, it would be a repricing toward a low-teens handle unless another bidder appeared. Merck is paying roughly 5.4x the standalone weighted IV@15% because it is buying strategic position, synergies and a scarce reference-standard franchise, not a 15%-hurdle cash stream.
- Standalone quality is genuinely good — net cash, ~66% gross margins, OE positive 5/5 years, dividend paid — but quality is not the question at this price; the OE yield is 1.86% after three flat revenue years and a ROIC slide. As a fundamentals investment TECH stopped clearing any hurdle long before $72.57; it cleared 15% only under $13.50.
- Method range: narrow for once. Latest-FY base ($1.41) instead of the median ($1.35), or the preliminary moat-5 run, move the weighted IV@15% only within roughly $13–14 and the weighted IRR within ~4.9–5.0% — every defensible variant is a deep Tier D. The tier does not rest on an override; there are no overrides in this run.
