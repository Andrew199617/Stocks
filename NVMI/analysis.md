## What it is
Israeli semiconductor metrology and process-control equipment maker (Rehovot; Nasdaq + TASE, ordinary shares). Dimensional, materials and chemical metrology for advanced logic, memory and advanced packaging — the measurement layer fabs need as Gate-All-Around (GAA) transistors and advanced packaging raise metrology intensity per wafer. FY2025 revenue $880,577,000 (statements.json): Product $705,598,000 (80.1% of total) / Service $174,979,000 (19.9%) (segments.json). One operating segment. Latest quarter (Q2 2026, 6-K earnings release, search-cited): record revenue $254,958,000, +16% YoY and +8% sequentially; GAAP net income $75,010,000; GAAP diluted EPS $2.20; gross margin 57% GAAP. Management guided Q3 2026 revenue to $277M–$287M (search-cited) and authorized a $200M share repurchase program on September 2, 2026 (search-cited).

## OVERRIDE — base (share-scale correction) and ads_ratio
- **The defect (worker-caught; the eighth instance of the SHARE_BASIS_SPEC_DRAFT.md family):** Nova presents weighted-average share counts **in thousands**. From FY2021 the EDGAR diluted-share facts are thousands-scaled (29,816 for FY2021; 32,800 for FY2025) while FY2016–FY2020 are in units (27,503,497–28,949,739) — the break is visible directly in financials_history.csv. The engine read the thousands figures as units, so the preliminary run printed base OE/share of $5,842.20, a market cap of $10,535,360, a fake **Tier S** and an IRR pinned at the 200% solver cap (IRR_CAPPED flag) with weighted IV@15 of $143,079.80 as printed. None of that was economics.
- **Cross-checks that the scale is exactly 1,000x:** (1) FY2025 diluted EPS as filed is $7.96 (fundamentals.json / statements.json) on net income of $259,223,000 — that reconciles only at ~32.8 million diluted shares, not 32,800. (2) fundamentals.json reports shares outstanding in units: 31,780,111 at FY2025 (20-F filed 2026-02-17), vs the engine's dei figure of 29,278,401 at FY2024 — the same ~30M order. (3) The company's own releases label the counts "(in thousands)": Q2 2026 diluted shares used in EPS 34,450 (in thousands); Q2 2025 32,053 (in thousands) (search-cited releases).
- **The decision (bounded authority, step 8(c)):** judgment.json carries `base_override = {value: 5.842195121951219, kind: "estimate", source: "This run's dcf_numbers.json base_oe_normalized_per_share ($5,842.195121951219) divided by 1,000 …", reason: "…"}` — built exclusively from this run's computed output. All three median years (FY2023–25: 32,089 / 32,138 / 32,800) carry the same thousands scale and every OE leg is filed in dollars, so the division corrects the median exactly. A `shares_override` cannot fix this: overrides fill only absent periods and never replace filed values (the NTNX/TEM precedent). The final engine run pins the base via `--base-oe-ps 5.842195121951219`, and make_score.py verified the pin matches the declared base_override. Both the base_override and the ads_ratio are recorded in score.json's registry trail (_system/overrides_registry.json).
- **Measured impact:** base OE/share $5,842.20 as printed → $5.84 corrected; the verdict moves from the fake Tier S at the 200% IRR cap (preliminary run) to **Tier D, weighted IRR 7.86%**. The engine's market_cap_est ($10.5M in key_numbers.md) remains the corrupted artifact — scope is unaffected (real cap ~$10.2B, above) and no other engine output uses it.
- **ads_ratio (step 8(d)):** judgment.json carries `ads_ratio = {ratio: 1.0, …}` sourced to the FY2025 20-F (filed 2026-02-17), Item 9.A/9.C: Nova's ordinary shares themselves trade on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under NVMI and on the TASE. There is no ADS program; the traded price is per ordinary share, so no scaling applies (the ESLT/ALC ratio-1.0 precedent).

## Moat: 6/10 — moderate-strong, not wide
Justification: tool-of-record metrology positions at all four GAA players, #2 share (~25%) in film/CD metrology with ~400bp of 2025 share gains, gross margins of 55.5–57.6% across FY2021–25 and roic_proxy of 18.1–33.8% over FY2020–25 — returns above the cost of capital through the FY2023 down year as well as the upcycle — capped at 6 because ROIC has drifted from the high-20s to ~20%, Nova is a niche player against KLA/Applied Materials scale, and demand is geared to a handful of customers' capex cycles.
- FOR: Tool-of-record qualification stickiness — partner for all four GAA players; WMC platform selected as tool of record by a leading global foundry for advanced-packaging flows (Q2 2026 reporting, search-cited); qualified metrology tools sit inside fab process flows and are costly to displace. Returns through a cycle: roic_proxy (fundamentals.json) 27.6% FY2021, 27.9% FY2022, 18.1% FY2023 (the down year), 20.7% FY2024, 19.6% FY2025. Pricing power: gross margin 55.5–57.6% every year FY2021–25 (fundamentals.json), 57% GAAP in Q2 2026. Share gains: #2 in film and critical-dimension metrology at ~25% share, +~400bp in 2025 (search-cited). Diversified dimensional/materials/chemical portfolio plus the services layer (19.9% of FY2025 revenue, segments.json).
- AGAINST: Niche scale vs KLA (process-control leader) and Applied Materials/ASML R&D budgets. Semi capex cyclicality — revenue fell in FY2023 and corrected OE/share swung $2.74 → $6.00 → $5.84 across FY2023–25. Customer concentration in a few foundry/logic/memory majors. ROIC drifting down (27.6–27.9% → 18.1–20.7%) as equity compounds and acquisitions add goodwill/intangibles (goodwill $48,317,000 → $90,807,000 in FY2025 on the Sentronics acquisition, statements.json; acquisitions $56,355,000). - A 6 gives CAP = 9.8 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 9.8 years, 15-year projection.
