## What it is
Swiss premium performance sportswear company (Zurich): CloudTec running footwear as the core, expanding into apparel, tennis, training and — via new athlete signings — football and golf. Sells through wholesale (54.3% of Q2 2026 sales, per the Q2 report summary) and a fast-growing direct-to-consumer channel (45.7% of Q2 2026 sales). Reports in CHF under IFRS, files Form 20-F, and its Class A ordinary shares are listed directly on NYSE as ONON (see ADS flag below).

## Moat: 5/10 — moderate / narrow
Justification: a genuine premium brand with proprietary CloudTec/LightSpray innovation and proven pricing power (Q2 2026 gross margin 65.4%, 2026 guidance raised to at least 65%) and a fast-growing higher-margin DTC channel (45.7% of Q2 sales), at only ~2-3% global running share so the runway is long — but footwear has no network effects or switching costs, brand heat must be re-earned each season, and Nike (regaining running share under a new CEO), Adidas and Hoka are larger, better-funded and now competing directly in On's premium lane, as the promotional Americas wholesale market in 2026 showed.
- FOR: Premium brand with proven pricing power: Q2 2026 gross margin 65.4% and 2026 guidance raised to at least 65%, absorbed US tariffs at full price; Proprietary product technology: CloudTec cushioning, LightSpray and SURREAL foam franchises that competitors cannot copy directly; DTC channel compounding fastest (Q2 +26% reported, 45.7% of sales) at structurally higher margins, plus a growing owned-store network; Small share (~2-3% of global running) against a huge premium TAM, with APAC momentum (Q2 APAC-led growth, >20% of sales) and apparel scaling (+47.7% in Q2); Affluent customer base that has held up better than mass-market consumers in a polarized economy.
- AGAINST: No network effects and near-zero switching costs: runners switch shoe brands freely and brand heat is fashion-exposed; Incumbents fighting back: Nike regained running share in North America/Western Europe in FY2026, Hoka gains above $120 price points; both outspend On by multiples; Wholesale still 54.3% of sales and decelerated sharply in Q2 2026 as On cut shipments to protect full-price positioning — growth is partly channel-managed; Guided deceleration is real: FY2026 constant-currency growth trimmed to low-20s from at least 23%, vs +48.6% reported revenue growth in FY2025; Single-brand, footwear-concentrated model now spending into unproven categories (football/golf via expensive athlete signings); CHF reporting adds FX noise to USD results.
- A 5 gives CAP = 8.5 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 8.5 years, 15-year projection.
