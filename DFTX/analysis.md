## What it is
Pre-revenue, clinical-stage psychedelic psychiatry company (New York HQ, Nasdaq), renamed from Mind Medicine (MindMed) Inc. in January 2026. The entire value story is one asset: **DT120 ODT**, a pharmaceutical-grade orally disintegrating tablet formulation of lysergide (LSD) tartrate (Zydis fast-dissolve), dosed once under supervision (~6 hours to discharge readiness) as a serotonin-2A agonist for brain-health disorders. It has now produced **three positive Phase 3 readouts** (search-cited): Emerge in major depressive disorder (MDD) in June 2026 (8.1-point placebo-adjusted MADRS improvement at Week 6, 7.3 points at Week 12, no serious adverse events or suicidality signal), Voyage in generalized anxiety disorder (GAD) in August 2026, and Panorama in GAD in September 2026 (HAM-A −9.8 on 100 µg vs −4.7 on placebo at Week 12). DT120 holds FDA Breakthrough Therapy designation in both GAD and MDD. Next steps per the company: a pre-NDA meeting with FDA in late 2026 and an NDA filing targeted for H1 2027; a second MDD trial (Ascend) is enrolling, a PTSD trial (Haven) is planned for 2027, and DT402 (an MDMA R-enantiomer) is only in Phase 2a for autism spectrum disorder. There has never been any product revenue: revenue is $0 / not reported in every fiscal year in the EDGAR history. The April 2026 White House executive order directing agencies to accelerate psychedelic treatments is a genuine policy tailwind, and big-pharma M&A in the space (Lilly/AtaiBeckley, AbbVie/Gilgamesh, Otsuka/Transcend) validates the category — and arms the competition.

## Moat: 4/10 — weak-to-moderate, and entirely contingent on approval
Justification: Weak-to-moderate and entirely contingent on approval: the most advanced LSD program in existence (three positive Phase 3 readouts across GAD and MDD, Breakthrough Therapy designation in both, pre-NDA meeting scheduled) plus ODT formulation/dosing IP, regulatory exclusivity if approved, supervised-delivery know-how, and a ~$1.1B cash pile that funds the path to an FDA decision — but the active molecule is lysergide, a long-known off-patent Schedule I compound, so the moat covers a formulation and a protocol, not the molecule; big-pharma-backed rivals (COMPASS, Lilly/AtaiBeckley, AbbVie/Gilgamesh) can route around it, no pricing power or payer coverage is demonstrated, functional unblinding is a live criticism, DEA/state rescheduling sits between approval and revenue, and the pipeline behind DT120 is a single Phase 2a asset.
- FOR: Execution/data lead in psychedelic psychiatry — three positive Phase 3 trials across GAD and MDD, Breakthrough Therapy designation in both
- FOR: Formulation and dosing IP around the ODT lysergide product (Zydis fast-dissolve) plus regulatory exclusivity if approved
- FOR: Delivery know-how: supervised single-dose model with ~6-hour discharge aimed at the existing interventional-psychiatry site base
- FOR: Balance-sheet staying power — ~$1.1B cash and investments at Q2 2026 funds operations into 2030, so the lead does not die for lack of cash before the FDA decides
- AGAINST: Active molecule is lysergide — a long-known, off-patent Schedule I compound; the moat covers formulation and protocol, not the molecule
- AGAINST: Big-pharma-backed rivals can route around it with differentiated compounds (COMPASS psilocybin, Lilly/AtaiBeckley, AbbVie/Gilgamesh)
- AGAINST: Zero pricing power demonstrated — no approved product, no payer contracts; reimbursement for a supervised multi-hour session is unproven
- AGAINST: Functional unblinding is a live scientific criticism of every psychedelic trial, pressable by payers and FDA reviewers
- AGAINST: Single-asset concentration — DT402 is only Phase 2a; if DT120 stumbles at FDA there is no second leg
- A 4 gives CAP = clamp(2 + 1.3 × 4) = **7.2 years** — moot for the DCF (no positive OE to fade), but it records the protected window even in an approval world.

## My thoughts / overlay
- This is the screen working as intended: DFTX is not an owner's-earnings investment at any price — with negative OE in every period, there is **no price at which the OE DCF clears a 15% hurdle**, and no margin-of-safety price to quote. Anyone buying at $34.56 is buying the probability-weighted pipeline: FDA acceptance/approval of an H1-2027 NDA, DEA rescheduling, payer coverage for supervised dosing, and uptake against cheap generic SSRIs/SNRIs, Spravato's installed base, and incoming Lilly/AbbVie-backed rivals.
- Credit where due vs the closest comp in this sweep (CMPS, also Tier F): Definium has the broader late-stage evidence (three Phase 3 wins across two indications vs COMPASS's TRD-only program), a shorter supervised session, and a much larger cash pile (~$1.1B reported vs CMPS's smaller balance). None of that changes the OE arithmetic today — it changes the quality of the option, not the tier.
- Momentum cuts both ways: the stock has roughly tripled in 2026 on the Phase 3 readouts (search-cited), so the approval option is far from unpriced; the June 2026 raise at $34.00 is roughly where the stock still trades ($34.56).
- What would change the tier: approval plus 2–3 years of post-launch filings showing revenue covering the ~$196M/yr and rising operating burn. Until the first positive-OE year exists, DFTX stays F in this sweep regardless of trial headlines.
- Method range: unusually, there is none — latest-FY, 3-yr-median, and filing-derived TTM bases all give Tier F. The spread in this stock's value lives entirely outside the OE method (approval probability × peak sales), which is the finding.
- The one judgment call in this run is the capex override: a $0 the filings' own cash-flow statements prove, with zero measured impact on the base. It exists so the v5 gate's paperwork matches the filings' presentation, not to move the verdict — the verdict is F with or without it on any zero-fill math. It is flagged out-of-band mechanically (no filed value exists to band against) and carries an explicit ack_reason; expect it to be reviewed as an unblocks-tier override, which is appropriate.
