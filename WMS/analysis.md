## What it is
North America's leading water-management products maker: corrugated HDPE pipe and stormwater systems, Infiltrator septic/leachfield chambers, plus the acquired Orenco (FY2025) and NDS (closed February 2026) product lines, reorganized after NDS into Stormwater and Wastewater segments. Sales split across non-residential, residential, infrastructure and agriculture construction end markets. Price $127.78 (nasdaq_quote_api); market cap $10.016B — in scope (under $40B).

## Moat: 6/10 — moderate-strong
Justification: North America's leading stormwater manager with scale in corrugated HDPE pipe, a dense local plant/distribution network that confers a freight-cost advantage on bulky pipe, Infiltrator's dominant plastic leachfield-chamber position, spec/code approvals and one of the largest plastic-recycling operations in North America (resin-cost integration) — against a near-commodity core product, resin/freight cost squeezes and construction cyclicality.
- FOR: #1 North American stormwater position and the broadest water-management product line (pipe, chambers, Infiltrator, NDS, Orenco); 60+ local plants — freight is the binding cost on bulky, low value-density pipe, so local density is a structural cost advantage smaller rivals cannot match; spec-in/code-approval position with engineers and distributors defaulting to ADS systems; recycled-resin vertical integration.
- AGAINST: JM Eagle, Lane Enterprises and regional makers plus concrete/steel pipe substitution contest every bid — the product itself is close to commodity; resin and transportation inflation compresses margin before pricing catches up; demand is construction-cyclical with no recurring-revenue lock-in; growth is increasingly bought (NDS, Orenco), adding goodwill, integration risk and debt.
- Evidence (fundamentals.json): ROIC proxy 17.6% (FY2021), 16.3% (FY2022), 29.3% (FY2023), 28.8% (FY2024), 21.5% (FY2025) — well above the cost of capital through a full resin cycle — easing to 14.5% in FY2026, when the ~$991M NDS deal closed one month before year-end and inflated invested capital ahead of its earnings. Gross margin: 34.8% → 28.9% (FY2022 resin squeeze) → 36.4% / 39.9% / 37.7% / 38.3% (FY2023–FY2026), 38.6% TTM — pricing power is real, but it arrives with a lag, which is why this is 6 and not higher.
- A 6 gives CAP = 9.8 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 9.8 years, 15-year projection.

## My thoughts / overlay
