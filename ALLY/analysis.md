## What it is
Financial-services holding company (bank holding / financial holding company) built from the former GMAC: the nation's largest all-digital bank (Ally Bank, $184.6B of assets within the group's $196.0B at December 31, 2025 per the 10-K) and an industry-leading automotive financing and insurance business, plus a corporate-finance arm lending to equity sponsors and middle-market companies. Q2 2026 (company release): adjusted EPS $1.21 (+22% YoY), adjusted total net revenue $2,276M (+10% YoY), core ROTCE 11.8% (vs 11.0% a year earlier), NIM excluding core OID 3.63% (+18bp YoY), consumer auto originations $13.3B (+21% YoY) on 4.6M applications, retail auto net charge-offs 1.57% (−18bp YoY, −40bp QoQ), Corporate Finance portfolio $13.7B (+25% YoY, reported 32% ROE in the quarter), 3.6M deposit customers (+7% YoY). GAAP common equity per share $44.38 and adjusted tangible book value per share $42.12 at Q2 2026 — the $37.68 price sits below both. Q3 2026 results are scheduled for October 20, 2026. Strategic scope has shrunk: Ally Lending sold March 1, 2024, Ally Credit Card sold April 1, 2025, consumer mortgage originations ceased in Q2 2025.

## Moat: 4/10 — weak-to-moderate; niche leadership that has not earned its capital
Justification: the nation's largest all-digital bank and a leading auto lender — against a commodity lending product with no structural pricing power, full consumer-credit and rate-cycle exposure, and a return history that proves the niche leadership is not translating into franchise economics. Fundamentals.json carries no roic_proxy or gross margin for a bank presentation (both null), so the return evidence is ROE/ROTCE: GAAP ROE (fundamentals.json, on total equity) fell to 7.0% in FY2023, 4.8% in FY2024 and 5.5% in FY2025 — below the cost of equity three straight years, after 13.3% in FY2022 and 17.9% in FY2021 — and core ROTCE of 11.8% in Q2 2026 is a recovery print at a benign point in the credit cycle (retail auto net charge-offs 1.57%), not a demonstrated through-cycle level. Goodwill impairments in all three median years ($149M / $118M / $305M) on the exited card and lending adjacencies are a capital-allocation charge against the moat, not a footnote.

Confidence is low, the base was replaced with the sector's honest proxy, and the tier is soft.

- **WIDE_SCENARIO_SPREAD** (6.01x): real — a consumer lender's outcome range across a credit cycle IS this wide (super-bear IV@15% $11.11 vs super-bull $66.75). Confidence is low (also mandated by the sector flag), and the verdict is sized to the conservative end (TIER_BAND).
- **HIGH_NET_DEBT** (13.77x normalized OE): an artifact of applying an industrial leverage lens to a bank, in both directions at once. The engine's net debt is $11,735,000,000 of borrowings net of cash; it excludes the $151,649M deposit base that actually funds the bank and the $133,964M net loan book that backs the funding. The leverage that matters is regulatory capital and credit quality: deposits of $151,649M against total assets of $196,002M at FY2025, an allowance for loan losses of $3,490M, and retail auto net charge-offs of 1.57% and falling at Q2 2026. The bear scenarios carry the credit stress this flag asks for; the ratio itself is not a solvency signal for a deposit-funded lender.
- **TIER_BAND** (bear tier D at 8.47% vs bull tier B at 16.84%): accepted — the verdict spans tiers because the credit cycle does. Any position sizing belongs at the bear end.

Anyone quoting one number for ALLY without its method is hiding the ball; both honest pairings land in Tier C (11.61% to 13.54%), and the verdict sits inside that range, not at its top.
- The bank cross-check the model cannot do agrees with C: at $37.68 the market pays 0.89x adjusted tangible book ($42.12) for a franchise printing 11.8% core ROTCE and rising, with a $1.20/share dividend (fundamentals.json dps_ttm $1.20). That prices in roughly cost-of-equity returns with modest re-rating — an expected return in the low teens, not the high teens and not single digits.
- What would change the verdict: ROTCE stalling at ~12% (the bull case is then the ceiling and even C is generous), a consumer credit turn (the super-bear is a lived FY2024 experience, not a tail), or the price falling to the weighted IV@15% of $32.94, at which point the weighted case itself clears 15%.
- Q3 2026 results land October 20, 2026 — two days after this run. The print (NIM path, charge-offs, ROTCE) is the next evidence on which scenario family is right; nothing in this score anticipates it.
