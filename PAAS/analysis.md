## What it is
One of the world's leading silver producers, headquartered in Vancouver: operating silver and gold mines in Canada, Mexico, Peru, Bolivia, Argentina, Chile and Brazil, plus the Escobal mine in Guatemala, suspended and not operating. In September 2025 it closed the acquisition of MAG Silver, adding a 44% joint venture interest in the Juanicipio silver mine in Zacatecas, Mexico (operated by Fresnillo plc), for US$500 million in cash and approximately 60.2 million Pan American shares. That followed the transformative Yamana Gold transaction that closed in 2023. The NYSE listing is the common share itself, directly listed (see ADS_RATIO flag below) — the same shares trade on the TSX under PAAS.

## Moat: 4/10 — weak-to-moderate, a price-taker with scale
Justification: one of the world's largest silver producers with a diversified mine portfolio, a 44% stake in the high-grade, low-cost Juanicipio mine, a large reserve base and a net-cash balance sheet — but no pricing power over silver or gold, a depleting asset base, and operating control of its best asset sitting with Fresnillo.
- FOR: scale — one of the world's leading silver producers, operating mines across Canada, Mexico, Peru, Bolivia, Argentina, Chile and Brazil; 44% of Juanicipio (via MAG Silver, closed Sept 2025): large-scale, high-grade, low-cost silver mine adding near-term cash flow and reserves; industry-leading silver reserve base, hard to replicate given permitting scarcity for new primary silver mines; net-cash balance sheet (net debt -$501M, -1.19x normalized OE) funds capex, dividends and buybacks without forced dilution; diversification across many mines and two metals cushions single-mine outages.
- AGAINST: pure commodity price-taker — earnings are set by silver/gold prices it does not control (Q2 2026 realized silver price $70.97/oz vs $32.91/oz a year earlier: the price, not the company, did the work); depleting asset base — reserves are consumed by production and must be continually replaced via M&A or exploration success; jurisdiction risk (Mexico, Peru, Bolivia, Argentina; Escobal in Guatemala suspended since 2017); growth has been bought, not built — Yamana (2023) and MAG Silver (2025) were paid for largely in shares, diluted share count CAGR +21.9%/yr in the engine blend; Juanicipio is operated by Fresnillo plc, not by Pan American; cyclical, volatile OE — OE/share spans negative to $2.67 over the last 5 fiscal years.
- A 4 gives CAP = 7.2 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 7.2 years, 15-year projection.
