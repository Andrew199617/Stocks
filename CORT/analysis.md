## What it is
Commercial-stage cortisol-modulation biotech. For over a decade a one-product company: Korlym (mifepristone) and its authorized generic for Cushing's syndrome / hypercortisolism. That changed on March 25, 2026, when the FDA approved its second product, Lifyorli (relacorilant + nab-paclitaxel) for platinum-resistant ovarian cancer — $47.6M of revenue in its first quarter (Q2 2026, via search of the Q2 2026 release). Q2 2026 total revenue was $256.1M, up 32% YoY ($208.6M Korlym/authorized generic, $47.6M Lifyorli), net income $43.0M, and management raised 2026 revenue guidance to $1.1–$1.2B (vs $761.4M in FY2025). The same molecule, relacorilant, has a separate Cushing's application that drew a Complete Response Letter in December 2025, was resubmitted June 17, 2026, and carries a PDUFA date of December 17, 2026.

## Moat: 4/10 — weak-moderate, concentrated
Justification: specialty cortisol-modulation franchise with ~98% gross margins and ROIC proxy of 22–38% in 2020–2024, but the whole business rests on one molecule family, the Korlym patent defense against Teva failed at the Federal Circuit, an authorized generic launched in June 2024 is already cutting price, and the Cushing's relacorilant application is a binary December 17, 2026 PDUFA after a CRL.
- FOR: Korlym specialty franchise in Cushing's/hypercortisolism — record prescriptions, prescribers and active patients, with CATALYST/MOMENTUM screening expanding the diagnosed pool; ~98% gross margin every year 2020–2025 and ROIC proxy 22–38% through 2024 (fundamentals.json: 23.1% 2020, 37.6% 2021, 22.6% 2022, 24.6% 2023, 21.7% 2024) — proven pricing power in a rare-disease niche; a second marketed product from the same platform (Lifyorli, approved March 2026, $47.6M in its first quarter, >1,300 patients started); debt-free balance sheet ($544.6M cash and investments at June 30, 2026) self-funding the pipeline; deep pipeline (oncology expansions, miricorilant in MASH, dazicorilant in ALS).
- AGAINST: single-molecule-family concentration — Korlym, its authorized generic and Lifyorli all rest on cortisol modulation / relacorilant, one scientific and legal thesis; generic threat is realized, not theoretical — the Federal Circuit ruled against Corcept vs Teva and the June 2024 authorized generic already cut average price (Q1 2025 volume +23.5% was offset by a 13.3% price decline, via search); regulatory binary — December 2025 CRL for relacorilant in Cushing's, resubmission PDUFA December 17, 2026, plus a securities class action over the CRL disclosures; SBC consuming the franchise (59.5% of FY2025 OCF, $96.1M of $109.7M TTM OCF); launch-spend dependence — FY2025 operating margin collapsed to 5.9% from 20.3% and the ROIC proxy to 6.7% (fundamentals.json) as SG&A surged.
- On the rubric, a 7+ would need ROIC sustained well above the cost of capital through a cycle *and* durability; the 2020–2024 ROIC history qualifies, but the durability leg fails — one molecule family, a lost patent appeal, and a pending binary — so 4, not higher. A 4 gives CAP = 7.2 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 7.2 years, 15-year projection.

## My thoughts / overlay
- **Method range:** preliminary default run (moat 5) gave weighted IRR 3.76% and IV@15% $9.60 — Tier D. This final run (moat 4) gives 3.66% / $9.16 — Tier D. On the TTM base ($0.09/share) the numbers would be an order of magnitude worse; on the best single year (FY2024, $1.19/share) they would be better and still nowhere near the price. The honest headline is **D on every defensible base** — unusually, the base-choice debate that dominates most of these runs does not matter here.
- What the price requires: $110.16 is 159x the normalized OE/share and ~17x guided 2026 revenue at the midpoint, for a company whose TTM owner's earnings are $10,815,000 total. The market is paying for the Lifyorli launch trajectory (management projects >$1B of eventual U.S. revenue in platinum-resistant ovarian cancer alone, via search), a relacorilant Cushing's approval on December 17, 2026, and a $3B–$5B Cushing's franchise by 2030 (management projection, via search) — all as if the OE conversion and the SBC load resolve themselves. They may; none of it is in the filed owner's earnings yet.
- The genuine bull facts are not in dispute: revenue guided up 44–58% in 2026, Q2 net income $43.0M with opex flat QoQ, $544.6M of cash and investments, no debt, ~98% gross margins, and a pipeline with multiple 2026–27 readouts (BELLA and MONARCH by end of 2026, via search). The dispute is only price versus *current* owner's earnings, and on that measure the gap is not close.
- Watch items that would change this run: the December 17, 2026 PDUFA (binary, and the securities class action hangs on the CRL disclosures); Teva generic timing after the Federal Circuit loss; whether SBC/OCF (59.5% in FY2025, 88% TTM) bends down as revenue scales; and whether Q2's opex discipline (flat QoQ) holds through the Lifyorli launch year.
