Engine 5.4.0 run, 2026-10-17. All figures below are copied from dcf_numbers.json, financials_history.csv, or fundamentals.json — none are recomputed.

## What it is
Branded home-improvement and building products: Plumbing Products (Delta, Peerless, Brizo, BrassCraft faucets/fittings, Hansgrohe, Watkins Wellness / Hot Spring spas) and Decorative Architectural Products (Behr and Kilz paints sold exclusively through Home Depot in North America, Liberty Hardware — being integrated into Delta Faucet from 2026). Demand is mostly repair-and-remodel of an aging US housing stock, so it is cyclical but not new-construction-dependent. Market cap $15.303B at $72.87 (nasdaq_quote_api) — in scope under $40B.

## MOAT: 7/10 — strong, not wide
Justification: leading branded positions with pro-channel preference and proven pricing power, on ROIC that stayed far above the cost of capital through a downturn; capped by shelf-level substitutability, Home Depot concentration, and cyclicality the brands could not offset.
- Evidence (fundamentals.json, annual): **ROIC proxy** (after-tax operating income over equity + debt − cash) was 0.5265 (2021), 0.4434 (2022), 0.4777 (2023), 0.5042 (2024), 0.4461 (2025) — 44-53% every year, including the 2022-25 revenue decline. Caveat in words: the denominator is small because buybacks have driven book equity negative, so the level is flattered by the capital structure; the *persistence through a cycle* is the real signal. **Gross margin:** 34.19% (2021), 31.26% (2022), 35.60% (2023), 36.17% (2024), 35.43% (2025) — held and recovered while revenue fell, i.e. pricing offset volume.
- FOR: ROIC proxy 0.44-0.53 every year 2021-25, well above cost of capital through a downturn; Behr paint's exclusive Home Depot distribution since 1978 and Delta/Hansgrohe pro-channel preference; proven pricing power — gross margin held 31.3-36.2% and expanded in Q2 2026 while volumes fell; #1/#2 branded positions (Delta, Behr, Hansgrohe, Hot Spring) in repair-and-remodel categories driven by an aging housing stock.
- AGAINST: no real switching costs — DIY consumers can switch paint/faucet brands at the shelf (Sherwin-Williams, Moen, Kohler, private label); customer concentration — Behr sells exclusively through Home Depot in North America; housing and R&R cyclicality — revenue fell from $8,680,000,000 (2022) to $7,562,000,000 (2025) despite the brands; tariff and commodity exposure on imported plumbing components and paint inputs.
- A 7 gives CAP = 11.1 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 11.1 years, 15-year projection.

## My thoughts / overlay
- Quality is not the debate: this is a high-ROIC, pricing-power franchise that held gross margin and ~$0.8-1.1B of annual owner's earnings through a three-year revenue decline, pays a dividend (DPS TTM $1.25, fundamentals.json) and buys back ~3-4% of the count a year. The debate is price: at $72.87 the market is paying for the R&R recovery before it has shown up in revenue — the weighted IV@15% is $34.91 and the super-bull case ($64.01) still does not reach the price.
- Method range: on the preliminary default run (moat 5, blend growth −0.68% with default offsets) the weighted IRR was 7.75% and IV@15% $30.80 — also Tier D. The final run's friendlier growth recalibration and moat 7 move the answer to 8.60% / $34.91, still solidly D.
