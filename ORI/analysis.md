## What it is
Chicago-based insurance holding company founded in 1923, operating two engines: **Specialty Insurance** (commercial auto, workers' compensation, general liability, property, financial indemnity, surety, home/auto warranty) and **Title Insurance** (one of the largest U.S. title underwriters), plus a small Corporate & Other segment (the RFIG run-off sat there until its sale on May 31, 2024). FY2025 (company results summary): net premiums and fees earned of $8.1B, up 10.1% — Specialty $5.2B, up 10.9%, underwriting income $352.6M at a 93.2% combined ratio; Title $2.9B, up 9.1%, underwriting income $69.9M at a 97.6% combined ratio — consolidated revenue $9,136,300,000 (financials_history.csv), consolidated combined ratio 94.7%, net income $935,400,000, book value per share $24.21, up 22.0% inclusive of dividends declared. Latest quarter, Q2 2026 (earnings call / 10-Q coverage): net income jumped 58% to $324.4M, but nearly all of the increase was investment gains (+$172.4M, including $134.2M unrealized on equities); net operating income **fell** 11% to $186.0M, $0.76 per diluted share vs $0.83, at a 95.3% consolidated combined ratio (Specialty 95.5% with a $40M reserve strengthening; Title 95.1% with pretax operating income more than doubling to $56M). Book value per share $25.33 at Q2 2026, up 7.2% year-to-date including dividends.
