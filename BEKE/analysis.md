## What it is
China's leading integrated online and offline platform for housing transactions and services ("Beike"), built around the Lianjia brokerage brand and the ACN agent-cooperation network that lets competing brokerages share listings and split commissions. Segments: existing-home transactions, new-home transactions, home renovation and furnishing, and home rental services (Carefree Rent). Q2 2026 (company release via GlobeNewswire): GTV RMB933.8B (+6.3% YoY), net revenues RMB24.5B (−5.7% YoY), net income RMB2,624M (+100.8% YoY), 60,274 stores and 540,634 agents. Q1 2026 was much weaker: revenues −19.0% YoY, new-home GTV −37.2% YoY, profit up only through cost cuts. The profit story in 2026 is margin/cost restructuring, not demand recovery — China's housing market remains in a multi-year downturn.

## Moat: 6/10 — moderate, real network effects inside a brutal macro
Justification: Beike/Lianjia is China's dominant integrated online-offline housing platform: the ACN agent-cooperation network, the largest verified listing database, the Lianjia brand and ~60k-store / ~540k-agent scale (Q2 2026 release) create real network effects and data advantages rivals cannot quickly replicate. But the moat is narrow-to-moderate, not wide: demand is hostage to China's housing cycle and policy, agents and consumers can multi-home, take rates face regulatory and developer pressure, and the newer renovation/rental lines carry structurally lower margins — earnings power has swung from strong profits to a negative-OE year within the last 3 fiscal years.
- FOR: ACN agent-cooperation network — shared listings/commission splits across ~60k stores and ~540k agents (Q2 2026), scale rivals cannot quickly replicate; largest verified housing listing database + Lianjia brand trust in a fraud-prone resale market; data/AI tooling and transaction infrastructure (VR tours, escrow, closing services) raise agent productivity and platform stickiness; dominant share of existing-home transactions in tier-1/2 cities — existing-home GTV +8.0% YoY in Q2 2026 even in a weak market.
- AGAINST: earnings hostage to China's housing cycle and housing policy — FY2025 operating cash flow went negative; agents and consumers can multi-home, and competing platforms plus developer direct-sales channels pressure take rates; new-home segment tied to distressed developer volumes (Q1 2026 new-home GTV −37.2% YoY); growth lines (renovation, rental) are lower-margin and working-capital heavier than the core brokerage platform; China/VIE/ADS regulatory overhang and Class B weighted-voting governance concentration.
- A 6 gives CAP = 9.8 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 9.8 years, 15-year projection.

## Flags — every fragility flag resolved
- **STALE_FY** (FY ended 9.5 months ago): ttm.py was run and **failed** — "ttm.py: cannot compute TTM for BEKE (CIK 1809587): ocf: no annual (FY) fact; sbc: no annual (FY) fact; shares_diluted: no annual (FY) fact; capex: no capex facts in companyfacts under any candidate tag and no capex_override in judgment.json — refusing to zero-fill". The engine's annual history reads BEKE's filed USD-translation facts that ttm.py's leg-matching does not pick up, so no TTM pin exists. Per spec the engine's normalized median base ($0.66/ADS) is used instead. The 2026 interim prints were still checked qualitatively: Q1 2026 operating cash outflow of RMB1.5B was attributed by the CFO (Q1 earnings call) to timing of accrued employee-compensation payments, and Q2 2026 operating cash flow was reported at RMB6.61B with net income +100.8% YoY — consistent with a volatile-but-not-broken cash engine, and with keeping the median base rather than the negative FY2025 year.
- **BASE_DIVERGENCE** (latest FY OE/share −$0.36 vs 3-yr median $0.66): the **latest FY is the distorted one** — FY2025 net income was positive ($428,132,000) while OCF collapsed to −$53,794,000 on working-capital absorption (ΔNWC +$932,992,000 in financials_history.csv) in a weak housing year. The median is retained as the base; the divergence is cyclicality + WC timing, not a restatement.
- **OE_VOLATILE** (OE/share spans >3x over 5 years): real — this is a housing-cycle business (conservative OE: FY2023 $995,522,000 → FY2025 −$413,099,000). Handled by the normalized median base plus bear-skewed probabilities (45% on bear-or-worse).
- **NI_OCF_DIVERGENCE** (2023, 2024, 2025): identified per year from financials_history.csv. In 2023–2024 OCF ran far **above** net income (2023: OCF $1,571,522,000 vs NI $828,636,000; 2024: $1,294,255,000 vs $556,891,000) — the gap is the non-cash SBC addback (SBC $452,901,000 in 2023, $373,471,000 in 2024, expensed in NI but added back in OCF) plus working-capital release (ΔNWC −$1,074,477,000 in 2023). In 2025 the sign flipped: ΔNWC +$932,992,000 absorbed cash and drove OCF negative despite positive NI. No single one-off tax/impairment item is named in the computed outputs; the divergence is SBC addbacks + WC swings in both directions. The OCF-based conservative OE used throughout already nets SBC out, so the base is not contaminated by the addback side.
- **FX_CONVERTED**: BEKE reports in CNY; the engine converted CNY facts to USD at period-end ECB reference rates (frankfurter.app) — rates recorded in dcf_numbers.json (2024-12-31: 0.137; 2025-12-31: 0.14284 USD per CNY). ## Thoughts / overlay
- **Method range:** there is no defensible input range spanning the hurdle here. On the normalized median base, every scenario — including super bull at 1.25x OE and +18.55% growth start — returns less than 15% at $17.83. Using the latest-FY base instead would be worse (it is negative; no DCF at all). The bull case for BEKE at this price is a balance-sheet/multiple story (net cash, buybacks, cash, cash equivalents, restricted cash and short-term investments of RMB53.9 billion per the Q1 2026 release — a cited search-result figure, not recomputed) and a housing-recovery option, not an owner's-earnings story.
- **Hurdle-clearing price:** the weighted IV@15% is **$6.39/ADS** — the price at which the weighted path clears 15% before any margin of safety. With a margin of safety for China/VIE and cycle risk, an entry would need to be below that.
- What would change the verdict: two consecutive years of positive conservative OE at or above the FY2023–24 level (proving FY2025 was the trough, not the new run-rate), which would lift both the median base and the OE yield at any given price.
