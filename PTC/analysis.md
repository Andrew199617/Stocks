# PTC — PTC Inc.
v5 run, 2026-10-15. In scope (market cap $23.310B at $193.00, nasdaq_quote_api). All figures below are copied from key_numbers.md, dcf_numbers.json, ttm.py output, or financials_history.csv — none are recomputed. Deal and guidance facts are from the cited search results of 2026-10-15.

## What it is
Industrial product-lifecycle software: Creo and Onshape (CAD), Windchill and Arena (PLM), Codebeamer (ALM), ServiceMax and Servigistics (service lifecycle). Sold to large discrete manufacturers (aerospace, automotive, medical devices, industrial equipment). 95% of FY2025 revenue was recurring (company reporting via search results). Fiscal year ends September 30.

**Posture-changing event:** On 2026-10-05 Schneider Electric and PTC announced a definitive agreement for Schneider to acquire PTC for **$205.00/share in cash** ($22.6B equity value, $23.7B enterprise value, a 42.3% premium to the prior close), expected to close in Q3 2027 subject to PTC shareholder approval and regulatory clearances, with a $700M termination fee in specified circumstances (PTC announcement and Dow Jones Newswires reporting via search results). The stock jumped from a $144.03 pre-deal close into the $190s. **The current $193.00 price is therefore a merger-arbitrage price sitting below a signed cash offer — not a standalone multiple.** The DCF below is still run on the standard method; its verdict and the deal overlay are kept separate, and the tier is not overridden.

## Moat: 7/10 — strong, not wide
Justification: Windchill PLM is the system of record for product data at large discrete manufacturers and Creo/Onshape sit inside daily engineering workflows, so ripping PTC out means migrating decades of product history — real switching costs, 95% recurring revenue (FY2025), and Gartner Leader positioning in PLM. Capped at 7 because Siemens (NX/Teamcenter) and Dassault Systèmes (CATIA/ENOVIA) are equally entrenched at many of the same accounts, the SaaS/cloud transition (Windchill+, Onshape) gives customers a natural re-evaluation point, and end-demand is industrial capex, which is cyclical.
- FOR: Windchill PLM is the system of record for product data — migration means moving decades of engineering history; Creo and Onshape embedded in daily engineering design workflows; 95% recurring revenue in FY2025, ARR ex-divestitures $2.448B at Q3 FY26; Gartner Magic Quadrant Leader for PLM (Windchill), highest on Ability to Execute (2026); breadth across CAD, PLM, ALM (Codebeamer) and SLM (ServiceMax) supports cross-sell / digital-thread lock-in; asset-light — capex is de minimis vs OCF, so owner's earnings convert almost fully to cash.
- AGAINST: Siemens (NX/Teamcenter) and Dassault Systèmes (CATIA/ENOVIA) are equally entrenched rivals at the same accounts; SaaS/cloud migration (Windchill+, Onshape) is a natural re-evaluation point where share can be lost; end demand tied to discrete-manufacturing capex cycles — cyclical, not secular-only; ARR growth has decelerated to ~9% constant-currency — pricing/expansion power is real but not accelerating; SBC is a heavy, persistent cost ($240.7M TTM), roughly a quarter of TTM operating cash flow; AI-driven design tools from larger platforms could compress the CAD/PLM value pool over the CAP.
- A 7 gives CAP = 11.1 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 11.1 years, 15-year projection.

## My thoughts / overlay
- **The takeover dominates the standalone math, and it is deliberately NOT in the tier.** At $193.00 the market is pricing the $205.00 signed cash offer (expected close Q3 2027, shareholder and regulatory approvals outstanding) at a mid-single-digit gross discount — a merger-arb spread for ~a year of deal risk, not a bet on PTC's standalone cash flows. If the deal closes, the DCF is irrelevant; if it breaks, the pre-deal price was $144.03 and the standalone IV@15% here is $70.46 — the downside reference points are both far below today's price. That asymmetry is the real position-sizing fact.
- Quality is not the debate: near-zero capex, 95% recurring revenue, ARR compounding at ~9% ex-divestitures, and heavy buybacks (about $1.625B of repurchases expected in FY2026 per company guidance reported in search results, with diluted shares guided toward ~116M from 121M in FY2025). The historical share-count CAGR of +0.71%/yr that penalized the engine's growth blend by −0.71pp has already reversed — TTM diluted shares are 117,844,000 vs 120,777,000 in FY2025 — so forward per-share OE gets a buyback tailwind the blend does not credit; the bull cases are where that shows up, and even they do not clear 15% at $193.00.
- The honest headline is the method range, and here it is narrow: defensible runs (median base with engine growth; TTM base with guided growth) both land at Tier D, weighted IRR ~7.1–7.5%. Unlike an inflection name, there is no plausible input set on current prints that gets standalone PTC to a 15% IRR at this price — the price embeds the deal, and pre-deal it embedded a premium software multiple.
- Margin of safety: standalone, the stock clears a 15% hurdle only below the weighted IV@15% of **$70.46** — and a buyer demanding a margin of safety against the bear cases would need to be lower still (bear IV@15% $51.33). Nobody is being paid to own the standalone business at $193.00; they are being paid ~the deal spread to underwrite closing risk.
