## What it is
Global generics and legacy-brands pharmaceutical company formed in November 2020 from Mylan + Pfizer's Upjohn division: 1,400+ approved molecules sold in 165+ countries, including off-patent brands (Lipitor, Viagra, Celebrex). Since 2022 it has been shrinking to a simpler base — biosimilars contributed to Biocon Biologics (2022), then OTC, women's healthcare and Indian API businesses divested through 2024–25 — and in February 2026 announced a further restructuring (~10% workforce reduction, expected $700–850M of charges, ~$650M of annual savings targeted by end-2028, per the FY2025 10-K and company reporting).

- Latest quarter (Q2 2026, reported Aug 6, 2026, per company results coverage): revenue $3.76B, +4.9% YoY; adjusted EPS $0.69; adjusted EBITDA $1.19B; GAAP net loss $118.8M, mainly a $177.8M non-cash write-down tied to the planned sale of Tyrvaya rights. Management raised FY2026 guidance midpoints: revenue $14.75B, adjusted EPS $2.52, adjusted EBITDA $4.4B, operating cash flow ~$2.0B.
- Capital return: a repurchase program of 103,547,908 shares for ~$1.15B was completed around the Q2 report and the $0.12 quarterly dividend was affirmed ($0.48/yr policy).
- Supply overhangs: Nashik, India remediation/disruption is expected to reduce H2 2026 revenue by $100–150M; the Indore, India facility remains under an FDA warning letter and import alert (11 products barred from the U.S.) dating from December 2024.

## OVERRIDE — shares (filing kind, bounded authority)
The preliminary engine run exited 4 with `DATA_GAP:shares` — no full-year diluted share count for FY2024 or FY2025 exists in EDGAR companyfacts under any candidate tag. The reason is mechanical: in both loss years diluted shares equal basic shares (share awards are anti-dilutive), and those loss-year counts are not carried in the aggregated facts. The counts are printed in the filing itself, verified this run directly in the FY2025 10-K document:

- **Value: 1,170,700,000** — diluted weighted-average shares, FY2025.
- **Source:** Form 10-K for fiscal year ended December 31, 2025 (filed 2026-02-26), Consolidated Statements of Operations and EPS note: diluted weighted-average shares 1,170.7M for FY2025, 1,193.3M for FY2024, 1,206.9M for FY2023 (in millions).
- **Band check:** in band — last filed value 1,206,900,000 (override_checks in dcf_numbers.json: out_of_band false), so no ack_reason is required.
- **Mechanical effects, stated plainly:** the engine applies this one value to both missing periods (periods_filled 2024-12-31 and 2025-12-31 in dcf_numbers.json). FY2025 is exact. FY2024 OE/share is computed on 1,170.7M instead of its own filed 1,193.3M, which overstates that one year — and FY2024 is the median year, so the normalized base is flattered by roughly that same ~2% (direction stated, not recomputed). This is disclosed rather than adjusted; the TTM-base pass in the method range below brackets it from the conservative side.
- **Measured impact (key_numbers.md, on the final configuration and the median base actually used):** base OE/share $2.03 (no override) → $1.56 (with override), −22.79%; weighted IRR 11.47% → 10.18% (−1.29pp). The no-override side is the zero-fill basis, which silently used only pre-gap years — the override's real effect is to put FY2024/FY2025 back into the base at their filed counts. Unblocks tier: YES — without it there is no tier at all.

## Moat: 4/10 — weak; scale without pricing power
Justification: global scale and distribution are real, but the products are commodities — off-patent brands and generics with structural price erosion, powerful buyers, a live manufacturing-quality liability, and 7.1× net debt/OE.
- FOR: Global scale and distribution — 1,400+ approved molecules in 165+ countries (Mylan generics manufacturing + Upjohn commercial footprint); low-cost global manufacturing footprint, the cost position a generics producer needs to survive price erosion; legacy brand recognition (Lipitor, Viagra, Celebrex) inside a base business with positive conservative OE in 5 of the last 5 years; specialty pipeline optionality — five positive Phase 3 readouts in 2025 (ophthalmology phentolamine programs, selatogrel, fast-acting meloxicam) plus the Feb 2026 restructuring targeting ~$650M of annual savings by end-2028.
- AGAINST: Commodity products, no pricing power — generics and off-patent brands face structural price erosion (revenue 3-yr CAGR −4.22%, latest FY YoY −3.01%, engine); the channel holds the power — wholesalers and PBMs squeeze generic prices and Viatris must launch new products just to hold revenue; manufacturing quality overhang — FDA warning letter/import alert at Indore (11 products barred from the U.S.) and the Nashik disruption ($100–150M H2 2026 revenue hit); leverage — net debt $13,088.1M = 7.1× normalized OE on a shrinking OE base; a shrinking, churned base — the 2022–25 divestitures cut revenue, and the Feb 2026 program cuts another ~10% of the workforce at an expected $700–850M cost.
- A 4 gives CAP = 7.2 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 7.2 years, 15-year projection.
