## What it is

On 2026-09-24 Akamai signed a $11.6B, seven-year dedicated AI-cloud capacity commitment with Anthropic (Reuters), expandable by up to ~$9B, with an Anthropic warrant for up to ~7.7M shares (~5%) at $111.33; the company put total capex for the initial commitment at ~$5.5B. Total large multiyear cloud contracts signed in 2026: $14.4B, requiring 95–105 MW. Management guided on the Q2 call to revenue growth accelerating from single digits in 2026 to the low teens in 2027; FY2026 revenue guidance $4,445–4,530M.

## Moat: 5/10 — moderate/narrow
Justification: the world's most distributed edge network and a $2.4B/yr security franchise growing 10%, but measured returns are converging to the cost of capital — ROIC proxy (fundamentals.json) fell 15.6% → 17.9% → 14.3% → 13.0% → 10.5% → 10.5% across FY20–25, and gross margin eroded six straight years, 64.6% → 63.3% → 61.7% → 60.4% → 59.4% → 58.9%, as delivery commoditizes and lower-margin compute mix rises. A moat above 6 would need ROIC sustained well above the cost of capital; the measured history says the opposite trend.
- FOR: most distributed edge network in the industry (4,100+ PoPs, ISP-embedded) — expensive and slow to replicate, best-in-class for large live-event delivery; security franchise at scale ($604M in Q2 2026, +10% YoY, led by Guardicore segmentation and API security); deeply embedded enterprise base with high retention, delivery cash flows funding the pivot; contracted AI-cloud backlog ($14.4B of multiyear CIS commitments signed in 2026, incl. Anthropic $11.6B/7yr) — multi-year revenue visibility peers lack.
- AGAINST: delivery (36% of Q2 revenue) declining −6% YoY against Cloudflare and hyperscaler bundling — the legacy moat is melting; ROIC and gross margin in long decline (above); no owned demand surface and weak developer mindshare vs Cloudflare; the Anthropic pivot concentrates counterparty risk and demands ~$5.5B capex at unproven returns, and the warrant dilutes holders up to ~5%.
- A 5 gives CAP = 8.5 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 8.5 years, 15-year projection.

## My thoughts / overlay
- The honest headline is the *range across the two defensible bases*: median-of-3-FY base ($3.75/share, preliminary run, default scenarios) → weighted IRR 8.71%, IV@15% $41.66; TTM base ($2.94/share, final run, bear-tilted scenarios) → weighted IRR 6.91%, IV@15% $29.41. - What the price is paying for: the street narrative (consensus one-year target $158.87 per StockStory's 2026-10-15 note) prices the Anthropic portfolio as if the ~$22M/MW revenue at mid-60s–70s cash gross margins were already run-rate. On owner's-earnings math, the next two years move the *wrong* way first: capex roughly triples-to-quadruples from the $491M TTM level toward the ~$5.5B committed program while SBC already consumes $510M TTM. OE gets worse before the contracted revenue can make it better.
- Quality is mid, not bad: OE positive 5/5 years, security is a genuinely good business, and the backlog is real contracted demand, not pipeline. The problem is price and capital intensity, not fraud or a broken core.
- Watch items: Q3 2026 report (2026-11-05) — CIS revenue acceleration, 2026 capex print, any Anthropic ramp timing; whether buybacks survive the build (TTM buybacks $615,744,000 per fundamentals.json vs issuance $69,176,000 — still net-positive for now).
