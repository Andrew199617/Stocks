## What it is
Swiss-headquartered global eye-care pure-play (spun off from Novartis in 2019), listed directly on NYSE and SIX Swiss Exchange. Two segments: Surgical (intraocular lenses — PanOptix, Vivity, Clareon; CENTURION/UNITY phacoemulsification equipment and consumables) and Vision Care (daily-disposable contact lenses — Dailies Total1, Precision1/7; Systane/TRYPTYR ocular health). FY2025 net sales were $10.401B (financials_history.csv), up 4.94% YoY (rev_yoy_latest, dcf_numbers.json); revenue CAGR 8.77% over 5 years and 6.06% over 3 years.

## Moat: 7/10 — strong
Justification: Global eye-care pure-play leader: #1 in premium intraocular lenses and a phaco equipment installed base that pulls through consumables and creates real surgeon training/switching costs, plus recurring Vision Care revenue — all riding aging-population cataract demand. But every category is contested, contact lenses carry low patient switching costs, premium IOL uptake depends on elective spend and procedure volumes, and pipeline risk is proven (PowerVision).
- FOR: #1 premium IOL franchise (PanOptix, Vivity, Clareon) with leading presbyopia-correcting IOL share; phaco ecosystem lock-in — CENTURION/UNITY installed base pulls through consumables, surgeon training raises switching costs; recurring Vision Care revenue (daily-disposable lenses, Systane ocular health); pure-play scale in R&D and a direct global salesforce, riding aging-population cataract and myopia demand.
- AGAINST: J&J Vision, Bausch + Lomb, Zeiss and CooperVision contest every category (implantables faced competitive pressure in 2026 prints); contact lenses are contested consumer products with low patient switching costs and online/private-label pressure; premium IOL penetration depends on elective out-of-pocket spend and cataract procedure volumes, which were soft in 2026; pipeline/acquisition execution risk is real money (~$402M non-cash PowerVision impairment in Q2 2026); tariff and manufacturing-cost headwinds flagged in 2026 reporting.
- A 7 gives CAP = 11.1 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 11.1 years, 15-year projection.

## Flags — every fragility flag resolved
- **STALE_FY** (FY ended 9.4 months ago): ttm.py exits 3 for this IFRS filer (message quoted in full under Owner's earnings) — expected; the engine's median base is used per spec. Q2 2026 prints (sales +8% YoY, core EPS +10.5% YoY, guidance raised) show no deterioration that would make the FY2025-based median stale in a harmful direction.
- **OE_VOLATILE** (OE/share range over the last 5 years spans >3x): real and identified — conservative OE was $429,000,000 in FY2022 and $571,000,000 in FY2023, then stepped up to $1,442,000,000 (FY2024) and $1,557,000,000 (FY2025) as OCF rose from $1,388,000,000 to $2,077,000,000 to $2,271,000,000 while capex fell (all financials_history.csv). Whether the OCF step-up is durable margin/WC structure or partly one-off is the central uncertainty; the median base ($2.90 vs latest $3.14) is the mitigation, and the super-bear case (0.75x OE) prices a partial reversal.
- **NI_OCF_DIVERGENCE** (2024, 2025): net income $1,018,000,000 vs OCF $2,077,000,000 in FY2024 and $980,000,000 vs $2,271,000,000 in FY2025 (financials_history.csv). The gap is structural non-cash charges in reported earnings, not cash leakage: Alcon's reported FY2025 diluted EPS was $1.98 vs core diluted EPS of $3.07 (company FY2025 reporting), the difference dominated by intangible amortization and impairment-type items, plus the SBC addback ($162M FY2024, $171M FY2025, both deducted in OE anyway). Q2 2026 shows the same pattern — a ~$402M non-cash PowerVision impairment drove reported EPS to $0.00 vs core EPS of $0.84. The OCF-based conservative OE deducts both capex and SBC, so the divergence does not inflate the base.
- **ADS_RATIO_MISSING**: resolved by a declared ads_ratio of 1.0 in judgment.json under the bounded authority — Alcon is direct-listed: the Form 20-F cover registers "Ordinary Shares, nominal value CHF 0.04 per share" (symbol ALC) on the NYSE and SIX Swiss Exchange; the NYSE security is the ordinary share itself, not an ADS, so per-share figures and the traded price are on the same basis.
