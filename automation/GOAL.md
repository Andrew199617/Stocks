## Description
Hourly, one company at a time, run a thorough owner's-earnings DCF (SEC EDGAR history, two owner's-earnings definitions, bear/base/bull, implied IRR vs Andrew's 15/20/25% hurdles) and write it into ~/workspace/stocks/<TICKER>/ with analysis.md and score.json. Once a day, send a filterable HTML ranking of all completed companies sorted by implied IRR and upside at a 15% discount rate.
