# Russell 3000 owner's-earnings DCF sweep

Goal ID: goal_806e0a2de739
Goal slug: russell-3000-owner-s-earnings-dcf-sweep

## Description
One company at a time, work out its **true owner's earnings** from the filings themselves (operating cash flow minus capex minus stock-based compensation, every data gap surfaced instead of zero-filled, a TTM base when the fiscal year is stale) and run a probability-weighted five-scenario DCF on that base, with a moat score — argued on evidence such as the return-on-invested-capital history and margin stability — setting how fast growth fades. Report the implied return against Andrew's 15 / 20 / 25% hurdles, write the work into ~/workspace/stocks/<TICKER>/ (analysis.md, judgment.json, score.json and the enrichment files the pages read), keep every verdict on current filings and current prices, and publish the ranking and company pages (site + daily attachment) so the verdict and its evidence are readable on a phone. The original wording of this goal ("hourly, bear/base/bull, implied IRR vs hurdles") is preserved below in the status log; the method history lives in `_system/README.md`.
