Run a SECOND PASS on ONE company already scored by the owner's-earnings DCF sweep. Read ~/workspace/stocks/_system/SECOND_PASS.md first and follow it, plus the v4 method in ~/workspace/stocks/_system/README.md / DCF_AUDIT.md where SECOND_PASS.md defers to it. The APP folder (~/workspace/stocks/APP/) shows the pass-1 output shape.

Steps:
1. Candidates: read ~/workspace/stocks/_system/queue.json — entries with status "done", in queue order, whose folder has a score.json — plus the APP pilot (folder ~/workspace/stocks/APP/ with score.json, treat as last candidate if not in queue). Read ~/workspace/stocks/_system/second_pass_queue.json ({"entries":{}} — create it if missing). Pick the FIRST candidate whose ticker is absent from entries or marked "pending". Immediately rewrite second_pass_queue.json marking it "in_progress" with today's date — a parallel second-pass job runs offset ~8 min and this marking is the lock; re-read the file immediately before marking, and if your pick was just taken, take the next candidate. If no candidate is pending, finish silently.
2. Read the company folder fully: analysis.md, score.json, dcf_numbers.json, financials_history.csv. Identify pass 1's load-bearing assumptions: base OE choice, blended growth and scenario starts, moat, scenario probabilities, and how each fragility flag was resolved. Your job is to attack the assumptions most likely to be wrong — not to redo the pass.
3. Filing freshness + forensic battery (every run, before the research): run `python3 ~/workspace/stocks/_system/forensic_pull.py <TICKER>` and paste its full output into second_pass.md. (a) FRESHNESS: if it reports a newer 10-K/10-Q than pass 1 used (newer period than dcf_numbers.json's latest_period_end, filed after pass 1's analysis date), recompute the TTM owner's-earnings base filing-direct from the new filing using the v4 YTD math (FY − prior-year YTD + current YTD for OCF, capex, SBC, shares) — a materially different recomputed base is a MATERIAL FINDING under step 6; if the base barely moves, note the recompute and both values in second_pass.md. Note any material 8-Ks for step 4. (b) BATTERY: resolve every tripped battery flag in writing in second_pass.md — margin structure and gross-margin trend as moat evidence (≥40% gross margin is the durable-advantage rule of thumb, judged sector-relative); COGS % of revenue swings; SG&A-vs-COGS split migration (when the split moves but combined (COGS+SG&A)/revenue is flat, it is optics — trust the combined series); NI-vs-operating-income gap (careful when operating income is near zero); the prepaid battery (the deferral pattern is prepaids spiking while earnings improve and OCF weakens, reversing into expense later); discontinued ops on the books. Then the judgment checks from SECOND_PASS.md: divestiture optics with a pre/during/post comparison (operating income, SG&A, CFO, FCF, stranded costs — state plainly: real improvement or accounting optics); noncore income reconstruction (core revenue and core operating profit excluding interest, investment, financing and one-time gains — if noncore is material to the base, rebuild base OE on core numbers); capitalized-cost shifting (capex or capitalized balances growing faster than revenue while SG&A/R&D fall and earnings outrun OCF — the WorldCom pattern). A hard forensic hit (deferral pattern, cost shifting, noncore carrying the base) can be a MATERIAL FINDING on its own. Also state which of pass 1's two OE definitions (total-capex vs maintenance) is the honest one for this business.
4. Targeted research (2-4 web searches) hunting for what a standardized filing pass misses, in BOTH directions: (a) segment-level growth masked by consolidated figures — sunset/discontinued/legacy lines dragging a growing core, or a dying segment flattering the total; check latest 10-Q segment disclosures and earnings coverage for segment growth rates; (b) one-offs pass 1 normalized away that actually recur, or "one-offs" that are structural; (c) events after the last filing pass 1 used — guidance changes, earnings-call disclosures, M&A, major customer wins/losses, product sunsets; (d) SBC/dilution trajectory vs pass 1's share-count adjustment; (e) any flag pass 1 resolved on thin evidence.
5. Verdict — NO MATERIAL FINDING: write ~/workspace/stocks/<TICKER>/second_pass.md with what you checked (including the forensic battery output and filing-freshness result), the sources, and why the score stands. Do NOT touch score.json. Mark the second-pass queue entry "done_no_change" with today's date, rebuild `python3 ~/workspace/stocks/_system/build_daily_html.py`, finish silently.
6. Verdict — MATERIAL FINDING: quantify it and rerun the engine under the same v4 rules as pass 1: `python3 ~/workspace/stocks/_system/dcf_engine.py <TICKER> --moat <score> --probs <5 decimals> [--offsets <5>] [--mults <5>] [--base-oe-ps <value>] [--gterm <x>] [--cik <cik>]`, with the pass-1 sanity checks (every scenario growth start compared against actual recent prints incl. segment prints; recalibrations carry written reasons). Update score.json with the new pass-1-schema fields — including the moat_for / moat_against pillar lists (refresh them whenever the pass changes the moat picture) — PLUS a "second_pass" object: {date (today), verdict "changed", prior_tier, prior_weighted_irr, prior_iv_at_15, summary (evidence + sources in 2-4 sentences)}. Update one_liner to reflect the revision. Write second_pass.md with the full reasoning: what pass 1 missed, the evidence with sources, old vs new inputs, old vs new five-scenario table and weighted result. Append a short dated "## Second pass (YYYY-MM-DD)" addendum to analysis.md summarizing the change and pointing to second_pass.md — never rewrite pass-1 content. Mark the second-pass queue entry "done_changed", rebuild the daily HTML.
7. Reporting: silent EXCEPT when a change moves the company INTO Tier S/A (new weighted IRR ≥20%) or OUT of Tier S/A (was ≥20%, now below) — then one short line: ticker, old tier → new tier, old → new weighted IRR, price, new weighted IV@15%, one-line reason. Also report plainly if the second-pass system itself is blocked. Never invent numbers — every figure comes from dcf_numbers.json, the forensic battery output, your documented arithmetic, or a cited search result.
