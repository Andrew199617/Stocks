## What it is
A self-administered equity REIT owning 104 predominantly mixed-use / shopping-center properties in high-barrier, affluent coastal markets — Santana Row (San Jose), Assembly Row (Boston), Pike & Rose (North Bethesda) are the flagships — with retail plus residential and office components. The model is densification and redevelopment of land it already owns, plus selective acquisitions and recycling of mature assets. It is the REIT sector's Dividend King: the Q2 2026 raise to $1.16/share quarterly ($4.64 indicated annual) was the 59th consecutive annual increase.

Current trading (Q2 2026 release, via search results): Core FFO $1.88 per diluted share, +6.8% YoY (Nareit FFO $1.88, −1.6%, only because Q2 2025 carried a one-time $13.0M / $0.15-per-share new-market-tax-credit gain); full-year 2026 Nareit and Core FFO guidance raised and tightened to $7.48–$7.56/share, +6.5% Core growth at the midpoint. Record comparable leasing of 819,000 sq ft in the quarter at +15% cash rent spreads (trailing-12-month comparable rollover +17%); portfolio 96% leased, small-shop 93.9% leased / 92.3% occupied — levels not seen since 2007. Net debt/EBITDA 5.4x, fixed-charge coverage 3.9x, liquidity $1.2B (Q2 2026 reporting). GAAP EPS is noisy by design: FY2025 net income carries a $150,111,000 gain on sale of real estate and Q2 2026 EPS fell YoY on a smaller sale gain — neither touches the OCF-based base below (the gain is backed out in the cash-flow reconciliation).

## Moat: 5/10 — moderate / narrow
Justification: prime, supply-constrained coastal mixed-use centers with demonstrated pricing power — Q2 2026 comparable leases at +15% cash rent spreads, trailing-12-month rollover +17%, portfolio 96% leased, small-shop leased 93.9% (highest since 2007) — but measured returns are thin: roic_proxy in fundamentals.json runs 3.8% / 4.8% / 5.8% / 4.4% / 4.9% / 5.9% for FY2020–FY2025, well below wide-moat territory (partly a depreciated-cost REIT book effect, but the level is the level); the filer reports no gross-margin split (fundamentals.json gross margin is null), and operating margin swings 35–49% with sale gains.
- FOR: Irreplaceable high-barrier coastal locations — 104 mixed-use / shopping-center properties (Santana Row, Assembly Row, Pike & Rose) in affluent, supply-constrained markets; proven pricing power — Q2 2026 comparable leases at +15% cash rent spreads, trailing-12-month comparable rollover +17%, the highest in over 10 years; occupancy and demand depth — portfolio 96% leased, small-shop 93.9% leased / 92.3% occupied (levels not seen since 2007), record 819,000 sq ft of comparable leasing in Q2 2026; durability through cycles — 59 consecutive years of dividend increases (longest in the REIT sector), raised FY2026 Core FFO guidance to $7.48–$7.56/share (+6.5% at midpoint).
- AGAINST: Low measured returns on capital — roic_proxy only ~4–6% across FY2020–FY2025 in fundamentals.json; capital intensity — filed capital expenditures run $246.8–311.1M/yr, roughly half of operating cash flow, and development/redevelopment must be re-earned continuously; growth partly bought, not only earned — $735,274,000 of real-estate acquisitions in FY2025 funded with debt and share issuance (shares_diluted 81,313,000 in FY2023 to 86,405,000 in FY2025, financials_history.csv); leverage and rate exposure — net debt $4,856,187,000, 5.4x net debt/EBITDA in Q2 2026 reporting, plus retail-tenant and anchor-transition risk.

## Thoughts / method range
- The honest tension is the capex line. Charging all $291.3M of development, redevelopment and other capital expenditures against OE treats a growth program as upkeep; charging none of it (the preliminary zero-fill) printed a $6.6676/share base. Unlike REG (capex above D&A), FRT's filed capex runs *below* D&A every year. The one kinder series is the engine's NI-anchored maintenance OE (median $4.146/share, sized in the sensitivity run above: 7.47%, still D). Beyond it, the only larger line is the acquisition line ($735.3M in FY2025), which no worker convention charges to OE.
- Method range, all on filed figures: engine normalized median $3.1813/share (override-mechanics artifact, disclosed above) → still Tier D; true per-year median $3.65/share → Tier D; TTM pin $3.6995 → 6.94% weighted IRR, Tier D; true TTM ~$3.63/share → Tier D. **Every defensible base gives Tier D.**
- What the price is actually paying for: cap-rate value on irreplaceable coastal real estate and a 59-year dividend record, neither of which an owner's-earnings DCF at a 15% hurdle credits. That is a legitimate way to own this asset; it is not a 15% owner's-earnings return at $106.53.

## Verdict
**Tier D — sized to the conservative end.** A genuinely high-quality, moderately moated REIT (moat 5/10) whose TTM owner's earnings of $3.6995/share support a 6.94% weighted IRR at $106.53. It clears the 15% hurdle only below ~$36 (weighted IV@15% $36.40), 20% below ~$26 (IV@20% $25.53) and 25% below ~$20 (IV@25% $19.57). Confidence low (sector model misfit, wide scenario spread).
