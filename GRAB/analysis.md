## What it is
Southeast Asia's leading super-app: Deliveries (FY2025 revenue $1,800M, 53.4% of the $3,370M total), Mobility ($1,219M, 36.2%), Financial Services ($347M, 10.3% — payments, lending, digital banks GXS/GXBank and the Superbank consolidation), Other $4M (segments.json, FY2025 20-F). Listed directly on Nasdaq in Class A ordinary shares via the December 2021 Altimeter SPAC merger. Q2 2026 (6-K earnings release, August 4, 2026): revenue $997M, +22% YoY; on-demand GMV $6.5B, +21%; adjusted EBITDA $168M, +54%; record 54 million monthly transacting users; FY2026 guidance raised to revenue $4.10–4.15B and adjusted EBITDA $720–740M, plus a new $750M buyback authorization.

## Moat: 5/10 — moderate, real density, unproven cash economics
Justification: Regional density leadership and super-app cross-sell are real but unproven in cash terms: ROIC was negative for FY2021–24 and only barely positive in FY2025, users/drivers/merchants multi-home, and growth is shifting onto a credit balance sheet (lending book, Atome).
- FOR: density leader in Southeast Asia mobility + deliveries (record 54M monthly transacting users in Q2 2026, on-demand GMV +21% YoY); super-app cross-sell on one account/wallet lowers the acquisition cost of each added service; gross margin on the clean post-presentation-change years rose 36.5% (FY2023) → 42.0% (FY2024) → 43.2% (FY2025) per fundamentals.json; net-cash balance sheet (−$1,380M net debt) funds the fintech build-out without forced dilution.
- AGAINST: ROIC (fundamentals.json roic_proxy) −29.9% / −17.9% / −9.9% / −3.5% / +0.9% for FY2021–25 — never above the cost of capital in the filed history; multi-homing across GoTo, ShopeeFood/Sea and Foodpanda with near-zero switching costs, and consumer + partner incentives of ~$706M in Q2 2026 alone (Q2 2026 results coverage); growth bought into credit — the scaling lending book and the Atome acquisition ($1.49B initial consideration, September 2026) move risk from take-rate to credit losses, and loan growth absorbs operating cash flow; SBC of $241M against $79M of FY2025 OCF with the share count growing 3.3%/yr; eight-country regulatory patchwork plus ASEAN FX headwinds the company puts at 2–3% inside FY2026 guidance.
- A 5 gives CAP = 8.5 years: growth would hold 2 years, then converge linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 8.5 years — moot this run, since the negative base produces no scenario values.

## My thoughts / overlay
- This is a "great franchise candidate, no owner's earnings yet" F, not a broken-business F. The operating trajectory is genuinely strong — revenue +20.5% in FY2025, +22% in Q2 2026, adjusted EBITDA margin 16.9% of revenue and compounding for 18 straight quarters, guidance raised twice in 2026 — and the balance sheet carries net cash. What the sweep measures, though, is cash left for owners after SBC and capex, and on filed IFRS numbers that figure has been positive in exactly one of the last five years.
- The swing factor is FY2025's OCF collapse ($852M → $79M) while net income turned positive. If that is mostly loan-book growth (a chosen investment that reverses as the book seasons), forward OE inflects fast; if lending losses and incentive intensity are structural, adjusted EBITDA will keep outrunning cash OE. The Atome deal ($1.49B initial, plus a performance-priced buyout of the remaining 40% ~2 years after close) doubles down on exactly that question, and the market has de-rated the stock ~39% over the three quarters to September 2026 (price $2.94 vs the 52-week high of $6.24) while insider buying (CEO ~$29.9M of Class A shares on 2026-09-21, per insider-trade coverage) goes the other way.
- Method range: every filing-based method lands on F — latest FY −$0.06/share, 3-yr median −$0.06/share, maintenance definition −$287M total. The only route off F is a forward/adjusted construction (guided FY2026 adjusted EBITDA of $720–740M minus SBC at the FY2025 level and capex would be solidly positive), which is precisely the construction this method refuses to score. That refusal is the finding: the gap between adjusted EBITDA and owner's earnings IS the investment question on GRAB.
- What would change the tier: an FY2026 20-F (or filing-derived TTM) showing OCF recovering toward the FY2024 level while SBC keeps falling — at FY2024's OE/share (+$0.12) the DCF would still not clear 15% at $2.94, so a re-score needs both cash conversion AND another year of OE growth, not just one good print.

## Final verdict
- **Final tier: F** (tier by numbers F, no override; F sub-type sbc_exceeds_cash)
- Confidence: **Low** (high on the filed OE arithmetic — both definitions negative, median and latest bases agree; low on how fast the lending-book cash absorption reverses, which decides the next score)
- One-liner: Southeast Asia's leading super-app, moat 5/10 — Tier F on numbers: normalized owner's earnings are negative (-$0.06/share) because SBC ($241M in FY2025) exceeds what thin operating cash flow leaves after capex, so it clears no hurdle at $2.94 on filed OE; the bull case rests on adjusted-EBITDA growth (Q2 2026 +54% YoY, FY2026 guidance raised) converting into cash owner's earnings, which the filings have not yet shown.
