## What it is
Clinical-stage oncology biotech built around a single in-licensed molecule: ivonescimab, a PD-1/VEGF bispecific antibody licensed from Akeso in a deal that closed January 2023 — Summit holds development/commercialization rights for the United States, Canada, Europe and Japan; Akeso retains China and the rest of the world, and is owed up to $4.5B in milestones plus low-double-digit royalties (deal terms as announced at closing). The FDA has accepted the BLA for ivonescimab plus chemotherapy in EGFR-mutant NSCLC after prior targeted therapy, with a PDUFA goal date of November 14, 2026 (reported). Phase 3 evidence: HARMONi showed a progression-free-survival benefit (overall survival not statistically significant in the global readout), and HARMONi-6 in China was reported as the first head-to-head Phase 3 to show overall-survival superiority over an anti-PD-(L)1 combination regimen. A broad Phase 3 program (HARMONi-3, HARMONi-7, colorectal, bladder) is running. Post-period support: AstraZeneca agreed in 2026 to invest $2B through convertible preferred shares, and collaborations with Daiichi Sankyo and Pfizer expand the trial program (reported). There is no approved product and no product revenue.
