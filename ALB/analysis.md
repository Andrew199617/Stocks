## What it is
One of the world's largest lithium producers (Energy Storage: Salar de Atacama brine in Chile, Greenbushes and Wodgina hard rock in Australia via JVs, Silver Peak in Nevada) plus a leading bromine / specialty chemicals business (Specialties) and, until the Q1 2026 sale of a 51% controlling stake, the Ketjen refining-catalysts business (now equity income in Corporate). Earnings are dominated by the lithium price: management's own 2026 outlook is framed as three lithium-price scenarios — at ~$10/kg LCE, net sales $4.1–4.3B and adjusted EBITDA $0.9–1.0B; at ~$20/kg, $5.7–6.0B and $2.4–2.6B; at ~$30/kg, $7.5–7.8B and $4.2–4.4B (company 2026 outlook, via search). Q2 2026 printed the recovery: net sales $1.74B (+31.1% YoY), adjusted EBITDA $858.1M (+155%), Energy Storage realized price $19.53/kg LCE (+60.5% YoY) on volumes of 65kT LCE (+11%); management expects 2026 to land toward the high end of the $20/kg scenario, with 2026 Energy Storage volumes of 225–235kT LCE (flat to down ~4%) after the June 9 CGP3 fire at Greenbushes (restarted Aug 1), and full-year 2026 capex guided to ~$500M.

## Moat: 4/10 — weak-to-moderate: tier-1 cost assets, no pricing power
Justification: Tier-1 low-cost assets (Salar de Atacama brine, Greenbushes hard-rock JV, leading bromine position) give a real structural cost advantage, but Albemarle is a commodity price-taker with no pricing power: ROIC (fundamentals.json roic_proxy) ran 8.8% / 6.1% / 8.2% / 21.4% / 1.6% / −11.4% / −2.6% across FY2019–FY2025 — above the cost of capital only in the 2022 lithium spike — and gross margin swung from 42.0% (FY2022) to 12.3% (FY2023), 1.2% (FY2024) and 13.0% (FY2025), recovering to 23.8% on the TTM window in fundamentals.json. A cost-curve position that cannot defend returns through the cycle is a weak-to-moderate moat.
- FOR: Salar de Atacama (Chile) brine and Greenbushes (Australia, JV) hard-rock stake — among the lowest-cost, highest-quality lithium resources in the world. Leading global bromine position (Jordan / Arkansas) with steadier pricing — Specialties Q2 2026 sales +20.5% YoY, adjusted EBITDA +61.3%. Scale and technical know-how in lithium conversion; long-term contracts cover ~40% of lithium salts volume. Balance sheet repaired in the trough: capex cut from $2,154M (FY2023) to $590M (FY2025), $1.3B of debt repaid in Q1 2026, Ketjen 51% stake sold.
- AGAINST: Pure price-taker: realized lithium price swung from the 2022 spike to ~$10/kg in 2025 and back to $19.53/kg realized in Q2 2026 — earnings follow the commodity, not the franchise. Through-cycle ROIC below the cost of capital in most years (FY2023 1.6%, FY2024 −11.4%, FY2025 −2.6%); gross margin 42.0% → 1.2% peak-to-trough. Chinese / African supply overhang and lepidolite capacity cap lithium prices; new supply is the bear case's engine. Heavy capex in expansions: capex of $2,154M in FY2023 against OCF of $1,327M produced negative owner's earnings at the trough. CORFO commissions and the Chilean fiscal take rise with lithium prices, skimming the upside at Atacama.
- A 4 gives CAP = 7.2 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 7.2 years, 15-year projection.

## DCF — final run (2026-10-18)
The engine's blended growth is −4.33% (rev CAGR 5y +10.45%, 3y −11.10%, latest rev YoY −4.37%) — a trough-revenue artifact that straddles the price spike and collapse, so scenario starts were set explicitly (offsets −0.057 / −0.007 / +0.063 / +0.103 / +0.143 vs the blend) and the OE multipliers were widened to the company's own price-scenario geometry, with written reasons:
- **Multipliers are lithium-price scenarios, anchored on filed data.** Super bear 0.50× = the actual $10/kg year just filed (FY2025 OE total $652.2M vs TTM $1,304.5M). Bull 1.30× and super bull 1.60× sit inside the company's own $20→$30/kg EBITDA geometry ($2.4–2.6B → $4.2–4.4B, ~1.7×) because CORFO commissions and taxes skim the top. Bear 0.75× is a partial retracement toward ~$15/kg.
- **Growth-start sanity check (mandatory):** every start is below the latest prints — TTM revenue +18.3% YoY (fundamentals.json) and Q2 2026 revenue +31.1% YoY — deliberately: those prints are lithium-*price* recovery off a trough, not volume growth. Volumes are guided flat to down ~4% for 2026 (225–235kT LCE) with CGP3 reaching full run rate only in early 2027, so base growth of +2.0% is a volume-plus-mix assumption at a held ~$20/kg price, not a continuation of the price bounce. Price-level risk is carried in the multipliers, not in compounding growth rates.
- Probabilities 15/25/30/20/10 (defaults shifted 5pp from super bull to super bear): the TTM base already embeds a price recovery, and Chinese supply overhang makes a retracement at least as likely as a spike. Price $95.48.

## Flags — every fragility flag resolved

## My thoughts / overlay
- **Method range is the headline:** F on the filed 3-yr median base (−$7.34/share, a capex-peak/price-trough window), C on the filing-direct TTM base ($9.58/share, weighted IRR 12.69%), and the bull end of that same TTM run reaches B. Anyone quoting one tier for ALB without its base is hiding the cycle.
- The TTM base is mid-cycle, not peak: it averages two trough quarters (Q3/Q4 2025, net losses on ~$10/kg pricing) with the H1 2026 recovery, and Q2 2026 alone (OCF $710.0M in the quarter, fundamentals.json quarterly) annualizes well above it. At a sustained $20/kg the company's own scenario table implies more than the base case here — that upside is deliberately carried in the bull/super-bull weights (30% combined), not in the base.
- The mirror risk is equally filed, not hypothetical: at $10/kg Albemarle just printed FY2025 — OE/share of $5.54 even *after* slashing capex by two-thirds, and negative OE in FY2023–24 when capex was still running. The 0.50× super-bear multiplier is the company's own recent history.
- Capital allocation through the trough was good: capex $2,154M → $590M, Kemerton idled, Ketjen control sold, $1.3B of debt repaid, dividend maintained (DPS declared $1.62 in FY2025, fundamentals.json). The balance sheet no longer forces the equity story; the lithium price does.
- **Hurdle-clearing price:** the weighted IV@15% is **$77.73** (20% at $55.41, 25% at $43.04) — at $95.48 the market is already paying for a good part of the $20/kg-holds case, and a margin of safety against the bear case means the $40s.
