1. **Base OE is the TTM figure, not the 3-yr median (−$0.78, Tier F) or the
   latest FY alone ($0.04).** The median was rejected as distorted by FY23–24
   peak SBC and the post-ironSource reset.
2. **Growth starts are revenue-anchored absolute levels, not the engine blend
   (4.96%).** Base 16% sits deliberately below the Q2 +24% total print because
   Vector laps its May-2025 launch, the non-strategic tail shrinks to zero,
   and Create grows low-single digits.
3. **Segment masking was the central adjustment, not an afterthought:**
   Strategic Grow +63%, strategic revenue +38%, non-strategic −33% in Q2.
4. **SBC is a real, but falling, cost:** $342M TTM, 16.9% of TTM revenue,
   ~14% in Q2; diluted shares +4.9% YoY in Q2.
5. **NI/OCF divergence is structural** (SBC + ironSource intangible

   clean measure. Maintenance OE was rejected (convertible reclass artifact).

figures are exact: Strategic Grow $328.955M +63% YoY, Strategic Create
$157.456M +5% (+14% ex the $12M one-time item in Q2 2025), total Strategic
$486.411M +38%, Non-Strategic $60.057M −33%, total revenue $546.468M +24%;
Grow Solutions $389M +35%, Create Solutions $158M +2%.
Q3 2026 guidance in the same release: Strategic $540–550M (+44–47%),
Strategic Grow $380–385M (+68–70%), Strategic Create $159–163M (+7–10%),
Non-Strategic ~$20M (about one month of Supersonic, sold 2026-08-04;
ironSource Ads Network closed 2026-04-30), adjusted EBITDA $185–190M.
Implied Q3 total revenue is $560–570M, i.e. roughly +20% YoY on the guided
mix — between pass 1's base start (16%) and bull start (24%), and fully
consistent with its written reasoning (Vector strength offset by the
non-strategic runoff to zero and low-single-digit Create). No segment
pass 1 missed, in either direction: the dying tail and the growing core
were both already in the starts, and Create ex-one-off (+14%) was already
credited.

- **Intangible impairment:** H1 2026 impairment of $278.666M in the Q2
  release's non-GAAP reconciliation — the non-strategic/ironSource
  write-down pass 1 named. Non-cash; it widens GAAP net loss, it does not
  inflate the OCF-based owner's earnings. No OE adjustment.
- **Restructuring:** $31.359M in Q2 2026 alone, $38.262M H1 vs $31.231M in

  did not normalize it away: its OE is OCF − capex − SBC, and cash
  restructuring is already inside OCF. (It is only added back in the
  company's adjusted EBITDA, which pass 1 did not use as its base.)
  Conclusion: recurring, already charged, no change.
- No new recurring gain flattering OCF was found.
