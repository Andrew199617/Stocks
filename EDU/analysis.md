## What it is
China's largest provider of private educational services (20-F filer, fiscal year ends May 31; EDGAR SIC 8200, Services-Educational Services, per fundamentals.json): educational services and test preparation courses, overseas study consulting, educational materials and distribution, private-label products and livestreaming e-commerce (company description in its September 22, 2026 Form 20-F filing announcement, PR Newswire). Listed on NYSE as ADSs (each ADS = ten common shares) and in Hong Kong (9901), the two listings fully fungible. The post-2021 business is the rebuilt one: after the 2021 "double reduction" policy banned for-profit K-9 academic tutoring, the company pivoted to non-academic tutoring, high-school and adult programs, overseas-related services, tourism and livestreaming e-commerce.

## Moat: 5/10 — moderate/narrow, regulatory-contingent
Justification: China's largest private-education brand with a nationwide learning-center network, sustained 52–58% gross margins and ROIC that recovered to 21.7% in FY2026 (21%+ in FY2017–19 pre-crackdown) — but the moat is regulatory-contingent: the 2021 'double reduction' policy destroyed the core K-9 tutoring business overnight (FY2022 operating loss, ROIC −30.4%), switching costs for parents/students are low, teaching talent is poachable, and the VIE structure's enforceability is untested. A franchise the state can drain by decree is a narrow moat, however strong the brand.
- FOR: Largest private-education brand in China — nationwide learning-center network and the default name for test prep / overseas study consulting; sustained gross margins of 52–58% across the last decade (54.6% in FY2026) — pricing power through the cycle; ROIC recovered to 21.7% in FY2026, matching the 21%+ prints of FY2017–19 — the post-2021 pivot rebuilt a high-return model; net-cash balance sheet funds expansion and buybacks without capital markets; diversified post-2021 revenue mix — no single segment is the whole company the way K-9 academic tutoring was before 2021.
- AGAINST: Regulatory discretion is the moat's ceiling (2021 precedent, below); VIE structure legally untested; low switching costs — parents choose per course/per term, against local and online rivals including free/low-price summer courses management itself has flagged; people business — star teachers and livestream anchors can leave (East Buy's top anchor departure in 2024 is the standing example); OE conversion has lagged revenue (OE/share CAGR 2y −1.81% in key_numbers.md).
- Evidence (fundamentals.json): ROIC proxy — FY2017 21.3%, FY2018 21.7%, FY2019 21.3%, FY2020 14.9%, FY2021 2.6%, FY2022 −30.4%, FY2023 7.7%, FY2024 11.4%, FY2025 15.2%, FY2026 21.7%. Gross margin — FY2017 58.3%, FY2022 43.5% (crackdown trough), FY2024 52.5%, FY2025 55.4%, FY2026 54.6%. The ROIC recovery is real but it is a recovery to the pre-2021 level, not proof of durability through the next policy cycle — that is why the score is 5 and not 7+.
- A 5 gives CAP = 8.5 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 8.5 years, 15-year projection.
