## What it is
Danish biopharma built on the TransCon prodrug platform, now a commercial rare-endocrine company: YORVIPATH (palopegteriparatide — the only parathyroid-hormone replacement therapy for adults with hypoparathyroidism), SKYTROFA (once-weekly growth hormone), and YUVIWEL (TransCon CNP for achondroplasia, FDA-approved February 2026). Plus platform licenses (Novo Nordisk, Teijin in Japan) and the Eyconis ophthalmology spin-out. One operating segment per the 20-F.

## Moat: 5/10 — moderate/narrow, platform real, economics unproven
Justification: TransCon prodrug platform validated by three approved rare-endocrine products (YORVIPATH — the only PTH replacement therapy for hypoparathyroidism; SKYTROFA weekly growth hormone; YUVIWEL for achondroplasia, approved Feb 2026) with specialty pricing power in the numbers: gross margin 83.4% (FY2023) → 87.8% (FY2024) → 86.8% (FY2025) in fundamentals.json. But there is no demonstrated return on capital: book equity is negative (-€162.8M at FY2025), the roic_proxy series in fundamentals.json is denominator noise (+180.6% in FY2019, -55.9% in FY2018, -3.0% in FY2025 despite an operating loss), and conservative owner's earnings are negative in 0 of the last 5 years. Revenue is concentrated (YORVIPATH ~80% of Q2 2026 product revenue), weekly-GH and PTH rivals are in market or in Phase 3, and SBC (€116.2M in FY2025) exceeds FY2025 free cash flow.
- FOR: TransCon platform validated across three approved products; specialty pricing power (gross margin 83–88% across FY2023–25); rare-disease commercial infrastructure (YORVIPATH in 35+ countries, SKYTROFA >20,000 patients since launch); platform monetization beyond own sales (Novo Nordisk license, Teijin Japan royalties up to mid-20s %, Eyconis milestones/royalties).
- AGAINST: no demonstrated returns on capital (OE negative 0/5 years, negative book equity, ROIC not measurable through a cycle); single-product concentration (YORVIPATH ~80% of Q2 2026 product revenue, €252M of €315M); competition on both lead products (eneboparatide in Phase 3 for hypoparathyroidism; Sogroya and NGENLA weekly-GH rivals); SBC €116.2M in FY2025 exceeding FY2025 FCF, share count up from 56,287,060 (FY2023) to 60,607,131 (FY2025) in the EPS note; finite patent/exclusivity horizons and rare-disease pricing politics.
- A 5 gives CAP = 8.5 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 8.5 years, 15-year projection. (Moot for the score — see DCF.)

## My thoughts / overlay
- There is no method range to hide behind here: zero-fill base -$7.21/share, override base -$7.03/share, latest-year-only -$1.37/share, TTM not computable for this IFRS filer. Every defensible variant is Tier F. Anyone quoting a DCF value for ASND today is projecting the inflection, not measuring owner's earnings.
- The inflection itself is real on the top line: H1 2026 revenue €585.9M in the Q2 2026 6-K (vs €259.0M H1 2025), Q2 product revenue €315M (+105% YoY; YORVIPATH €252M), Q2 non-IFRS operating profit +€92M, and management guiding FY2026 operating cash flow above €500M. But the same 6-K's IFRS profit is flattered by items that are not owner's earnings — a €158M priority-review-voucher sale in Q2 and a €654M income-tax credit in H1 — and non-IFRS profit excludes the SBC this screen counts as a real cost. The 2028 convertible notes were converted into equity in 2026 (per Q2 reporting), which cuts the debt leg but adds shares.
- What would change the tier: a filed year in which OCF − capex − SBC is positive. On management's own OCF guide that is plausible for FY2026 on a latest-year basis; the 3-yr median needs more than one such year. The FY2026 20-F (expected Feb 2027) is the refresh trigger — if its latest-year OE prints positive, this name deserves an immediate re-score rather than waiting for the queue.
- Quality note for the file: gross margin ~87% and a first positive OCF year are genuine franchise evidence; SBC at €116–136M a year against that OCF is the whole ballgame on this screen. If SBC merely flattened while OCF follows the guide, the F flips quickly — that convexity is exactly what a $225.25 price (13.7B market cap) is already paying for, with no margin of safety measurable in OE terms.
