## What it is
US national-security technology and services contractor (Reston, VA): mission IT, DevSecOps, SIGINT, electronic warfare and space-based sensing for the Department of Defense and the intelligence community, ~90% national-security work with federal civilian exposure largely exited. FY2026 revenue $9,567,779,000 (+10.89% YoY, of which 7.2% organic per the earnings release), operating margin 9.6%, EBITDA margin 12.3%. Total backlog $32.0B with funded backlog up 29% YoY and FY2026 contract awards of $10.2B (book-to-bill 1.1x) per the earnings release. On 2026-03-09 CACI closed the all-cash $2.6B acquisition of ARKA Group (space-based EO/IR sensors, hyperspectral imaging, agentic-AI geospatial software; ~1,100 employees), debt-funded — the defining event of the fiscal year.

## OVERRIDE — base (bounded authority (c))
- **The problem (BASE_DIVERGENCE):** latest FY OE/share $31.59 vs the engine's normalized 3-yr median $18.81 — both are distorted, in opposite directions.
  - The **latest FY is flattered**: deferred income taxes added $145,387,000 to FY2026 OCF (vs −$27,060,000 in FY2025, FY2026 earnings-release cash-flow statement) as the ARKA purchase-accounting deferred tax liability built (deferred income taxes on the balance sheet rose from $142,636,000 to $376,580,000); working-capital drag eased (receivables build of $198,983,000 vs $269,215,000 in FY2025) on what the release calls efficient working capital management; and ARKA contributed only a ~4-month stub of earnings against the enlarged capital base.
  - The **median is depressed**: it is the FY2025 print, from the low-OCF-margin years (FY2024–25 OCF ran ~6.5% / ~6.3% of revenue, the low end of the decade range, while receivables built through 14–15% revenue-growth years), and it assigns zero weight to the ARKA step-up — which FY2027 guidance corroborates (free cash flow of at least $900M guided for FY2027, per the earnings release, vs the $735M the company reported for FY2026).
- **The decision:** judgment.json carries `base_override = {value: 22.4079, source: "This run's financials_history.csv / dcf_numbers.json OE-per-share prints: FY2024 $16.8228, FY2025 $18.8107, FY2026 $31.5901; mean of the three", reason: (full text in judgment.json)}` — built exclusively from figures this run's engine outputs printed, under bounded authority (c). ## Moat: 5/10 — moderate / narrow
Justification: classified incumbency, a cleared workforce and differentiated owned technology are real advantages, but through-cycle returns on capital are unremarkable and every contract is eventually recomped.
- **ROIC evidence (fundamentals.json `roic_proxy` series):** FY2017 6.6%, FY2018 8.7%, FY2019 7.7%, FY2020 9.3%, FY2021 11.5%, FY2022 8.6%, FY2023 9.4%, FY2024 10.2%, FY2025 9.4%, FY2026 7.6% — a decade spent barely above the cost of capital, with FY2026 diluted by the ARKA capital base. That record does not support a moat above 5. CACI does not file a usable gross-margin line in recent years (fundamentals.json gross margin is null from FY2020 on; cost of revenue absorbs direct labor), so the margin evidence is the operating margin trend: 8.0% (FY2022) → 8.5% → 8.5% → 8.9% → 9.6% (FY2026) — real mix improvement, from a low base.
- FOR: classified incumbency and cleared workforce — high barriers to entry on intelligence / DoD mission programs; differentiated owned technology (SIGINT, electronic warfare, space-based sensors via ARKA) vs pure labor-hour services; $32.0B total backlog (~3.3x FY2026 revenue), funded backlog +29% YoY, FY2026 book-to-bill 1.1x; mix shift to technology lifting operating margin 8.0% → 9.6% and EBITDA margin to 12.3%; ~90% national-security concentration in enduring, well-funded budget priorities.
- AGAINST: ROIC proxy only 7.6–11.5% over the last decade — no wide-moat economics; contracts recompete — incumbency is rented, not owned, and Leidos, Booz Allen, GDIT, SAIC and Peraton bid the same task orders; single-customer complex — US federal budgets, procurement delays, shutdowns and administration priorities set demand; people-based delivery limits pricing power; growth partly acquired, not organic (ARKA $2.6B cash; FY2026 revenue +10.9% total vs +7.2% organic).
- A 5 gives CAP = 8.5 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 8.5 years, 15-year projection.

## My thoughts / overlay
- The honest headline is the *range*: Tier D on the median and normalized-mean bases, Tier C on the flattered latest-FY base. No method clears the 15% hurdle at $614.31 — the market is pricing CACI as if FY2026's cash conversion and double-digit growth are the permanent run-rate, and paying for the ARKA step-up in full, in advance.
- Quality is not the debate: OE-positive 5/5 years, backlog ~3.3x revenue, funded backlog +29%, margins expanding, buybacks shrinking the count. The debate is price and leverage: an OE yield of 3.65% on the normalized base, with net debt at 9.48x that base, leaves no margin of safety — the bear IV@15% ($195.22) sits at roughly a third of the price.
- Watch items for pass 2: whether FY2027 OCF confirms the ≥$900M FCF guide without another deferred-tax tailwind; ARKA revenue/ROIC as a full year consolidates (FY2026 ROIC proxy fell to 7.6% on the enlarged capital base); pace of deleveraging vs the low-3s-by-June-2027 guide; funded-backlog conversion.
