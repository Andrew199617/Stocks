## What it is
The largest US tower REIT, and — since May 1, 2026 — a pure-play one: ~40,000 US towers leased to the big wireless carriers under long-term contracts with ~3% annual escalators (weighted-average remaining tenant-contract term ~6 years; $23.7B of contracted amounts at Dec 31, 2025). On May 1, 2026 it closed the sale of its Fiber Solutions and Small Cell businesses for ~$8.4B net proceeds, then repurchased $1.0B of shares and repaid more than $7B of debt (Q2 2026 release, via search). What remains is a slow-growth, high-payout rental business: Q2 2026 site rental revenues $967M (−4% YoY), Adjusted EBITDA $675M (−4%), AFFO $488M / $1.13 per share (+11%). FY2026 outlook (continuing operations only): site rental revenues $3,855M (−5% vs 2025), Adjusted EBITDA $2,690M (−6%), AFFO $1,975M / $4.59 per share (+4% / +5%). Dividend reset to $4.25/yr (secondary reporting of the sale announcement).

## Moat: 6/10 — moderate-strong: scarce contracted sites, concentrated tenants
Justification: Scarce permitted tower sites with real relocation switching costs, ~6-yr weighted-average contract term, $23.7B contracted amounts and ~3% escalators, plus pure-play US scale — against extreme tenant concentration (AT&T ~28% of site rental revenue; Sprint cancellations and DISH terminations still shrinking reported revenue), no pricing power beyond escalators, US carrier capex ex-growth, a ~93% AFFO dividend payout that retains nothing, and a fiber decade that ended in a ~$5B impairment.
- FOR: Scarce permitted tower sites (~40,000 US towers); carrier networks are built around specific sites — real relocation switching costs. Contract structure: ~6-yr weighted-average remaining term, $23.7B contracted amounts, ~3% fixed escalators. High incremental margins on added tenants/equipment; Q2 2026 organic billings +3.9% ex DISH/Sprint. Only large US-listed pure-play tower company after the fiber exit; investment-grade, 100% fixed-rate debt.
- AGAINST: Extreme tenant concentration — AT&T ~28% of site rental revenue; top-3 carriers dominate. Tenants can merge or fail: Sprint cancellations and DISH terminations still shrinking reported revenue (DISH default is now a >$3.5B litigated claim, recovery uncertain and possibly capped if treated as lease rejection — search reporting). No pricing power beyond contractual ~3% escalators; US carrier capex is ex-growth. Dividend consumes ~93% of guided AFFO — almost nothing retained to compound. Fiber decade ended in a ~$5B impairment and a sale — capital allocation drag on top of the tower moat.
- A 6 gives CAP = 9.8 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 9.8 years, 15-year projection.

- **NI_OCF_DIVERGENCE** (2023, 2024, 2025): found and named in the 2026-10-13 run and unchanged: (1) 2024 — the ~$5.0B non-cash goodwill impairment of the Fiber reporting unit drove GAAP net income to −$3,903M (financials_history.csv) while OCF was $2,943M; (2) 2025 — net income of $444M includes held-for-sale valuation losses on the Fiber Business against OCF of $3,057M; (3) structural REIT wedge in all three years — ~$690-790M/yr of D&A is non-cash. Direction matters: GAAP income understates cash generation here; the OCF-based conservative OE is the right series, on the continuing basis (done above).
- **SECTOR_MODEL_MISMATCH** (Real Estate): fires by rule — OE/capex DCF fits REITs poorly (AFFO is the sector's native measure). Confidence **low**, tier soft. Mitigation done, not waved: the AFFO cross-check from the 2026-10-13 run (price = 17.3x guided AFFO at that run's $79.56; dividend-yield-plus-growth ≈ 8%) independently lands on the same Tier D answer, so the mismatch is not what keeps CCI below the hurdle — the price is.
- **HIGH_NET_DEBT** (engine: 16.74x normalized OE): the engine's net debt ($27.021B, key_numbers.md) is the stale pre-sale FY2025 balance sheet. Post-sale reality documented in the 2026-10-13 run from the Q2 2026 10-Q: total debt $18,239M, cash $1,042M → net debt $17,197M = 10.7x continuing TTM OE and ~6.4x guided FY2026 Adjusted EBITDA. The >$7B repayment is real deleveraging, but that is still a levered equity: a 10% OE disappointment is a much larger equity event, and the dividend already takes ~93% of AFFO. Mitigants are genuine: 100% fixed-rate debt and ~$4.5B of revolver availability (Q2 2026 release). The bear/super-bear OE multipliers (0.90/0.75) are where this stress is priced.
- **BASIS_BREAK_SUSPECT** (in substance; the engine did not fire it because the mixed-basis OE/share series happens to look flat at ~$6.41): the central fact of the run — the Fiber/Small Cell businesses were sold May 1, 2026.
