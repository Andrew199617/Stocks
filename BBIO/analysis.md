## What it is
Commercial-stage biopharmaceutical company (Palo Alto) built on a hub-and-spoke model for genetic diseases. Its one marketed product is Attruby (acoramidis), an oral near-complete (≥90%) transthyretin stabilizer approved in Nov 2024 for transthyretin amyloid cardiomyopathy (ATTR-CM), sold ex-U.S. by partners as Beyonttra with royalties back to BridgeBio. It competes with Pfizer's Vyndaqel/Vyndamax and Alnylam's Amvuttra. Three more Phase-3-positive programs are in FDA review: BBP-418 for LGMD2I/R9 (PDUFA Nov 27, 2026), encaleret for ADH1 (PDUFA May 8, 2027), and oral infigratinib for achondroplasia (NDA submitted, U.S. launch expected mid-2027) — all per the Q2 2026 results release (SEC Ex-99.1).

## Moat: 5/10 — moderate/narrow, a real product franchise not yet proven in cash
Justification: Attruby is a differentiated, patent-protected product taking first-line share in a growing category at ~95% product gross margins, and the same development engine has produced three more Phase-3-positive assets — but the franchise is one product, the two competitors are far larger, and there is no year of positive operating income in the company's history to prove the economics survive competition.
- FOR: Near-complete (≥90%) TTR stabilization — the differentiation claim versus tafamidis — with first-line share still climbing and real-world data associating acoramidis with fewer cardiovascular events/hospitalizations than tafamidis (Q2 2026 release); product gross margin 95.3% in Q2 2026 and total gross margin 95.82% FY2025 / 98.25% FY2024 (fundamentals.json) — pricing power is real at the product level; ex-U.S. royalty stream (Beyonttra) with zero commercial cost; a repeatable rare-disease development platform — three additional NDAs in FDA review at once (BBP-418, encaleret, infigratinib).
- AGAINST: Single-product concentration — Attruby is essentially all product revenue; Pfizer (Vyndaqel/Vyndamax, the entrenched incumbent) and Alnylam (Amvuttra) are larger, better-capitalized competitors in the same category; **no ROIC evidence**: operating income has been negative every fiscal year on file (FY2025 −$523,411,000), equity is −$2,086,610,000, and the roic_proxy series in fundamentals.json prints positive values only because its equity + debt − cash denominator is distorted by the negative equity — it is an artifact, not a return on capital; heavy and growing claim stack ahead of common (convertibles, deferred royalty obligations, 7% preferred) plus 9.1%/yr share dilution.
- A 5 gives CAP = 8.5 years — moot here, since there is no positive base OE to project.

## My thoughts / overlay
- This is the rare F where the business is visibly working: Attruby is annualizing above $1B of revenue at ~95% product gross margin within two years of launch, and three more approvals could land between Nov 2026 and mid-2027. The OE-DCF still has nothing to grip — five straight years of negative owner's earnings on both definitions, a TTM base of −$2.98/share, and H1 2026 operating cash use of $268,380,000 (ttm.json current-YTD leg).
- The gap between the product economics and the shareholder economics is the story: a ~95%-gross-margin drug is being carried by a cost base sized for four simultaneous launches and a claim stack (convertibles, royalty obligations, 7% preferred) that keeps layering ahead of the common. Management's own framing (Q4 2025 call reporting) has the pipeline turning cash-generative in late 2027 — until a filed quarter shows positive OCF, that is a forecast, not a base.
- There is no hurdle-clearing price to quote: with negative owner's earnings at every base, no positive price produces a 15% OE-implied return. The 15% question only becomes askable after the company prints its first positive-OE year; until then any entry price is paying for launch execution and pipeline optionality, not cash flows.
- Sizing: the F is a statement about the screen, not a short thesis — commercial-stage biotechs mid-launch fail this screen by construction. But on Andrew's hurdles there is nothing here to underwrite today, and the next hard evidence is binary and dated: BBP-418 PDUFA Nov 27, 2026.
