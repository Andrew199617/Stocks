All figures below are copied from key_numbers.md, dcf_numbers.json, or ttm.json — none are recomputed. Filing figures quoted for the capex override and the litigation item are cited to the FY2025 10-K.

## What it is
The largest US skilled-nursing roll-up: independent operating subsidiaries run skilled nursing, senior living and rehabilitative facilities (373 facilities at FY2025 year-end, 17 states, ~37,900 skilled beds), mostly bought distressed and turned around by local leaders under a decentralized model. A captive REIT, Standard Bearer, owns the real estate (158 owned properties at year-end, 120 operated by Ensign affiliates, the rest leased to third parties under triple-net leases). Revenue is overwhelmingly Medicare/Medicaid/managed-care reimbursement — the government sets the price.

## OVERRIDE — capex (filing)
- **The gap:** the preliminary engine run exited 4 with `DATA_GAP:capex` — Ensign files no capex under any candidate tag from FY2020 onward (the last filed value is FY2019, $71,541,000), so v5 correctly emitted no tier on zero-filled capex.
- **The decision:** judgment.json carries `capex_override = {value: 193557000, kind: "filing", source: "Form 10-K for fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows, investing activities line 'Purchase of property and equipment': $(193,557) thousand for FY2025, $(158,240) thousand for FY2024 and $(106,180) thousand for FY2023 (statement in thousands)"}`. The override fills only periods with no filed value (FY2020–FY2025 in the annual history; all three TTM legs in ttm.py); filed values are never replaced.
- **Direction of the distortion:** a single value must stand in for six years, and the value chosen is the latest and largest year. Charged against FY2023 (actual filed-statement capex $106,180,000) and FY2024 ($158,240,000) it overstates those years' capex, so it understates the median base — conservative. The same $193.6M against FY2025 is the filer's own exact figure, and management budgets ~$190M of renovation capex for 2026 (FY2025 10-K MD&A), so the TTM leg is at the right level, not padded.
- **Measured impact (key_numbers.md, same final configuration with overrides toggled off):** on the TTM base actually used, base OE/share $9.28 without the override → $6.03 with it (−35.04%); weighted IRR 12.90% → 9.62% (−3.28pp). Unblocks tier: YES — without it the filing gap blocks scoring entirely. The impact is large because the no-override comparison zero-fills capex, which is exactly the fiction v5 exists to refuse.

## Moat: 6/10 — moderate; a real operating system, no pricing power
Evidence first (fundamentals.json, filing-direct): ROIC proxy 25.1% / 21.8% / 21.3% / 17.4% / 18.2% / 17.2% for FY2020–FY2025 — well above the cost of capital through a full reimbursement and labor cycle, but drifting down as the portfolio scales. Gross margin slid the other way: 23.1% (FY2021) to 20.1% (FY2025) — labor inflation is winning a point or so a year against fixed reimbursement. A 6, not a 7+: the returns say the turnaround system is real; the payor structure says Ensign can never price its way out of a cost squeeze.
- FOR: turnaround playbook replicated for 26 years — CMS data published Feb 2026 shows same-store survey results 24% better than state peers and a 19% advantage in 4–5-star buildings, most of which were 1–2-star at acquisition; ROIC 17–25% every year FY2020–FY2025; decentralized local-leader model with cluster density (373 facilities, 17 states) and shared Service Center overhead; Standard Bearer captive REIT (158 owned properties) adds cost control and a rent stream; demographic demand tailwind.
- AGAINST: no pricing power — Medicare/Medicaid set the rates; labor is the product and the cost, regulated and scarce; the June 2026 Hunterbrook ("Ensign: The Nursing Home Empire Built on Fatal Neglect", June 8, 2026) and Muddy Waters (June 11, 2026 — administrator licenses allegedly rented at an estimated ~20% of facilities) reports allege part of the margin is understaffing and data gaming — unadjudicated, but they attack the durability of the cost advantage itself, and a securities class action followed (Perusek v. The Ensign Group, C.D. Cal., lead-plaintiff deadline Dec 7, 2026); no switching costs for patients, referrers or staff; growth is substantially bought (102 operations added since 2024); repeat False Claims Act history ($48M settlement in 2013; the 2023 relator settlement below).
- A 6 gives CAP = 9.8 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 9.8 years, 15-year projection.

## Flags — every fragility flag resolved

- **STALE_FY** (latest FY ended ~10 months ago): resolved by construction — the base is the TTM figure from ttm.py through Q2 2026, legs quoted above.
- **BASE_DIVERGENCE** (latest FY OE/share $5.48 vs 3-yr median $2.66): the median is the distorted one, twice over. (1) The single-value capex override charges FY2025's $193.6M against FY2023 (actual $106.2M) and FY2024 (actual $158.2M), cutting those years' OE/share mechanically. (2) FY2024 OCF troughed at $347,186,000 despite net income of $297,973,000 — the 2023 accrual builds (below) reversed and the litigation settlement was paid in cash during 2024. A median built on each year's own filed capex would sit materially higher; rather than hand-build one, the TTM base is used, and the median is retained in the reporting as the conservative bound.
- **NI_OCF_DIVERGENCE** (2023: net income $209,399,000 vs OCF $376,666,000): the one-off is identified. FY2023 earnings carry $60.8M of expenses for litigation matters outside the ordinary course (FY2025 10-K shareholder-letter footnote) — the False Claims Act / Anti-Kickback relator case settled in December 2023 (government had declined to intervene; ~$47M settlement, no admission) — accrued but unpaid at year-end, so it depressed net income while the accrued-liability build lifted OCF; the cash went out in 2024 (case dismissed Dec 10, 2024). Add-backs of D&A $72,387,000 and SBC $30,767,000 (FY2023, 10-K cash-flow statement) and accrued-wage/self-insurance builds explain most of the rest, partly offset by a $79,818,000 receivables build. - **OE_VOLATILE** (OE/share range >3x over 5 years): real, and mostly accounting-path noise rather than business cyclicality — the 2023 accrual, the 2024 cash-payment trough, and the override's single capex value against three different actual years.
