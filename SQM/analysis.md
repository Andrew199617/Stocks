## What it is
Chilean producer of lithium (Salar de Atacama brines, plus the Covalent Lithium operation in Australia), iodine (world's largest producer), potassium nitrate / specialty plant nutrition, potassium and industrial chemicals. Listed in Santiago and on the NYSE as ADSs, each representing one Series B share. The earnings engine is the lithium price: everything else is a diversifier around it.

## Moat: 5/10 — moderate; a resource moat, not a pricing moat
Justification: the Salar de Atacama brine operation is structurally lower-cost than spodumene rivals at world-leading lithium scale, and SQM leads globally in iodine and potassium nitrate — but it is a price-taker in lithium, its core asset is state-owned and leased, and new supply it does not control caused the last collapse.
- FOR: Salar de Atacama brine resource — solar-evaporation brine extraction is structurally lower-cost than hard-rock spodumene, at world-leading lithium scale; world-leading positions beyond lithium (largest iodine producer, leader in potassium nitrate / specialty plant nutrition) that earn through the lithium cycle; long-dated resource access — Corfo lease to 2030 plus the Codelco partnership (Nova Andino Litio) extending Salar operations into 2031–2060; diversified earnings base (OE positive 3 of the last 5 filed years despite the collapse); proven through-cycle cash generation (FY2022 conservative OE $3,172,348,000).
- AGAINST: commodity price-taker — revenue swung from $10,710,578,000 (FY2022) to $4,528,761,000 (FY2024) on an unchanged asset base; the core asset is leased from the state (Corfo lease expires December 31, 2030, lease rates up to 40% of the lithium price, Codelco becomes majority productive partner for 2031–2060); supply growth from China/Africa/Argentina/Australia drove the 2023–24 collapse and can repeat it; Atacama water/community/environmental scrutiny and Chilean resource politics cap the share of upside SQM keeps; capex intensity is real and rising ($971,792,000 in FY2024 against OCF of $1,274,678,000; ~US$3 billion guided for 2026–2028, company-reported).
- A 5 gives CAP = 8.5 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 8.5 years, 15-year projection.

## My thoughts / overlay
- **Method range is the headline.** The mandated base for this run is the median of FY2022–FY2024 OE/share, which lands on the FY2024 trough ($1.06/share). The same filed history contains FY2022 conservative OE of $3,172,348,000, and the company's own post-period reporting (H1 2026 net income US$1,024.7 million in six months, 6-K) implies current earnings power far above the base. A base built from those post-FY2024 figures would produce a materially higher tier — but a base_override may only be built from this run's code outputs under the worker's bounded authority, and neither ttm.py (us-gaap only) nor the engine's history reaches past FY2024. Re-basing SQM on its FY2025 20-F / 2026 interims is therefore a main-agent call, flagged here rather than taken. On the base this run is required to use, the answer is unambiguous: even the super-bull scenario (18% start, 1.25x OE) yields only 7.20% IRR at $60.75.
- Quality is not the debate: world-scale low-cost resource, leading iodine/nitrate franchises, OE of $3,172,348,000 in a strong-price year. The debate is the state take and the cycle: Corfo lease rates up to 40% of the lithium price, a state majority partner from 2031, and accrued payments to the Chilean State of over US$1.6 billion in H1 2026 alone (company-reported, 6-K) mean SQM keeps only part of any upcycle.
- Capex discipline matters for any re-base: FY2024 capex of $971,792,000 consumed most of OCF of $1,274,678,000, and ~US$3 billion of 2026–2028 capex is guided (company-reported) — owner's earnings will stay well below net income through the expansion.
- Margin of safety on this run's numbers: the weighted IV@15% of $9.34 sits 84.6% below the $60.75 price; equivalently the price is far above even the super-bull IV@15% of $20.55. On this base there is no margin of safety at any scenario — a buyer at $60.75 is paying for earnings power the filed-through-FY2024 base does not show, i.e. for the 2025–26 recovery being both real and durable.
- Hurdle-clearing price on this base: ~$9.34 (the weighted IV@15%).
