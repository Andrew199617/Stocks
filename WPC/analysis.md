## What it is
A diversified net-lease REIT (founded 1973): single-tenant, operationally-critical industrial, warehouse and retail properties in the US and Europe under long-term triple-net leases — tenants bear property taxes, insurance and maintenance. As of Q2 2026 (Q2 2026 release, PR Newswire 2026-07-28): 1,748 properties, 188 million sq ft, 384 tenants, weighted-average lease term 12.2 years, occupancy 98.5%, top-10 tenants 18.1% of ABR, ~99% of leases carry escalators (48% CPI-linked, 49% fixed). Q2 2026 AFFO $1.34/share (+4.7% YoY); 2026 AFFO guidance raised and narrowed to $5.19–$5.27/share (+5.2% at midpoint), investment volume assumption raised to $1.7–$2.1B; Q2 investment volume ran at a 7.4% weighted-average initial cash cap rate. Contractual same-store rent growth 2.6% YoY. Dividend $0.95/quarter declared for Q4 2026 ($3.80 annualized, ~73% of FY2025 AFFO). A REIT: the market prices it on AFFO/NAV, not on OCF-minus-capex — see SECTOR_MODEL_MISMATCH below; this DCF is a cash-discipline check, not the market's model.

Against the true per-year figures it overstates FY2023 capex by $13,702,000 (~1.3% of FY2023 OCF, lowering that year's OE — conservative direction), is exact for FY2024, and understates FY2025 capex by $55,886,000, flattering FY2025 OE by ~$0.25/share; FY2025 feeds the normalized median but is *not* the base used (the TTM pin is), so the flattered year does not set the verdict. ## Moat: 5/10 — moderate/narrow
Justification: Durable contractual cash flows (12.2y WALT, escalators on ~99% of leases, 98.5% occupancy, 384 tenants) plus an investment-grade cost-of-capital and sourcing advantage — but net lease is commodity capital with no pricing power past market cap rates, thin cap-rate-over-funding spreads, and growth bought with dilutive external capital.
- FOR: contractual duration — 12.2-year weighted-average lease term, 98.5% occupancy, ~99% of leases with escalators (48% CPI-linked, 49% fixed), 2.6% contractual same-store rent growth; diversification — 1,748 properties, 384 tenants, top-10 only 18.1% of ABR, US + Europe; cost-of-capital advantage — BBB+/Baa1 investment-grade unsecured access, 3.2% weighted-average debt rate and $2.7B liquidity at Q2 2026, forward-equity ATM machine; sale-leaseback sourcing franchise built since 1973.
- AGAINST: no pricing power beyond market cap rates at origination/renewal — net lease is commodity capital; growth is externally funded (share count +3.3%/yr) so per-share compounding leaks to new shareholders; returns are the cap-rate/funding-cost spread, which a higher-for-longer regime compresses; tenant credit events recur (Hellweg insolvency in 2026 — exposure cut to ~0.9% of ABR per the Q2 call; Q2 2026 impairments $79,400,000) and single-tenant assets are binary on default; ~73% AFFO payout retains little.
- ROIC evidence, as required: fundamentals.json produces **no roic_proxy series for WPC** (no operating-income series in its extraction) and its ~99% "gross margin" is a rental-accounting artifact (rent as revenue, property costs borne by tenants and recorded below the line), so neither series can carry the score. The 5/10 rests on the contractual/structural evidence above and sits in line with scored peers (KIM 5, EXR 5, CCI 6, VICI 7 — VICI's scarcer experiential assets and CCI's tower scarcity both rate above a diversified commodity net-lease book).
- A 5 gives CAP = 8.5 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 8.5 years, 15-year projection.
