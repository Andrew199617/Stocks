## Moat: 5/10 — moderate
Justification: Scale network and long-term contractual revenue give real switching friction, but the model is capital-intensive, pricing is contested by Penske/Enterprise and private fleets, and revenue growth has stalled.
- FOR: Scale North American network: ~240,000 vehicles under management and ~800 maintenance locations (company profile, Oct 2026) — density smaller rivals cannot replicate; long-term contractual model: over 90% of revenue contractual per management on the Q2 2026 call, seventh consecutive quarter of comparable EPS growth; integrated offer — Fleet Management, Dedicated Transportation and Supply Chain (~320 warehouses, ~100M sq ft) embedded in customer operations, raising switching costs.
- AGAINST: Capital intensity: fleet capex exceeded operating cash flow in FY2023 and FY2024 and has consumed nearly all of it for a decade — owner's earnings positive in only 2 of the last 5 years; freight-cycle and used-vehicle-price exposure (rental utilization and used tractor/truck pricing swing earnings; FY2025 revenue +0.23% YoY, 3-yr revenue CAGR 1.78% in dcf_numbers.json); Penske, Enterprise and customers' own private fleets cap pricing, and leverage is structural (net debt $7,447M plus $1,035M lease liabilities).
- ROIC evidence: the engine could not compute an ROIC proxy or recent gross margins — operating-income and COGS tags are null in EDGAR for Ryder — so there is no filing-direct evidence of returns above the cost of capital; ROE was 13.2% / 15.7% / 16.3% in FY2023–25 (fundamentals.json), respectable but levered. A 5 gives CAP = 8.5 years (dcf_numbers.json).

## Thoughts / method range
- The OE model's answer is a statement about fit as much as about Ryder: a lessor whose capex *is* its product will show negative owner's earnings in any fleet-investment window and strongly positive ones when capex pauses — FY2020 OE/share was $19.19 and FY2025 $10.05 in the CSV, on the same business. The median window (FY2023–25) happened to catch the heaviest investment years ($3,234M and $2,683M capex) plus the first year of the downturn in spend.
- The equity story the market is pricing at $237.00 (JPMorgan upgraded to Overweight on October 5, 2026, target $298) is the comparable-EPS / ROE / buyback story: $14.40–$14.80 of guided 2026 comparable EPS at ~16x, ROE 17–18%, and a shrinking share count. None of that contradicts the F: it says the cash the fleet throws off after replacing and growing the fleet was negative on the normalized window this method capitalizes.
- If capex stays at the FY2025/TTM run-rate (~$1.8–2.1B) against ~$2.5–2.7B of OCF, a future run's median window turns positive and this name re-scores mechanically — that is the thing to watch, starting with the October 22, 2026 Q3 print and its capex guide.
