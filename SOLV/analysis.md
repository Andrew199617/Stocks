## What it is
The former 3M health care business, spun off on April 1, 2024. Three reportable segments after the September 2025 divestiture (segments.json, FY2025 revenue): MedSurg $4,817,000,000 (57.9% of sales, +3.88% YoY) — advanced wound care (incl. V.A.C. negative-pressure therapy) and infection prevention / surgical solutions; Health Information Systems $1,360,000,000 (16.3%, +4.13%) — 360 Encompass revenue-cycle and coding software; Dental Solutions $1,349,000,000 (16.2%, +4.17%) — Filtek restoratives and orthodontics. Purification & Filtration contributed a $497,000,000 stub in FY2025 before its sale to Thermo Fisher Scientific closed on September 2, 2025 for $4.0 billion in cash before customary adjustments, with net proceeds of $3.4 billion used primarily to pay down debt (company release, paraphrased). All Other (drinking-water filtration, product supplied to 3M) was $302,000,000. In Q2 2026 the company announced it plans to separate HIS as well, and it closed the Acera acquisition in December 2025 (acquisitions of $696,000,000 in FY2025, fundamentals.json).

## Moat: 5/10 — moderate / narrow
Justification: category leadership and recurring consumables, but measured returns and margins do not clear a wider bar. Gross margin fell every year on file — 57.7% (FY2022) → 57.3% → 55.6% → 53.5% (FY2025), 54.7% on the TTM in fundamentals.json. The ROIC proxy (fundamentals.json) ran 11.5% (FY2022) and 11.9% (FY2023) on a carve-out capital base, fell to 8.0% in FY2024, and prints 21.5% in FY2025 only because reported operating income of $2,181,000,000 contains the Purification & Filtration gain (the Q3 2025 cash-flow statement backs a $1,518,000,000 gain on sale of business out of net income; the gain sits in operating income per the company's non-GAAP reconciliations — sources paraphrased).
- FOR: #1 advanced wound care position (V.A.C. negative-pressure wound therapy) with recurring consumables pull-through; premium dental restorative brands (Filtek) and orthodontic franchise with dentist familiarity / switching friction; HIS 360 Encompass revenue-cycle platform embedded in hospital coding and billing workflows — real switching costs; regulatory-cleared portfolio, hospital channel relationships and materials IP (owned for its field of use) inherited from 3M; diversified recurring-revenue mix (MedSurg 57.9% / HIS 16.3% / Dental 16.2% of FY2025 sales).
- AGAINST: gross-margin erosion of ~4pp in three years — cost, mix, the 3M supply-agreement markup and tariffs have beaten pricing power so far; underlying ROIC proxy of 8.0% in FY2024 is at, not well above, the cost of capital; contested in every segment (Medtronic / Smith+Nephew in wound care, Dentsply Sirona / Envista in dental, Oracle Health in HIS, per search-result summaries) with pricing pressure in surgical supplies; the 3M long-term supply agreement gives the supplier a 2027 option to raise certain raw-material costs, disclosed as a possible ~100bp margin headwind; the planned HIS separation removes the stickiest software asset, and reported revenue growth on file is under 1% a year.
- A 5 gives CAP = 8.5 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 8.5 years, 15-year projection.

## Flags — every fragility flag resolved
- **STALE_FY** (latest fiscal year ended 10 months ago): ttm.py was run and its full legs are quoted above. The TTM base (−$1.63/share) was NOT pinned, for the documented separation-peak reason stated in the Owner's earnings section; the median base is used and the TTM result is carried as the F end of the method range. Post-FY evidence (Q2 2026: guidance raised, normalized organic growth ~4%) is reflected in the recalibrated scenario starts rather than in a hand-built base.
- **NI_OCF_DIVERGENCE** (net income and OCF differ by >40% in 2024, 2025): identified, two different one-offs. FY2025: net income of $1,556,000,000 contains the $1,518,000,000 pre-tax gain on the P&F sale (Q3 2025 cash-flow statement, paraphrased source), which is investing cash, not operating cash — reported operating income and the maintenance OE definition are flattered by it, the conservative OCF-based series is not. FY2024: net income of $479,000,000 vs OCF of $1,185,000,000 is the spin year — separation costs, the new standalone interest burden and carve-out tax allocations hit earnings ahead of cash. Consequence: the verdict rests on the conservative series only.
- **BASE_DIVERGENCE** (latest FY OE/share −$0.98 vs 3-yr median $3.99): both ends are distorted, in opposite directions. The latest FY is the trough: OCF of $369,000,000 absorbed peak separation and divestiture costs in the year the P&F cash contribution left. The median ($3.99, FY2024) is the least-distorted filed year — first standalone year, full debt load — but it still includes a full year of the now-sold P&F business and a partial year of separation costs. The median is used as the standardized base, the latest/TTM are disclosed as the F end of the range, and probabilities are skewed bear-ward to charge for the distortion.
- **WIDE_SCENARIO_SPREAD** (super-bull IV 4.52x super-bear IV): real — the outcome genuinely hinges on whether separation costs roll off as guided. Confidence is low (and would be capped at medium by rule in any case); the verdict is sized to the conservative end.
- **HIGH_NET_DEBT** (net debt 5.94x normalized OE): stated from the filing — total debt $5,035,000,000 (all long-term at FY2025), cash $878,000,000, net debt $4,157,000,000, plus lease liabilities of $212,000,000 (dcf_numbers.json). Leverage is down from $8,010,000,000 of gross debt at FY2024 thanks to the P&F proceeds, and buybacks have begun ($355,000,000 of repurchases in H1 2026, fundamentals.json dividends block), but at 5.9x the normalized OE base a failed normalization squeezes equity value fast — this is priced into the 45% bear-or-worse weight.

## My thoughts / overlay
- The honest headline is the method range: on the normalized median base ($3.99/share) this run prints Tier D (weighted IRR 7.41%, IV@15% $33.24); on the latest-FY base (−$0.98) and the TTM base (−$1.63) there is no positive conservative OE at all — Tier F. The preliminary default run (median base, default probabilities/offsets) printed 7.18% / $32.08, also D, so the recalibration did not manufacture the verdict. Every defensible input set fails the 15% hurdle at $85.81 by a wide margin: even the super-bull scenario alone yields only a 12.71% IRR.
- The bull story is real but it is a normalization story, not a DCF story at this price: separation costs roll off in 2027, HIS separates, Acera compounds, buybacks shrink the count. Management's own numbers say the prize is roughly $1 billion of underlying free cash flow against roughly $200 million reported for 2026 — at a $15.04B market cap, even full delivery of that underlying figure is a mid-single-digit cash yield before SBC, which is exactly what the 7.41% weighted IRR is saying.
- What would change the verdict: two filed periods of conservative OE back at or above the FY2024 level with separation costs visibly rolling off (which would lift both the median and any TTM base), or a price near the hurdle levels below.
- Hurdle-clearing prices (dcf_numbers.json hurdle_prices, median base): clears 15% at ≤$33.24, 20% at ≤$23.59, 25% at ≤$18.26.
