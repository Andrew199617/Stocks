## What it is
America's largest military shipbuilder: Newport News (sole U.S. designer/builder/refueler of nuclear-powered aircraft carriers, one of only two builders of nuclear submarines, co-building Virginia- and Columbia-class with General Dynamics), Ingalls (amphibious assault ships, surface combatants, Coast Guard cutters), and Mission Technologies (unmanned systems, AI/cyber/C5ISR). Revenue FY2025 $12.484B, up 8.2% YoY. Backlog $57.3B at 2026-06-30 (search: Q2 2026 reports), with $6.7B of Q2 awards and a $5.1B sole-source Truman refueling/complex overhaul award in September 2026. Q2 2026 revenue $3.418B, up 10.9% YoY, EPS $5.27; management raised 2026 shipbuilding revenue guidance to $10.2–$10.4B and shipbuilding margin guidance to 6.0–6.5%.

## Moat: 6/10 — moderate-strong franchise, capped economics
Justification: an irreplaceable sole-source position in nuclear carriers and a duopoly in nuclear submarines, with a $57.3B backlog — but the customer is a monopsonist that caps margins by contract structure, and measured returns have compressed to single digits.
- FOR: Sole U.S. designer, builder and refueler of nuclear-powered aircraft carriers (Newport News) — the Truman RCOH award was made without competition because only Newport News has the facilities and knowledge; one of only two nuclear-submarine builders; barriers are effectively absolute (nuclear certification, dry docks, cleared skilled workforce, decades of Navy integration); $57.3B backlog at 2026-06-30 gives multi-year revenue visibility; every carrier built creates a ~50-year refueling/overhaul tail at the same sole-source yard.
- AGAINST: Single-customer concentration — substantially all backlog is U.S. government orders; the government sets the economics: cost-plus and fixed-price-incentive contracts cap upside and leave cost overruns, labor shortages and throughput delays with HII; no pricing power in the commercial sense; Mission Technologies competes in contested services markets; measured profitability has structurally compressed (below).
- **ROIC / margin evidence (fundamentals.json `roic_proxy` series):** ROIC proxy was 29.0% / 23.4% / 32.0% / 21.1% / 22.3% in FY2016–FY2020, then fell to 8.2% (FY2021), 7.7% (FY2022), 10.2% (FY2023), 6.5% (FY2024), 7.3% (FY2025). Operating margin compressed in parallel from 12.4% (FY2016) to 5.4% / 5.3% / 6.8% / 4.6% / 5.3% in FY2021–FY2025. Gross margin is only captured in fundamentals.json for FY2016 (38.0%) and FY2017 (40.3%); the later years' COGS tag is not separated there, so the operating-margin series is the usable trend. A moat of 7+ is not justified: recent ROIC sits in single digits, not well above the cost of capital through the recent cycle — the franchise is durable but its economics are contract-capped. Hence 6.
- A 6 gives CAP = 9.8 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 9.8 years, 15-year projection.

## My thoughts / overlay
- The honest headline is the range: even the super-bull case (19.3% OE growth start, 1.25x OE) only reaches a 17.96% IRR at $270 — no scenario in the set clears 20%. The stock at $270 prices in a margin/throughput recovery that the last five years of ROIC (6.5–10.2%) and operating margin (4.6–6.8%) do not yet show.
- Quality is not the debate: the franchise position is as durable as any in U.S. defense, backlog is $57.3B and growing, revenue is compounding at 5–11%, and SBC is trivial ($54M FY2025). The debate is cash conversion: OCF has printed $760M (FY2021), $766M (FY2022), $970M (FY2023), $393M (FY2024) and $1,196M (FY2025) (financials_history.csv) with no stable relationship to earnings, and H1 2026 was cash-negative.
- Method range: on the TTM base there is no positive OE to capitalize at all (Tier F mechanics); on the latest-FY base ($18.83) the same scenarios would print a higher IRR. The median-base C is the middle, defensible reading, and the spread between those bases is itself the finding.
- Hurdle prices (dcf_numbers.json): clears 15% only at or below the weighted IV@15% of $163.99; IV@20% $114.06; IV@25% $86.84. At $270 the engine's final upside_at_15 is −39.26% (dcf_numbers.json).
