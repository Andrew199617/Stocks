## What it is
Closed-end, non-diversified management investment company regulated as a business development company (BDC), founded 2004 and externally managed by Ares Capital Management LLC (Ares Management subsidiary). At FY2025 it was the largest publicly traded BDC by market capitalization, with $31,235M of total assets and a $29,485M investment portfolio at fair value (FY2025 10-K / Q4 2025 release). It lends primarily first-lien senior secured loans to US middle-market (mostly sponsor-backed) companies. As a BDC/RIC it must distribute the bulk of taxable income: dividend declared $1.92/share in FY2025, FY2024 and FY2023 (fundamentals.json dps_declared), paid $1,264M in FY2025.

**TTM check (ttm.py, ttm.json):** OCF FY −$1,717,000,000 − prior YTD −$517,000,000 + current YTD $520,000,000 = TTM −$680,000,000; capex $0 and SBC $0 (all three legs filled by the overrides); shares 718,000,000 → TTM OE −$680,000,000 = **−$0.9471/share**. **Base used = median of the last 3 fiscal years' net income per diluted share, $2.4391/share** (base_override in judgment.json, bounded authority (c) — construction from this run's financials_history.csv only): FY2023 $1,522,000,000 / 575,000,000 = $2.6470; FY2024 $1,522,000,000 / 624,000,000 = $2.4391; FY2025 $1,299,000,000 / 699,000,000 = $1.8584; median $2.4391. The median normalization mirrors the engine's own base method. Cross-check: the base sits above the current run rate (FY2025 NII $2.02/share; Q2 2026 core EPS $0.47/quarter) — that is deliberate normalization across the 2023–24 unrealized-gain years, and it is paid for with a 45% combined bear-or-worse probability weight and a flat base-case growth start. ## Moat: 5/10 — moderate/narrow
Justification: scale and sourcing, not pricing power over a proprietary asset. The measured return history caps the score: fundamentals.json ROE was 13.59% (FY2023), 11.40% (FY2024), 9.07% (FY2025) — around, and falling toward, the cost of capital. The `roic_proxy` series is null for this filer (an investment company presents no operating-income / invested-capital split the proxy can use) and no gross-margin series exists (a BDC presents total investment income and expenses, not COGS), so no ROIC or gross-margin claim is made.
- FOR: largest publicly traded BDC by market cap ($31.2B total assets, FY2025 10-K) — scale in origination, diversification across hundreds of portfolio companies, and funding access; Ares Credit Group platform — sponsor relationships and direct-lending origination/underwriting infrastructure built since the 2004 IPO, with exited investments generating ~13% asset-level gross IRR since inception (company reporting); cheap termed-out funding — investment-grade unsecured access, debt/equity 1.12x at FY2025 within the 0.9–1.25x target range, weighted average stated interest on debt 4.8–4.9% (Q2 2026 presentation); dividend durability — $0.48/quarter declared continuously with a taxable spillover buffer (~$1.38/share per Q2 2026 coverage) covering multiple quarters of payout; credit record — non-accruals 2.4% at cost / 1.4% at fair value at Q2 2026, below the ~4% BDC historical average.
- AGAINST: the product is commoditized credit — senior middle-market loans are replicable by BXSL, Blue Owl, Apollo, other BDCs and returning banks, and spread compression flows straight into NII/share; the franchise is rented, not owned — externally managed, with base and incentive fees paid to Ares Capital Management; measured returns are ordinary and falling (ROE above; NAV/share $19.94 at FY2025 → $19.35 at Q2 2026); leverage (1.12–1.15x debt/equity) amplifies credit losses, and PIK interest/dividends were ~15% of Q2 2026 total investment income (company release coverage) — earnings quality below the headline; per-share growth is bought with issuance — share count +10.5%/yr via ATM offerings, and NII/share fell from $2.30 (FY2024) to $2.02 (FY2025) on the company's reported figures.
- A 5 gives CAP = 8.5 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges to the 2.5% terminal rate over 8.5 years, 15-year projection.

- What the DCF cannot see is what BDC buyers actually underwrite: NAV and dividend coverage. On those, ARCC at $18.72 trades at a ~3% discount to Q2 2026 NAV of $19.35 with a $1.92 dividend covered by FY2025 NII of $2.02/share but **not** covered by Q2 2026 core EPS ($0.47 vs $0.48) for a second consecutive quarter — the spillover buffer (~$1.38/share) is what bridges that gap, and it is finite.
- Watch items: non-accrual trend (2.4% at cost at Q2 2026, up from 1.8% at FY2025), PIK share of income, whether core EPS re-covers the dividend, and NAV/share direction. Q3 2026 results are scheduled for October 27, 2026.
- Margin of safety: none on the weighted case — IV@15% $17.93 is 4.2% **below** the price. A buyer at $18.72 is paid if the base case (flat per-share earnings on the normalized base) holds, and is exposed to the bear case ($13.72) if credit turns.
