# CR — Crane Company
Engine 5.4.0 pass-1 run, 2026-10-18. Price $205.83 (nasdaq_quote_api), market cap $12.06B — in scope (<$40B). All figures below are copied from dcf_numbers.json, ttm.json, financials_history.csv or fundamentals.json — none are recomputed.

## What it is
Diversified manufacturer of highly engineered industrial products (founded 1855, Stamford, CT) in two segments since the April 2023 separation that spun the payment & merchandising business off as Crane NXT: **Aerospace & Advanced Technologies** (sensing, landing-gear, power and fluid components for commercial/military aerospace, defense and space; FY2025 sales $1.05B, segment operating margin 25.0%) and **Process Flow Technologies** (engineered valves, pumps and systems for chemical, water/wastewater and general process industries; FY2025 sales $1.26B, margin 21.0%). Portfolio is still moving: Engineered Materials was sold, cryogenics acquisitions (Vian, CryoWorks, Technifab) closed in FY2024 ($200.5M of acquisition payments), the Baker Hughes Precision Sensors & Instrumentation business (Druck, Panametrics, Reuter-Stokes; ~$390M of sales, ~$60M adjusted EBITDA) plus optek closed effective January 2026 for $1.15B cash (H1 2026 acquisition payments $1,355.5M in the filed history), and on September 14, 2026 Crane agreed to buy Trillium's U.S. pump business for $240M (~$115M of expected revenue).

## OVERRIDE — capex (filing)
- **The gap:** the preliminary engine run exited 4 with `DATA_GAP:capex` — Crane files capital expenditures only under the us-gaap tag `PaymentsForCapitalImprovements`, which is not among the engine's candidate tags, so no capex value exists in companyfacts under any tag the engine reads for any year.

Corroborated by Crane's Q4 2025 earnings-release Adjusted Free Cash Flow table (capital expenditures $53.5M FY2025 / $36.6M FY2024).
- **How it was applied:** the engine filled every period where the tag is absent (2021-12-31 through 2025-12-31; the tag is never filed, so all five history years carry capex_tag "OVERRIDE" — the pre-2023 years are old-perimeter history and are not used for the verdict). ttm.py filled all three capex legs on the OCF anchor timeline, prorated by days (FY leg $48,500,000 over 365 days; both YTD legs $24,050,685 over 181 days; they cancel, leaving TTM capex exactly $48,500,000) and reported them in overrides_used with no warnings.

## Moat: 6/10 — moderate, proven in margins and returns, not wide
Justification: specified-in, sole-source engineered components with certification switching costs and an aftermarket annuity. Evidence first (fundamentals.json): gross margin 40.3% (FY2023) → 40.7% (FY2024) → 42.2% (FY2025); roic_proxy 14.8% / 17.9% / 12.0% over the same years (FY2025's denominator is inflated by the pre-close PSI debt and escrow cash; FY2022's 0.2% is the separation-charge year).
- FOR: sole-source / specified-in mission-critical positions — aerospace sensing, landing-gear and fluid-handling components certified into airframes and process plants, so switching means requalification; aftermarket and repair annuity on a large installed base; pricing power in the filed numbers (gross margin expansion above; FY2025 segment operating margins 25.0% and 21.0%); returns above the cost of capital on the post-separation perimeter; Crane Business System discipline, record backlog and raised FY2026 guidance (adjusted EPS $6.85–7.05 after Q2 2026).
- AGAINST: mid-teens ROIC is not a wide-moat return profile and FY2022 printed 0.2%; Process Flow rides chemical/process capex cycles and Aerospace rides airframer build rates and defense budgets; growth is partly bought at full prices (PSI ~$1.15B for ~$60M of adjusted EBITDA, only a 10% ROIC targeted by year five; Trillium at 14.6x estimated 2026 adjusted EBITDA); larger rivals (Parker Hannifin, Eaton, Emerson, ITT) contest the same content; no network effects — qualification moats erode if programs are re-competed.
- A 6 gives CAP = 9.8 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 9.8 years, 15-year projection.
