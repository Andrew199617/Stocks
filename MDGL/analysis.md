## What it is
Single-product biopharmaceutical company built on Rezdiffra (resmetirom), a once-daily oral, liver-directed thyroid hormone receptor-beta agonist and the first FDA-approved therapy (March 2024) for metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (MASH) with moderate-to-advanced (F2–F3) liver fibrosis. Launched in the U.S. in 2024 and in Germany in September 2025 (per Q2 2026 reporting). Everything else — the oral GLP-1 MGL-2086 (Phase 1 dosing initiated June 2026), ervogastat, and six siRNA programs — is pipeline, acquired or licensed in 2025–26 and unapproved. One product is 100% of revenue.

## My thoughts / overlay
- There is no method range to hide behind here: TTM base -$11.27/share, latest-year-only -$12.84/share, 3-yr median -$20.09/share. Every defensible variant is Tier F. Anyone quoting a DCF value for MDGL today is projecting the inflection, not measuring owner's earnings.
- The inflection itself is real on the top line and is the bull case in full: nearly $1.3 billion of trailing-twelve-month net sales, more than 49,000 patients on therapy at June 30, 2026 (over 50,000 in early July), Q2 2026 sales up 71% year over year, a Germany launch, an expanded U.S. field force, three new resmetirom patents in Q2 2026, and a Phase 3 outcomes program in compensated cirrhosis (F4c) that would open a second, currently unapproved population (company releases and reporting, cited search results). None of that is owner's earnings yet: over the same twelve months the company burned $197,716,000 of operating cash and paid out $121,429,000 in stock compensation.
- The cost structure is the question, not demand. Q2 2026 operating expenses were $420.6 million against $364.3 million of revenue (company release, cited search result) — SG&A alone was $289,375,000 in the quarter as the launch build-out annualizes, and management guided FY2026 SG&A higher than FY2025 while R&D stays roughly flat including further business-development upfronts. Revenue growing 71% while the quarterly net loss widens year over year ($57.9 million in Q2 2026 vs $42.3 million in Q2 2025, cited search result) is the pattern that has to break.
- What would change the tier: a filed period in which OCF − capex − SBC is positive, on a TTM pin first and a fiscal year second. On the current trajectory that requires the launch cost base to stop growing faster than gross profit — watch SG&A leverage and the gross-to-net step-up into the high-30% range guided for 2026, which cuts the other way. The next earnings date is October 29, 2026 (cited search result); the FY2026 10-K is the hard refresh trigger.
- Quality note for the file: a first-in-class drug, ~$1.3B of trailing sales inside three years of approval, $838.9 million of cash and securities at June 30, 2026, and patent protection to 2045 are genuine franchise assets. The $449.74 price and $10.090B market cap are paying for the conversion of that franchise into cash flow that has not yet occurred — with no margin of safety measurable in OE terms, and with Wegovy now competing for the same patients.
