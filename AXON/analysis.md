# AXON — Axon Enterprise, Inc.
Run date: 2026-10-13 (engine v4). In scope: market cap ~$34.0B < $40B.

## What it is
The dominant U.S. public-safety technology platform: TASER conducted-energy weapons (>90% U.S. share), Axon Body body-worn cameras (est. 60–80% of U.S. law-enforcement agencies), and the Evidence.com cloud / records / AI software stack (Draft One report-writing, Axon 911 via the Prepared and Carbyne acquisitions, Dedrone counter-drone inside Platform Solutions) sold largely as bundled, multi-year — up to 10-year — Officer Safety Plan subscriptions. Two segments: Connected Devices (Q2 2026: $507M, +35% YoY) and Software & Services ($398M, +36% YoY). Q2 2026: total revenue $904M (+35% YoY, the tenth consecutive +30% quarter), ARR $1.639B (+39%), net revenue retention 126%, future contracted bookings $15.1B (+40%), adjusted EBITDA $242M (26.8% margin) vs GAAP net income of only $29M. FY2026 revenue growth guided up to 32–34% with adjusted EBITDA margin held at 25.5% and SBC guided at $590–620M. Revenue growth is not the debate here — it has compounded at ~33% for five years (FY2021 $863M → FY2025 $2,780M → TTM $3,219M). The debate is entirely what shareholders actually keep after stock compensation.

## Owner's earnings (SEC EDGAR, filing-direct)
- FY2025: operating cash flow $211.3M; capex $136.3M; SBC $634.2M.
- **Conservative owner's earnings FY2025: −$559.2M = −$6.79/share** (latest year). **Normalized base (median of last 3 FY OE/share): −$0.68/share** (2023 −$0.02, 2024 −$0.68, 2025 −$6.79). OE has been positive in only **1 of the last 5 fiscal years** (2022, +$1.01/share — the one year SBC dipped to $106M).
- Maintenance version FY2025: −$152.6M — also deeply negative, so the verdict does not depend on the capex definition. (Its gap vs the conservative figure is mostly the −$277M ΔNWC swing from receivables/contract-asset growth on +33% sales, not quality.)
- The single cause is SBC, and it is structural, not a typo: SBC was $131M in 2023 (8.4% of revenue) → $383M in 2024 (18.4%) → $634M in 2025 (22.8%) → $633M TTM (19.7%). The step-up is the broad-based 2024 eXponential Stock Plan plus the 2024 CEO Performance Award — roughly $330M of the 2025 total per company guidance, vesting in seven tranches on stock-price, operational, and time conditions. It recurs in guidance: 2026 SBC is guided at $590–620M, i.e. still ~16–17% of projected sales. Management's own long-range discipline target is only "annual SBC dilution below 2.5%" by 2028.
- Even before SBC, the cash engine is thin at this price: TTM free cash flow (OCF − capex) is $133.2M — a 0.39% yield on the $413.35 price. Axon guides to ~$450M of FY2026 FCF (its definition, i.e. before the SBC cost), which would still be only a ~1.3% yield if hit. Adjusted EBITDA (~25–27% margins) is real operating leverage, but it is paid out to employees in stock before it reaches owners.

## Moat score: 7/10 — strong
FOR — (1) Near-monopoly hardware anchors: >90% U.S. TASER share and 60–80% body-camera share, with no credible second source at scale. (2) Genuine switching costs: the camera, the weapon, Evidence.com chain-of-custody storage, records, and now 911 dispatch feed one workflow; agencies sign bundled plans up to 10 years, evidence archives cannot be casually migrated, and officers are trained on the system — NRR of 126% and $15.1B of future contracted bookings (+40%) are the revealed-preference proof. (3) Land-and-expand economics: ARR +39% to $1.64B, each hardware placement seeds a decade of high-margin software (Software & Services gross margin ~71–75%). (4) A widening platform: AI Era / Draft One monetizes the installed camera base, and Dedrone/Prepared/Carbyne extend the same installed relationship into drones and dispatch.
AGAINST — (1) Motorola Solutions is a real, well-capitalized rival with land-mobile-radio incumbency and its own video/CommandCentral stack (Silvus acquisition, 2025) — the moat is contested every renewal, not unassailable. (2) The customer is a municipal budget: procurement is political, cyclical, and price-visible; a >$400 stock comp-funded cost structure is hard to defend in a public bid forever. (3) Returns on capital do not yet corroborate a wide moat — GAAP operating income was negative in FY2025 (−$62M) and ROIC is low-single-digit once SBC is counted; the moat shows up in revenue retention, not yet in owner's earnings. (4) Concentration in one sector and one geography (U.S. law enforcement), plus live ethical/regulatory scrutiny of police AI/surveillance, caps the terminal story.
CAP from moat 7: clamp(2 + 1.3×7) = **11.1 years** — moot for the DCF (no positive OE to fade), but it records that the franchise itself is durable; what fails the screen is pay, not position.

## FLAGS — resolving every flag
The engine's Tier-F branch emits no `fragility_flags` list (dcf_numbers.json carries the F note instead), so each v4 flag was checked manually:
- **STALE_FY** — FY2025 ended ~9.5 months before this run. Resolved with the filing-derived TTM base above: TTM OE −$6.06/share. Verdict unchanged — if anything the TTM is the honest current run-rate: H1'26 OCF was still negative (−$11.4M YTD) on receivables/contract-asset build.
- **BASE_DIVERGENCE** — Fires in substance, and it is the story of the stock: latest FY −$6.79 vs 3-yr median −$0.68 is a 10× gap. Resolution: neither is a data error. The median is flattered by 2023, the last year before the 2024 XSP/CEO awards hit the SBC line ($131M); the latest year and the TTM (−$6.06) reflect the current, guided comp structure ($590–620M again in 2026). The current-structure bases (latest FY, TTM) are the right ones; the median is rejected with this written reason — and all three are negative, so the tier is identical under any choice.
- **NI_OCF_DIVERGENCE** — Borderline in FY2025 and resolved: net income $124.7M vs OCF $211.3M (gap $86.7M, just above D&A of $83.2M). The bridge is non-cash and identified in the 10-K: +$634.2M SBC addback, +$83.2M D&A, +$38.9M debt-inducement expense on the 2027 Notes conversion, partly offset by −$140.0M of fair-value gains on strategic investments/marketable securities and deferred-tax moves (FY2025 tax was a −$105.7M benefit, driven by SBC windfalls and the July 2025 tax-law change). Note the direction: GAAP net income *overstates* owner economics here because it also books those investment gains while operating income was −$62M. The OCF-based OE is the clean series; no KO-style tax-deposit distortion.
- **DILUTION** — Fires, hard: engine share-count CAGR +4.3%/yr (diluted shares 75.5M in 2023 → 78.6M in 2024 → 82.4M in 2025 → 82.5M H1'26). This is SBC issuance outrunning buybacks, already reflected in the per-share math; it is the balance-sheet twin of the SBC line and the reason revenue/share has grown ~200% while revenue grew ~280% over five years.
- **HIGH_NET_DEBT / balance sheet** — Not computable against a negative OE base, stated in absolutes instead: FY2025 net debt $609.6M (cash $1,201.1M − debt $1,810.7M, the debt being senior notes issued in 2025 — interest expense jumped from $7.1M to $94.2M). By Q2 2026 net debt had risen to ~$1,134M (cash $597.7M − debt $1,731.8M) after ~$550M of Q1'26 acquisition spend (Prepared/Carbyne) and negative H1 OCF. Leverage is serviceable against ~25%+ adjusted-EBITDA margins but the direction is wrong for an F-tier name: cash is being spent on M&A while owners' cash flow is negative.
- **LOW_OE_YIELD / HIGH_TV_SHARE / WIDE_SCENARIO_SPREAD** — Not computable, in the worst way: OE yield is negative and 100% of the $413 price is future/terminal value (consensus prices it at ~32× 2028 adjusted EPS). These are the substance of the verdict, not footnotes.
- **BASIS_BREAK_SUSPECT** — Does not fire: no >60% revenue year in the last five (range +27% to +36%). Acquisitions (Dedrone contributing >$100M in Q2'26, Prepared, Carbyne) inflate the small Platform Solutions line (+123%) but total-company growth is predominantly organic and the OE series break in 2024–25 is the SBC plans, resolved above — not a divestiture, restatement, or M&A basis change.
- **OE_VOLATILE** — Fires in substance: OE/share over five years runs +$1.01 → −$0.02 → −$0.68 → −$6.79 (and −$3.46 in 2021). Not business cyclicality — it tracks the SBC tranche calendar (2021 CEO award year, the quiet 2022–23 window, the 2024 XSP/CEO awards). The normalized median exists for exactly this, and it is still negative.
- **THIN_HISTORY / SECTOR_MODEL_MISMATCH** — Neither applies: 10 fiscal years of clean EDGAR history, and Industrials is a sector the OE model fits — Axon simply fails it on pay discipline. No customer-concentration or going-concern flag: the customer base is thousands of agencies; red flags are SBC/governance (repeated mega-grants), acquisition pace, and valuation, all covered above.

## My thoughts / overlay
- This is the SNAP pattern at 30× the market cap and with a genuinely strong franchise underneath: a 33%-growth, 126%-NRR platform whose entire operating cash generation — and more — is currently paid out in stock. The screen's answer is unambiguous: with negative OE in every base period, **there is no price at which today's OE stream clears a 15% hurdle**, and no margin-of-safety price to quote from this method. At $413 you are not buying owner's earnings; you are pre-paying for SBC normalization that management has guided *against* for 2026 ($590–620M) and only targets (<2.5% dilution) by 2028.
- What would change the tier: two consecutive filings of positive conservative OE — which requires either OCF roughly tripling at current SBC, or SBC roughly halving at current OCF. The 2028 targets (~$6B revenue, ~28% adjusted EBITDA margin, 60% FCF conversion) would get OCF there *if* SBC simultaneously falls to the targeted dilution rate; that conjunction is the bull case, and it is a 2028 story being sold at a 2026 price (the stock is already down ~41% in a year and still fails).
- Method range: unusually, there is none on the tier — latest-FY, median, TTM, and maintenance bases all give F. The spread lives in how one values the post-2028 normalized business, which is outside the OE screen by design.

## Final verdict
- **Final tier: F** (= tier by numbers; no override — there is no positive owner's earnings to underwrite any hurdle)
- Confidence: **Medium** — high confidence in the arithmetic (OE is negative under every base and both definitions, on 10 years of filings), medium overall because a credible normalization path exists on paper (2028 targets); it is simply not in any filing or guidance yet.
- One-liner: Elite public-safety franchise, moat 7/10 — but SBC of $633M TTM is ~2.4× its pre-SBC free cash flow, so owner's earnings are negative in 4 of the last 5 years (−$6.06/share TTM) and it cannot clear the 15% hurdle at any price on an OE DCF; at $413.35 you pay ~$34B for a 2028 SBC-discipline promise, not for current owner's earnings.
