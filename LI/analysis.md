# LI — Li Auto Inc.
Engine 5.4.0 run, 2026-10-18. Price $11.34 per ADS (nasdaq_quote_api). All figures below are copied from key_numbers.md, dcf_numbers.json, financials_history.csv, fundamentals.json, or the cited Q2 2026 reporting — none are recomputed.

## What it is
Chinese premium new-energy vehicle maker: extended-range (EREV) L-series family SUVs (L6/L8/L9) plus a growing battery-electric line (i6/i8, MEGA). Dual-listed Nasdaq (LI) / HKEX (2015). It was the EREV/family-cabin pioneer; that niche is now contested by Huawei AITO, Leapmotor and others, and FY2025–2026 is a downturn: FY2025 revenue fell (latest rev YoY -18.85% in key_numbers.md; -22.3% in RMB in the FY2025 report), deliveries fell to 406,000 in 2025, and Q2 2026 revenue was RMB 25.7B, down 15.1% YoY, with deliveries 98,330, down 11.5% YoY.

## Moat: 3/10 — weak
Justification: Once-clear EREV/family-SUV differentiation has been copied, pricing power has collapsed, and returns on capital have not been sustained.
- **ROIC evidence (fundamentals.json, roic_proxy):** -2.09% (FY2020), -4.18% (FY2021), -18.04% (FY2022), -26.05% (FY2023), +45.22% (FY2024), -1.80% (FY2025). That is not a franchise earning well above its cost of capital through a cycle — only FY2024 is strongly positive, and the proxy itself is noisy in the early negative-equity years. A moat of 7+ would need the opposite pattern, so 3/10 is the evidence-based score, not a conservative haircut.
- **Gross margin evidence (fundamentals.json):** 21.33% (FY2021), 19.41% (FY2022), 22.20% (FY2023), 20.53% (FY2024), 18.68% (FY2025) — a slow fade in the annuals, then a cliff in 2026: Q2 2026 total gross margin 11.0% and vehicle margin 9.4%, vs 20.1% / 19.4% a year earlier in the cited quarterly reporting.
- FOR: Family-SUV brand and cabin/living-space positioning in the RMB 200,000+ segment, where it held a top NEV position in Q2 2026; scale (over RMB 100B revenue for three straight years, 406,000 deliveries in 2025); owned retail/service footprint and 5C supercharging network (4,141 stations at end-July 2026 per Q2 2026 reporting); in-house stack in development (Mach M100 chip, 5C batteries, drive-by-wire, VLA assisted driving); fortress cash (RMB 87.5B at Q2 2026) and buybacks ($631.5M repurchased per Q2 2026 reporting) cushion the downturn.
- AGAINST: EREV differentiation copied by Huawei AITO, Leapmotor and others selling into the same niche; no lock-in or network effect — buyers cross-shop on price/spec with near-zero switching costs in a price war; pricing power lost (vehicle margin 19.4% → 9.4% YoY in Q2 2026); BEV transition rocky (MEGA recall cost over RMB 1.1B in Q3 2025, per FY2025 reporting summaries); ROIC not sustained (above).
- A 3 gives CAP = 5.9 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 5.9 years, 15-year projection.

## Flags — every fragility flag resolved
- **STALE_FY** (latest FY ended 10 months ago, 9.6 months in diagnostics): ttm.py cannot compute a TTM for this 20-F filer (exits non-zero, us-gaap path only) — median base used per spec, stated above. The staleness is not ignored: Q2 2026 prints (revenue -15.1% YoY, net loss RMB 1.7B, vehicle margin 9.4%) drove the growth recalibration and the 50% bear-or-worse weight.
- **DILUTION** (share count growing 3.35%/yr, growth adj -3.35%): already deducted via the growth adjustment in the blend. SBC in financials_history.csv was $335,031,000 (FY2023), $360,432,000 (FY2024), $179,856,000 (FY2025) — SBC fell in FY2025 even as the diluted count reached 2,142,727,527, and Q2 2026 reporting cites $631.5M of buybacks, which if continued would reverse the dilution trend; no extra adjustment taken.
- **BASIS_BREAK_SUSPECT** (>60% revenue jump/drop in the last 5 yrs): no divestiture, big M&A or restatement explains it — it is the organic 2020–2023 scale-up (revenue in financials_history.csv rose from $1,449,288,000 in FY2020 to $17,444,096,000 in FY2023 as the L-series ramped). The consequence is exactly the one the flag warns about: the 5y/3y CAGRs straddle that inflection and are unusable as forward growth, which is why scenario starts were recalibrated to recent prints (above).
- **OE_VOLATILE** (OE/share range spans >3x over 5 yrs): real and central — OE went from spike (FY2023, payables-inflated OCF) to normal (FY2024) to negative (FY2025). Handled by the median base plus 50% bear-or-worse probability weight; confidence is low.
- **NI_OCF_DIVERGENCE** (>40% in 2023, 2024, 2025): working-capital / supplier-payables swings, not a tax-deposit one-off. FY2023 OCF $7,140,033,000 vs net income $1,663,280,000 (payables built as volumes surged; maintenance OE in financials_history.csv, $7,881,454,000, vs conservative $5,888,482,000, shows the same WC effect); FY2025 OCF $-1,231,413,000 vs net income $162,935,000 (the build unwound, plus the operating loss and MEGA recall era costs). Because the OCF-based conservative OE is itself the WC-volatile series here, the median — not any single year — is the only defensible base, and the FY2023 spike year must not be treated as run-rate.
- **FX_CONVERTED**: reporting currency CNY converted to USD at period-end ECB reference rates (frankfurter.app); flow facts use the period-end rate, not a period average; the rates used are recorded in dcf_numbers.json fx_conversion.
- **USD_TRANSLATIONS_USED**: the issuer files its own USD convenience translations alongside CNY; the engine used the filed USD figures where they exist.
- **ADS_RATIO_APPLIED**: 20-F filer — per-share figures scaled x2.0 ordinary shares per ADS (Form 20-F for FY2025, filed 2026-04-10: each ADS represents two Class A ordinary shares; judgment.json ads_ratio {ratio: 2.0, source, reason}), so they are comparable to the $11.34 ADS price. Without this scaling the preliminary run's per-ordinary IVs were half the per-ADS values.
- **BASE_DIVERGENCE** (latest FY OE/share $-1.88 vs 3-yr median $0.72): both are distorted in opposite directions, in different ways. The latest year is a trough (negative OCF, operating loss, recall-era costs, WC unwind — above); the median ($0.72, the FY2024 year) predates the Q2 2026 margin cliff, so it likely overstates the current run-rate. The median is used as the through-cycle base, with the overstatement risk carried in the bear weighting and low confidence — not by inventing a lower base, which the bounded override authority does not support from this run's outputs alone.
- **BIG_NET_CASH** (net cash is 4.4x normalized OE; net debt $-6.746B): a large part of the $11.34 price is a cash pile (RMB 87.5B cited at Q2 2026), not the operating business. That cushions downside and funds the BEV/AI pivot without leverage, but it does not make the operating DCF clear 15% — the engine already credits net cash in the IV, and the weighted IV@15% is still $6.06.
- **TIER_BAND** (bear tier D 7.78% vs bull tier C 12.50%): the verdict spans tiers; sized to the bear end — final tier D (below).

## My thoughts / overlay
- The honest headline is the method range: on the preliminary run's stale 31.38% growth blend (and before the ADS scaling), the engine printed a weighted IRR of 13.86% / Tier C; on recent-print growth with the ADS scaling applied, it prints 9.25% / Tier D. Even the super-bull case here (20% start, 1.25x OE, 5% weight) only reaches 16.91% IRR / $13.27 IV@15% — barely above the price. There is no defensible method in this run that gets LI to a B, let alone to 15% weighted.
- Quality has genuinely deteriorated, not just the multiple: gross margin 22.20% → 18.68% → 11.0%, OE from positive 4/5 years to negative in FY2025 and loss-making in H1 2026 reporting, deliveries falling. The offsets are the cash pile, buybacks, scale, and a full model refresh (L-series + i6/i8 + new MEGA/L9) that could stabilize volumes in H2 2026 — that stabilization is the bull case, and it is priced as if it were the base case at $11.34.
- Risks beyond the model: China EV price-war duration, Huawei's distribution/brand in the same segment, BEV execution, VIE/ADS and China regulatory tail risk, and commodity/memory cost inflation management cited in Q2 2026 (it said it would not pass costs to customers).
