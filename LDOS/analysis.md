## What it is
US government technology and services contractor: IT modernization, cyber, intelligence mission support, defense systems (incl. Dynetics hypersonics/air defense), health and civil programs. Revenue is overwhelmingly US-government contracts — fixed-price, cost-reimbursable and time-and-materials — won and re-won through competitive procurement. Q2 FY2026 (quarter ended 2026-07-03): revenue $4.56B, up 7.2% YoY (organic +3.9%, the rest largely the Entrust acquisition), adjusted EPS $3.26, adjusted EBITDA margin 13.8%, bookings ~$4.9B (book-to-bill 1.1; Defense segment 2.2), backlog $48.71B (+5.4% YoY) with funded backlog $10.22B (+44% YoY). Management raised FY2027 guidance at the print: revenue midpoint $18.3B, adjusted EPS midpoint $12.35, operating cash flow ~$1.85B.

## Moat: 5/10 — moderate/narrow
Justification: incumbency, a cleared workforce and a $48.71B backlog are real advantages, but the customer is a monopsonist that recompetes the work, the core product is cleared labor that scaled rivals also supply, and the measured returns only stepped up two years ago.
- FOR: $48.71B backlog (~2.8x TTM revenue of $17,577,000,000, fundamentals.json) with funded backlog up 44% YoY to $10.22B — multi-year revenue visibility few services peers match; incumbency on mission-critical classified programs (Navy SMIT network, intelligence infrastructure) where past-performance ratings and an already-cleared workforce are formal bid discriminators and take years to replicate; scale and portfolio breadth ($17.6B revenue across defense, intelligence, health and civil) that lets it bid primes-sized integrations; mix shift into differentiated defense-tech products is lifting gross margin (13.98% FY2023 → 17.72% FY2026) and the ROIC proxy (fundamentals.json roic_proxy: 9.95% / 10.47% / 9.78% / 3.89% across FY2021–FY2023-era years, then 16.94% FY2025 and 19.10% FY2026).
- AGAINST: the US government is a monopsonist customer that recompetes contracts on price — Leidos has litigated Navy pricing on SMIT, and recompete losses are a standing feature of this sector (Booz Allen, CACI, SAIC, Parsons and the primes contest every award); through-cycle ROIC was ~10% and the FY2023 print was 3.89% — the 17–19% of the last two years is not yet proven through a budget cycle, so it does not support a 7+ score under the rubric; even after the mix shift, gross margin is 17.72% — a services margin, not a product franchise margin; federal budget and efficiency pressure (terminations, insourcing pushes) hits the whole revenue base at once; growth leans on M&A (Entrust) and buybacks rather than organic pricing power — Q2 organic revenue growth was +3.9% against +7.2% reported.
- A 5 gives CAP = 8.5 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 8.5 years, 15-year projection.
