## What it is
Mid-tier gold producer with three operating mines: Côté Gold in Ontario (70%, the flagship — construction through 2023, ramped through 2024–25, now at nameplate), Essakane in Burkina Faso (85%), and Westwood in Quebec (100%). 2026 attributable production guidance is 720,000–820,000 oz (search-cited, company Q2 2026 release). It sells a commodity at a price it does not set.

## Moat: 3/10 — weak; cost/reserve/jurisdiction only, no franchise
Justification: A gold miner is a pure price taker — no pricing power, no switching costs, no network effects; the only candidate advantages are cost position, reserve life and jurisdiction, and IAMGOLD is mid-pack on all three.
- Evidence (fundamentals.json): **ROIC proxy** ran 3.9% (FY2020), −3.7% (FY2021), 1.2% (FY2022), −0.0% (FY2023), 20.1% (FY2024) and 18.5% (FY2025) — below the cost of capital through most of the cycle and positive only when the gold price surged; that is operating leverage to a commodity price, not a franchise return. **Gross margin** over the same span: 20.2%, 7.1%, 15.4%, 12.6%, 33.7%, 42.3% — the expansion is the gold price plus the Côté volume ramp, not pricing power the company controls.
- FOR: Côté Gold (70%, Ontario): large long-life open pit now at nameplate throughput, with the Côté-Gosselin expansion as organic upside; Westwood (100%, Quebec): high-grade underground mine in a tier-1 jurisdiction; Balance sheet transformed post-ramp: net cash at June 30, 2026 with an undrawn revolver (Q2 2026 release), and buybacks begun — 21.5M shares repurchased in H1 2026 (interim statements)
- AGAINST: Pure commodity price taker: earnings are a leveraged function of the gold price; the FY2025 base year was earned at peak-cycle prices; ROIC proxy below the cost of capital through most of the cycle: −3.7% / 1.2% / −0.0% in FY2021–23 (fundamentals.json), positive only in the FY2024–25 gold surge; Mid-pack cost position: consolidated AISC $2,271/oz sold in Q2 2026 (company Q2 2026 release); Essakane (85%, Burkina Faso) supplies roughly half of attributable production — junta and security jurisdiction risk; Depleting assets must be replaced with heavy capex: $907.3M of capex in FY2023 alone; owner's earnings positive in only 1 of the last 5 fiscal years; Dilution record: diluted share count compounding at 6.7%/yr (engine) to fund the Côté build-out
- A 3 gives CAP = 5.9 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 5.9 years, 15-year projection. Moot here — with no positive base there is nothing to fade.

## My thoughts / overlay
- The F is the method working as intended on a hard case: five filed years of owner's earnings contain exactly one positive year, and that year was earned at the top of the gold cycle after a $2.5B-plus construction program whose capex the conservative OE definition correctly charges. Normalizing that away is the point of the median base.
- What the F does NOT capture, stated plainly: (1) FY2025's $840.8M of OE is real cash (OCF $1,142.6M), not an accounting artifact, and H1 2026 interim prints run above it; (2) the balance sheet at June 30, 2026 is net cash (search-cited) versus the engine's $227.9M net debt at FY2025 year-end; (3) the Côté-Gosselin technical report update expected in Q4 2026 (search-cited) is a genuine expansion catalyst. Anyone re-underwriting this name on current numbers at $4,000+ gold gets a higher base — but at a through-cycle gold price the same math cuts the other way, and the filed record through a full cycle is what this pass scores.
- Watch items: gold price (the whole thesis), Essakane's Burkina Faso operating environment, Côté unit costs as the mine moves through pushbacks (Q2 2026 strip ratio 2.8:1, search-cited), and whether the new buyback survives a gold retracement.
- Method range: the defensible bases this run can construct are the 3-yr median (−$0.14/share → F) and the latest FY alone ($1.45/share). Unlike EQX (latest FY $0.17/share), IAG's latest year would support a DCF — but it is a single post-ramp year at peak gold, and the verdict is sized to the normalized base per the sweep's standing method for miners. That gap is the finding; it is flagged here rather than papered over with an override.
