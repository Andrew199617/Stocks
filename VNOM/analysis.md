## What it is
A pure-play mineral and royalty owner, formed by Diamondback Energy to own, acquire and exploit oil and natural gas properties, focused on mineral and royalty interests in the Permian Basin. Viper does not drill: operators bear all drilling, completion and operating costs on its acreage and Viper collects a royalty share of production revenue. At December 31, 2025 it owned mineral and royalty interests underlying 96,003 net royalty acres; proved reserves were 406,035 MBOE (~78% proved developed producing); net production averaged 95,126 BOE/d in 2025 and reached ~134,000 BOE/d in Q4 2025, after the all-equity Sitio Royalties acquisition (~$4.0B) closed August 19, 2025 and the 2025 Endeavor drop-down from Diamondback. Q2 2026 production averaged 65,077 bo/d (134,363 boe/d), and the company raised FY2026 production guidance to 132,500–135,000 boe/d including the Riverbend acquisition (closed July 1, 2026).

## BASE — the Up-C share-basis correction (base_override, bounded authority (c))
- **The defect:** Viper is an Up-C. The engine divides consolidated owner's earnings — 100% of the operating company, including the noncontrolling interest's share — by the filer's tagged diluted share count, which is Class A only. Class A is only about 54% of the economic units: the Class B shares / OpCo units held by Diamondback and other noncontrolling holders receive the same per-unit distributions and are exchangeable 1-for-1 into Class A. On the Class A basis every per-share figure is overstated by roughly the reciprocal of that fraction — the same failure shape as the NTNX/VIV share-scale artifacts, caught here before it shipped: key_numbers.md's measured impact shows the uncorrected TTM base printing a weighted IRR of 25.14% (a fake Tier S).
- **The decision:** judgment.json carries `base_override = {value: 4.143427182330288, source: "this run's ttm.json TTM OE total ($1,488,000,000) divided by total economic units of 359,123,000 = Class A 194,312 thousand + Class B 164,811 thousand shares outstanding at March 31, 2026 (Q1 2026 10-Q statement of changes in stockholders' equity)"}`. A shares_override cannot do this — overrides fill only absent periods and never replace filed values. Cross-check against the company's own per-unit math: Q2 2026 consolidated OCF of $487M (statements.json) over the same unit count is $1.356/unit, against the $1.37 per Class A share of cash available for distribution the company itself reported for Q2 2026 (GlobeNewswire, 2026-08-03) — agreement once the quarter's ~3.0M share buyback is allowed for. The denominator is the March 31, 2026 count, so the Q2 buyback makes the pin ~0.8% conservative.
- **Measured impact (from key_numbers.md, measured on the TTM base actually used, same final configuration with overrides toggled off):** base OE/share $7.97 without the override → $4.14 with it (−48.02%); weighted IRR 25.14% → 15.02% (−10.12pp). Unblocks tier: YES for the gap overrides below; the base_override itself does not unblock — it de-flates. On the engine-native normalized base, the capex/SBC overrides alone move weighted IRR 23.64% → 23.52% (dcf_numbers.json override_impact) — that basis is reported for completeness and is not the scored base.

## OVERRIDES — capex and SBC (filing-sourced, bounded authority (a))
- **The gap:** the preliminary engine run exited 4 with `DATA_GAP: sbc, capex` — neither field is filed under any candidate tag for FY2023–FY2025 (dcf_numbers.json data_gap). No tier was emitted, correctly.
- **capex_override = $0, kind "filing":** the FY2025 10-K Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows (p. 50) accounts for all investing activity in three lines — acquisitions of oil and natural gas interests $(1,549)M / $(696)M / $(830)M, related-party acquisitions $(875)M / $— / $(75)M, and sale proceeds — with no purchase-of-PP&E, fixed-asset or capital-expenditure line in any year. A royalty owner has no maintenance capex: operators fund the wells. The acquisitions are growth investment, not maintenance capex — the same distinction the sweep applied to Royal Gold. Impact is nil (the history already carried assumed-zero); the override converts a silent zero-fill into a sourced, registered decision.
- **sbc_override = $7,000,000, kind "filing":** the 10-K statement of changes in stockholders' equity (pp. 51–52) reports equity-based compensation of $7M (FY2025), $3M (FY2024), $1M (FY2023); the cash-flow statement carries no separate SBC addback (it sits inside the "Other" adjustment), which is why companyfacts has no fact. The engine applies the FY2025 value to all three years, overstating FY2023/FY2024 SBC — conservative, and immaterial at 0.7% of FY2025 OCF.
- Both overrides flag out-of-band mechanically (no filed value exists to anchor the band); each carries an ack_reason stating exactly that, per the TS/RGLD precedent.

## Moat: 6/10 — moderate-strong asset moat, pure price-taker
Justification: the largest pure-play mineral and royalty owner in the Permian — perpetual royalty interests bearing no drilling, completion or operating capex, a symbiotic Diamondback relationship supplying a disproportionate share of development on its acreage, and scale that earns the lowest cost of capital in a fragmented minerals market — but a pure commodity price-taker whose reserves deplete and must be replenished at market prices, with volumes set by third-party operators and essentially all value in one basin.
- FOR: perpetual mineral/royalty interests (96,003 net royalty acres at FY2025) bearing no well capex or lease operating expense — a structural cost advantage no producer can match; scale — largest pure-play mineral company after the Sitio acquisition, proved reserves 406,035 MBOE ~78% proved developed producing; Diamondback symbiosis — ~1,388 gross horizontal wells in active development on its Permian acreage at YE2025 plus ~1,370 line-of-sight wells; cash returns on capital well above the cost of capital before the FY2025 non-cash impairment (fundamentals.json roic_proxy 19.9% FY2023, 16.3% FY2024; operating margin 86.5% / 66.4% in FY2023/FY2024 — the filer reports no gross-profit line, so operating margin is the margin evidence); royalty breakeven far below current prices, so cash generation survives troughs that force producers to cut rigs.
- AGAINST: no pricing power — royalty revenue is a price-taker claim on oil, gas and NGL prices (the FY2025 ceiling-test impairment of $768M, 10-K p. 48/50, is what a price fall does to the book); reserves deplete — holding per-unit production requires continuous acreage acquisition at market prices ($1,549M + $875M in FY2025 alone); volumes depend on third-party operators' (mainly Diamondback's) drilling pace; single-basin concentration and long-run hydrocarbon decline cap the terminal story; the Up-C noncontrolling interest shares consolidated economics ahead of Class A holders, and net debt was $2,173M at FY2025.
- ROIC note, as the rubric requires: fundamentals.json roic_proxy prints −1.7% for FY2025 — that is the $768M non-cash ceiling-test impairment flowing through operating income (−$140M), not a cash return collapse: FY2025 OCF was $1,053M on revenue of $1,346M. The 6 rests on the FY2023–FY2024 cash ROIC history (19.9% / 16.3%) and the zero-capex royalty structure, with the impairment treated as the price-taker risk it evidences — which is also why the score is 6 and not TPL's 7 (TPL is debt-free with surface and water income; Viper carries debt and a single royalty stream).
- A 6 gives CAP = 9.8 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 9.8 years, 15-year projection.

## Flags — every fragility flag resolved

- **DILUTION** (share count "growing 38.6%/yr"): acquisition issuance, not SBC dilution. The filed Class A count rose because Sitio was paid for in stock (Aug 2025), the Endeavor drop-down was paid in units (Q2 2025) and equity was issued for cash ($1,232M in FY2025) — each issuance bought producing acres, and total production rose with it (95,126 BOE/d FY2025 average to 134,363 boe/d in Q2 2026). SBC itself is $7M (0.7% of OCF, by override). The −5.00pp share-count adjustment inside the blend is one more reason the blend was not used: scenario starts were set directly on per-unit economics (see DCF). Going forward the count is shrinking at the margin — ~3.0M Class A shares repurchased in Q2 2026 and a $1B buyback-authorization increase announced with Q4 2025 results.
- **NI_OCF_DIVERGENCE** (2023, 2024, 2025): found, and it is non-cash accounting, not cash quality. FY2025 net income of −$68M against OCF of $1,053M is the $768M ceiling-test impairment plus $607M of depletion (both 10-K, pp. 48/50) — write-downs of the oil and gas property book when prices fell, with no cash effect; FY2023/FY2024 gaps are the same depletion addback at smaller scale ($146M / $214M) plus deferred taxes (−$149M in FY2024, financials_history.csv tax line −$100M). - **WIDE_SCENARIO_SPREAD** (6.87x): real — a commodity-linked royalty stream's outcome range IS wide (super-bear IV@15% $14.64 vs super-bull $100.60). Confidence is held at low, and the verdict is sized to the conservative end.

Leverage is a real bear-case amplifier — royalty OCF falls one-for-one with prices while the debt does not — and it is one reason the bear weight is 25% with a 0.90 multiplier; it is not a solvency concern at current prices.

## My thoughts / overlay
- The method range is the headline. On the engine's own filed share basis this name prints a weighted IRR of 25.14% (Tier S) — a pure Up-C perimeter artifact, since the numerator is consolidated and the denominator is Class A only. On the corrected per-unit base it prints 15.02% (Tier B), and on the base case alone 14.57% — i.e., at $45.02 the market is already paying for the base case in full. Anyone quoting a single VNOM number without its share basis is hiding the ball.
- Quality is genuinely high: zero maintenance capex, SBC at 0.7% of OCF, an 86.5%/66.4% operating-margin history before the impairment year, a dividend funded from royalty cash flow (Q2 2026 base-plus-variable of $0.67 per Class A share; the base dividend was raised 32% annualized to $2.00 from Q3 2026 with the variable component dropped), and net debt falling toward target. None of that is the debate — the debate is commodity price and development pace, which the owner does not control.
- The depletion treadmill is the structural question the DCF can only approximate: reserves are consumed every quarter, and per-unit growth has been bought (Sitio, drop-downs, Riverbend) as much as drilled. The scenario starts assume bought-and-drilled growth nets to +8%/yr per unit in the base case; if acquisition prices rise with competition for Permian minerals, that net figure compresses toward the bear case.
- Hurdle math: the stock clears Andrew's 15% hurdle at the weighted IV@15% of **$45.11** — essentially today's $45.02 price, a 0.2% margin, which is no margin of safety at all. A buyer who wants the bear case covered needs a price at or below the bear IV@15% of $27.34; even the base-case IV@15% ($43.37) sits below the current price.
