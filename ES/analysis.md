## What it is
New England regulated utility holding company: electric distribution and transmission, natural gas distribution, and (until its pending/completed sale) the Aquarion water business, serving Connecticut, Massachusetts and New Hampshire. Essentially all go-forward earnings are regulated after the exit from offshore wind (South Fork Wind and Revolution Wind sold to Global Infrastructure Partners on 2024-09-30, per search results). Management's plan is rate-base compounding: a $26.5 billion capital plan for 2026–2030 focused on electric and gas distribution and transmission, with long-term EPS growth guided at 5% to 7% through 2030 off the 2025 recurring base (search results).

## Moat: 6/10 — durable regulated monopoly, capped economics
Justification: Exclusive regulated electric and natural-gas franchises across Connecticut, Massachusetts and New Hampshire plus New England's largest transmission network, with essential inelastic demand and a $26.5B 2026-2030 capital plan that grows rate base at regulated returns — a durable, visible compounding machine. Capped at 6 because regulators (PURA, DPU, NH PUC, FERC) set the allowed return so there is no pricing power beyond rate cases — FERC lowered transmission ROE to 9.57% in March 2026 — the plan is funded with debt (net debt $28,130M) plus recurring equity issuance (share count +2.25%/yr), and offshore-wind contingent liabilities and affordability pushback in CT/MA sit with the equity.
- FOR: Exclusive regulated electric and gas franchises in Connecticut, Massachusetts and New Hampshire — captive, essential-service customers; Largest electric transmission network in New England, FERC-regulated formula-rate returns; $26.5B 2026-2030 regulated capital plan grows rate base, recovered through rates; Essential, inelastic demand through cycles; pure-play regulated pivot after offshore-wind exit.
- AGAINST: Regulators cap returns — CT PURA, MA DPU, NH PUC and FERC set allowed ROE; FERC cut transmission ROE to 9.57% in March 2026; Growth is debt- plus equity-funded: net debt $28,130M and share count +2.25%/yr dilutes rate-base growth per share; Lingering offshore-wind contingent liabilities to GIP and Aquarion sale losses — charges recurred in 2024, 2025 and H1 2026; High customer rates and affordability politics in CT/MA constrain rate recovery for a $26.5B capital plan.
- A 6 gives CAP = 9.8 years by the sweep formula — academic here, since there is no positive OE stream to fade (key_numbers.md records CAP as None on this F run).
