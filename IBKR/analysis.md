# IBKR — Interactive Brokers Group, Inc.
Run date: 2026-10-13. Sector: Finance / online brokerage. Price $87.89 (Nasdaq quote API, engine). Market cap ~$39.8B on the fully-diluted economic share count (443.9M split-adjusted shares/units, incl. exchangeable IBG LLC units) — just inside the <$40B scope; the listed Class A float alone is roughly a quarter of that.

## What it is
Automated global electronic broker: 5.19M customer accounts at Q2 2026 (+34% YoY), $930B customer equity, access to 170+ markets, revenue = net interest income (largest line; margin loans $108.5B at Q2 2026, +67% YoY) + commissions (DARTs 4.82M in Q2 2026, +36% YoY). Q2 2026: net revenues $1.90B (+28% YoY), pretax margin 77%, adjusted diluted EPS $0.69 (+35% YoY). Accounts +35% YoY to 5.58M by end-September 2026. This is a genuinely elite operating business — the valuation question is separate, and the standardized OE model needs surgery before it means anything here (below).

## Owner's earnings — the engine's raw output is unusable, and why
- **Engine conservative OE (OCF − capex − SBC) is meaningless for a broker-dealer.** FY2025 OCF was $15.81B — that is client-balance plumbing (segregated cash, securities borrowed/loaned, customer receivables/payables), not distributable cash. It swung $3.97B → $8.72B → $15.81B over FY2022-25 with no relation to earning power. The preliminary run's $19.75/share normalized base and Tier S / 37.6% weighted IRR are artifacts and are **rejected in full**.
- **Consolidated net income also overstates the listed equity's earnings ~4.4×.** IBG, Inc. consolidates IBG LLC but owns only ~22-23% of it; the rest is noncontrolling interest (founder Holdings). FY2025: consolidated NI $4,357M, of which NCI $3,373M and **attributable to common stockholders only $984M** (EDGAR companyfacts, tag NetIncomeLossAvailableToCommonStockholdersDiluted; reported split-adjusted diluted EPS $2.22 on 443.86M shares).
- **Maintenance version (engine):** $4,418M consolidated — same NCI flaw. Attributable maintenance OE ≈ attributable NI + attributable D&A (~$14M) − minimal capex ≈ attributable NI. For a broker, attributable net income IS the honest owner's-earnings proxy (capex is trivial: consolidated D&A only $61M; the real "reinvestment" is regulatory capital, handled via scenario multipliers and low confidence, not a capex line).
- **Base used — TTM attributable OE through Q2 2026, from EDGAR 10-K/10-Q YTD figures (same TTM construction as the standard override, applied to attributable NI instead of OCF):** FY2025 $984M − H1 2025 $437M + H1 2026 $579M = **$1,126M**, ÷ 448.37M diluted shares (Q1 2026, split-adjusted) = **$2.51/share**. Cross-checks: sum of reported split-adjusted diluted EPS, FY25 $2.22 − H1'25 $0.99 + H1'26 ~$1.28 ≈ $2.51. Attributable EPS history: FY2023 $1.42 ($600M/423.4M), FY2024 $1.73 ($755M/436.0M), FY2025 $2.22, TTM $2.51. The 3-yr median ($1.73) lags a real compounding inflection (accounts +34%/yr), so the TTM base is used — and it is the *generous* choice: on the median base the IRR would be several points lower. Price = **35.0× TTM attributable OE**; OE yield 2.86%.

## Moat score: 7/10 — strong
CAP = clamp(2 + 1.3×7) = **11.1 years** (growth holds 2 years, then converges to 2.5% terminal over the CAP; 15-yr projection).
- **FOR:** (1) Structural cost advantage from end-to-end automation — 77% pretax margin at $1.9B quarterly revenue is unmatched by Schwab/Robinhood economics and funds both the lowest pro margin rates (benchmark +0.5-1.5%) and continued platform investment; (2) breadth as switching cost — 170+ markets, professional-grade tools, API/prime/advisor infrastructure; pro, institutional and advisor clients are embedded in workflows retail apps don't replicate; (3) scale flywheel in margin lending and securities lending (margin loans +67% YoY to $108.5B) plus global account growth compounding at +34-35%; (4) demonstrated through-cycle profitability and conservative risk culture (customer bad debt $1-2M/quarter).
- **AGAINST:** (1) No consumer brand or distribution moat in mass retail — Robinhood (27.6M funded customers) and Public are compressing margin rates toward IBKR's levels from below; (2) earnings are geared to exogenous variables — policy rates (net interest margin already slipped 2.07% → 1.93% YoY in Q2 2026) and trading volumes, both cyclical; (3) key-man/founder concentration (Peterffy) and the complex Up-C/LLC structure public holders don't control; (4) regulatory and market-structure tail risks (payment-for-order-flow rules, margin regulation, a client blow-up) are uninsurable and binary.

## FLAGS — every fragility flag resolved
- **SECTOR_MODEL_MISMATCH (the controlling flag):** Finance sector. Resolved by replacing the model's cash-flow definition: OCF-based OE discarded (broker-dealer client-balance flows), consolidated NI discarded (77% belongs to NCI), base rebuilt on attributable NI. Confidence capped at **low** per the standing rule, and the tier is soft — a dividend/ROE model would be the right v5 tool. The direction of the verdict, however, is robust: no defensible earnings definition attributable to the listed shares gets IV near $87.89 at a 15% discount.
- **NI_OCF_DIVERGENCE (2024, 2025):** Not a one-off tax deposit or impairment — it is structural for a broker-dealer. OCF includes changes in segregated client cash, securities borrowed/loaned and customer payables; NI does not. The >40% gap is the business model, which is exactly why OCF was removed from the base rather than "adjusted."
- **BASE_DIVERGENCE (latest $35.36 vs median $19.75):** Both engine figures are distorted by the same two defects (OCF base + consolidated totals). On the corrected attributable basis the series is $1.42 / $1.73 / $2.22 (FY23/24/25), median $1.73 vs latest $2.22 — a real, explained gap (account growth +34%, margin balances +67%, high rates), not a one-off. TTM $2.51 used; see above.
- **DILUTION (share count +63.6%/yr):** Artifact, not dilution. IBKR effected a 4-for-1 forward split on June 17, 2025; EDGAR periods before the restated filings carry pre-split counts (~101M in FY2022) while FY2023+ periods in the latest 10-K are split-adjusted (~423-444M), and the engine's per-period tag merge straddles the break. True split-adjusted diluted count growth is ~+1.5-2%/yr (423.4M → 436.0M → 443.9M → 448.4M), i.e. mild stock-comp/LLC-exchange dilution, already inside the reported attributable EPS figures used for the base.
- **OE_VOLATILE (>3× range over 5 yrs):** Also largely the split/OCF artifacts. Corrected attributable EPS grew +22% / +28% / TTM +13% — cyclical (rates and volumes) but not erratic. Cyclicality is handled by the 35% combined bear weight and the below-print base growth start rather than by widening further.
- **STALE_FY (FY ended 9.4 months ago):** Resolved with the TTM override above — FY2025 attributable NI rolled forward with H1 2025/H1 2026 10-Q YTD attributable NI from EDGAR ($984M − $437M + $579M = $1,126M = $2.51/share).
- **WIDE_SCENARIO_SPREAD (6.3×):** Real and retained — a broker's 15-year outcome genuinely ranges from rate/volume winter to continued 30% compounding. Consequence per the rules: confidence is low (already capped by the sector flag), and the verdict is sized to the conservative end.
- No HIGH_NET_DEBT / BIG_NET_CASH flag fired (engine net debt is null — balance-sheet cash/debt tags don't capture a broker's balance sheet; client cash is segregated and is not the firm's). No balance-sheet leverage adjustment is folded into the verdict beyond the sector confidence cap; IBKR's own capital position strengthened in Q2 2026 per its release.

## Thoughts / overlay
- **Method range:** the spread of defensible answers is unusually wide *by construction of the capital structure*, and saying so is the finding. Wrong-but-mechanical engine run: Tier S, 37.6% (reject — counts NCI earnings and client cash flows). Corrected on the 3-yr median attributable base ($1.73): weighted IRR would be roughly 5-6%, deep D. Corrected on the TTM base ($2.51, used here): 9.8%, D. Every defensible variant is D; the final number uses the most generous defensible base.
- Quality is not the debate: 77% pretax margins, +34% account growth, +40% customer equity growth, minimal bad debt. The debate is price: at 35× TTM attributable earnings (~27× forward consensus), the buyer needs the bull path to become the base path and stay there for a decade.
- What would change the answer: a rate/volume drawdown that reprices the stock toward the IV, or several more years of 25%+ EPS compounding delivered at this price (which mechanically raises the TTM base toward the price).
- **Hurdle math:** the stock clears a 15% IRR at ≈ **$46** (weighted IV@15%); with a 20% margin of safety, ≈ **$37**. At $87.89 it clears 15% only in the 10%-weight super-bull world.
