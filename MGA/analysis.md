## What it is
One of the world's largest and most diversified automotive suppliers: Body Exteriors & Structures, Power & Vision (including the Veoneer Active Safety business acquired in 2023), Seating, and Complete Vehicles (contract assembly at Graz, Austria — 4M+ vehicles produced). Sales $42,010M in FY2025 ($42,836M FY2024, $42,797M FY2023), operations in 28 countries, revenue concentrated in North America and Europe and in a small set of large OEM customers (GM the largest single name; Detroit Three plus German OEMs are over 70% of revenue). It sells content per vehicle, not vehicles — its cycle is global light-vehicle production, plus program wins and losses.

Current prints (post-FY): Q2 2026 sales rose 3% YoY to $11.0B against a 2% decline in global light vehicle production; adjusted EPS was a Q2-record $1.86 (+29% YoY); management raised FY2026 guidance to sales of $41.3–42.5B, adjusted EBIT margin 6.3%–6.6%, adjusted EPS $6.70–$7.30 and free cash flow $1.75–1.85B. H1 2026 also carried a $498M impairment on Lighting and Rooftop Systems assets classified as held for sale (the European Lighting sale closed 2026-06-29) — the recurring pattern of program/business charges landing in "other expense" continues. H1 2026 capital returns were heavy: $905M of buybacks (15.0M shares) plus $268M of dividends.

## Moat: 4/10 — weak
Justification: one of the world's largest and most diversified auto suppliers, with design-in switching costs for the life of a vehicle program and a unique complete-vehicle assembly franchise — but the returns do not show a moat. Evidence from fundamentals.json: gross margin was 14.2% (FY2021), 12.3% (FY2022), 13.1% (FY2023), 13.5% (FY2024), 14.3% (FY2025) — thin and cyclical, with no upward pricing-power trend; ROE was 12.8%, 5.4%, 10.2%, 8.8% and 6.6% over the same years. The `roic_proxy` series in fundamentals.json is null for every year because the filer tags no operating-income line, so no computed ROIC history exists to quote; on the computed ROE and gross-margin evidence that does exist, returns sit at or below a plausible cost of capital through the cycle — a 7+ moat score would need exactly the durable above-cost-of-capital ROIC record this filer does not present.
- FOR: scale and breadth (~$42B revenue across four segments; cross-selling and content-per-vehicle gains); design-in switching costs (once engineered into a program, supply runs the program's life; mid-program re-sourcing is costly for OEMs); complete-vehicle assembly at Graz, a capability almost no rival offers, now adding Chinese OEM programs (XPENG, GAC) for Europe; global footprint in 28 countries near OEM plants, plus Veoneer Active Safety ADAS content.
- AGAINST: no pricing power — OEM customers squeeze supplier margins annually (gross margin 12.3%–14.3%, ROE 5.4%–12.8% over FY2021-2025); customer concentration, with >70% of revenue tracing to Detroit Three and German OEMs now exposed to US tariffs; full cyclicality (OE/share $1.25–$7.92 over FY2022-2025); program and customer blow-ups land on Magna ($330M of Fisker impairment and supplier settlements inside FY2024 other expense; the end of Jaguar I-Pace/E-Pace production; the $498M Lighting/Rooftop held-for-sale impairment in H1 2026); capital intensity (capex $1,313M–$2,500M/yr against OCF of $2,095M–$3,634M over FY2022-2025).
- A 4 gives CAP = 7.2 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 7.2 years, 15-year projection.

## Flags — every fragility flag resolved
- **DATA_GAP:sbc**: resolved by the filing override — see OVERRIDE. data_gap_uncovered is empty in the final dcf_numbers.json.
- **ADS_RATIO_APPLIED**: ratio 1.0. Magna is a direct-listed foreign private issuer — the NYSE security is the common share itself (40-F cover page, FY2025, accession 0001193125-26-128771), so per-share figures and the traded price are already on the same basis; no ADS scaling applies.
- **STALE_FY** (latest FY ended 9.5 months ago): ttm.py was run and exits non-zero for this 40-F filer (no annual/quarterly facts under its tag and form handling for ocf, sbc, shares or capex), so no TTM pin exists and the normalized median base is used, per the worker spec's treatment of filers ttm.py cannot read. The post-FY evidence (Q2 2026 record adjusted EPS, raised FY2026 guidance on flat sales, $498M held-for-sale impairment) is mixed and is reflected in the scenario weights rather than a hand-built base.
- **BASE_DIVERGENCE** (latest FY OE/share $7.92 vs 3-yr median $4.90): the latest FY is the distorted one. FY2025 capex was $1,313,000,000 — below FY2025 D&A of $1,547,000,000 and far below FY2023 capex of $2,500,000,000 and FY2024 capex of $2,178,000,000 (financials_history.csv) — and FY2025 OE also rode a working-capital release (ΔNWC −$300,000,000 in the maintenance build). A single year of below-depreciation capex is not a run-rate for a manufacturer that must tool every program; the median ($4.90) is the honest base.
- **OE_VOLATILE** (OE/share range over the last 5 years spans >3x): real and structural — this is a cyclical supplier (OE/share $1.25 in FY2022 to $7.92 in FY2025). Handled by the normalized median base and by widening the bear weight: probabilities 15/30/25/20/10 put 45% on bear-or-worse.
- **NI_OCF_DIVERGENCE** (net income vs operating cash flow differ by >40% in 2023, 2024, 2025, gap exceeding D&A): the gap is mostly non-cash charges concentrated in "other expense, net" — $388M in FY2023, $464M in FY2024 and $736M in FY2025 (FY2025 figure from the Q4 2025 results release; $629M of it landed in Q4 alone). Named items: FY2024 other expense includes $330M of Fisker impairment and supplier-related settlements, $33M of Fisker warrant losses and $31M of Fisker restructuring, partly offset by $196M of deferred-revenue recognition on the same relationship; restructuring charges recur across all three years, and investment revaluations run through the same line. D&A of $1,436M–$1,547M/yr explains the first slice of the NI-to-OCF gap in each year; the other-expense impairments and restructuring explain the rest. These charges depress net income without touching operating cash flow, which is why the OCF-based conservative OE base is not contaminated by them — but their recurrence (Fisker, then Lighting/Rooftop in 2026) is a real economic cost of the model and is one reason the moat is 4, not higher.
- **OVERRIDE_APPLIED:sbc**: see OVERRIDE — gap, decision, source and measured impact are recorded in judgment.json, dcf_numbers.json (overrides_applied, override_impact) and score.json, not just here.
- **WIDE_SCENARIO_SPREAD** (super-bull IV@15 is 4.16x super-bear): real — a cyclical supplier's outcome range is genuinely this wide (FY2022 vs FY2025 OE/share proves it). Confidence is capped at medium, and the verdict below is sized to the conservative end.
- **TIER_BAND** (bear tier D at 8.75% vs bull tier C at 13.87%): the verdict spans tiers and neither end clears 15% — even the bull case (13.87%) sits below the hurdle, and only the 10%-weight super bull (18.65%) clears it. Sized to the bear end in the verdict.
