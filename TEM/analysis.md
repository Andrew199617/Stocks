## OVERRIDE — base_override (share-count unit correction)
- **The error:** Tempus presents weighted-average shares **in thousands** in its filing tables, and its XBRL facts carry that scale: FY2025 diluted shares of 174,264 — i.e. 174,264 thousand. The engine read them as units, producing a normalized base of -$3,932.44/share against a $64.72 price and a market_cap_est of $11,278,366.08 (the real capitalization approaches $12 billion). Proof from this run's own outputs and the filings: FY2025 diluted EPS as filed is -$1.41 (statements.json) on net income of -$245,028,000, which only reconciles at ~174 million shares; the Q3 2025 10-Q cover reports 172,863,470 Class A + 5,043,789 Class B shares outstanding at October 30, 2025; Q2 2026 weighted-average basic shares print 179,917 in the filing's thousands presentation against ~182.4 million shares outstanding at June 30, 2026.
- **Why not a shares_override:** the override mechanism fills only periods where a tag is *absent*; it never replaces a filed value. These values are filed — at the wrong scale for the engine — so the bounded route is a base_override built exclusively from this run's code outputs (authority (c) in WORKER_SPEC step 8; same construction as NTNX).
- **Construction (quoted in full):** base_override = base_oe_normalized_per_share from this run's dcf_numbers.json (-$3,932.4392632609865) ÷ 1,000 = **-$3.9324392632609864/share**. Every OE total leg (OCF, capex, SBC) is in dollars as filed; only the share denominator carried the thousands scale, and all three median years (FY2023 63,306 / FY2024 119,849 / FY2025 174,264) carry it uniformly, so the single ÷1,000 corrects the median exactly. Cross-check: the corrected latest-FY figure is -$2.09/share on OE of -$363,886,000 — consistent with 174,264 thousand diluted shares.
- **Measured impact:** none on the verdict. Without the correction the base is -$3,932.44/share; with it, -$3.93/share. The sign, the Tier F branch, and the f_subtype are identical either way — this override does not unblock a data gap (data_gap is empty) and does not create the tier; it stops a 1,000x-misstated per-share base from being recorded in score.json. It is registered via make_score.py in overrides_registry.json, and it is the judgment call to review hardest in this folder.
