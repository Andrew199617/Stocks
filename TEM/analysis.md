## OVERRIDE — base_override (share-count unit correction)
- **The error:** Tempus presents weighted-average shares **in thousands** in its filing tables, and its XBRL facts carry that scale: FY2025 diluted shares of 174,264 — i.e. 174,264 thousand. The engine read them as units, producing a normalized base of -$3,932.44/share against a $64.72 price and a market_cap_est of $11,278,366.08 (the real capitalization approaches $12 billion). Proof from this run's own outputs and the filings: FY2025 diluted EPS as filed is -$1.41 (statements.json) on net income of -$245,028,000, which only reconciles at ~174 million shares; the Q3 2025 10-Q cover reports 172,863,470 Class A + 5,043,789 Class B shares outstanding at October 30, 2025; Q2 2026 weighted-average basic shares print 179,917 in the filing's thousands presentation against ~182.4 million shares outstanding at June 30, 2026.
- **Why not a shares_override:** the override mechanism fills only periods where a tag is *absent*; it never replaces a filed value. These values are filed — at the wrong scale for the engine — so the bounded route is a base_override built exclusively from this run's code outputs (authority (c) in WORKER_SPEC step 8; same construction as NTNX).
- **Construction (quoted in full):** base_override = base_oe_normalized_per_share from this run's dcf_numbers.json (-$3,932.4392632609865) ÷ 1,000 = **-$3.9324392632609864/share**. Every OE total leg (OCF, capex, SBC) is in dollars as filed; only the share denominator carried the thousands scale, and all three median years (FY2023 63,306 / FY2024 119,849 / FY2025 174,264) carry it uniformly, so the single ÷1,000 corrects the median exactly. Cross-check: the corrected latest-FY figure is -$2.09/share on OE of -$363,886,000 — consistent with 174,264 thousand diluted shares.
- **Measured impact:** none on the verdict. Without the correction the base is -$3,932.44/share; with it, -$3.93/share. The sign, the Tier F branch, and the f_subtype are identical either way — this override does not unblock a data gap (data_gap is empty) and does not create the tier; it stops a 1,000x-misstated per-share base from being recorded in score.json. It is registered via make_score.py in overrides_registry.json, and it is the judgment call to review hardest in this folder.

## Moat: 5/10 — moderate / narrow, data asset real, economics unproven
Justification: one of the largest clinical-plus-molecular oncology data libraries, a diagnostics funnel that keeps feeding it, and a data-licensing layer growing +30.9% — set against zero years of positive owner's earnings, operating losses in every filed year, and no measurable return on capital through a cycle.
- FOR: One of the largest libraries of clinical and molecular oncology data in the world (FY2025 10-K, Item 1, quoted in profile.json) — multimodal data paired with clinical outcomes that compounds with every test run
- FOR: Data flywheel monetized, not just claimed: Data and applications revenue $316,408,000 in FY2025, +30.9% YoY (segments.json), with data licensing / Insights the recurring, higher-value layer
- FOR: Embedded clinical workflow and distribution: partnerships with 2,500+ healthcare institutions including 65% of U.S. academic medical centers and over 50% of U.S. oncologists (company descriptions in search results, 2026)
- FOR: Diagnostics scale with regulatory assets: Diagnostics revenue $955,381,000 in FY2025 (segments.json); FDA-cleared xT CDx companion diagnostic carrying ADLT pricing
- AGAINST: No demonstrated returns on capital: conservative owner's earnings negative in 0 of the last 4 filed years and on TTM through Q2 2026; operating losses every year; the roic_proxy series in fundamentals.json (+14.6% FY2022, +10.0% FY2023, +191.8% FY2024, +176.1% FY2025) is denominator noise on negative-then-thin equity with blank debt tags, not evidence of returns above the cost of capital
- AGAINST: Diagnostics concentration: $955,381,000 of FY2025 revenue (75.1% in segments.json), oncology-centric; Data and applications is only $316,408,000
- AGAINST: SBC is heavy and rising again: $124,747,000 in FY2025 after the $534,138,000 IPO year, $186,145,000 TTM through Q2 2026 (ttm.json) — a recurring tax on owners that alone exceeds TTM free cash flow in magnitude
- AGAINST: Competition on every layer: Guardant Health and Natera in diagnostics, Flatiron Health (Roche) in oncology data, Illumina in sequencing, plus hospital/EHR platforms that own the workflow
- AGAINST: Reimbursement and FDA-clearance dependence: the guided pricing uplift rests on ADLT pricing for xT CDx and an xF approval expected in late 2027 — regulatory outcomes, not contracted revenue
- **Evidence check (spec step 5):** a moat above 6 would need ROIC sustained well above the cost of capital or a written reason the history understates the franchise. Neither is available: the roic_proxy series prints +191.8% / +176.1% in years when operating income was -$691,082,000 / -$252,872,000, because book equity was -$1,382,163,000 at FY2023 (pre-IPO preferred structure) and the engine's debt tags are blank — the series measures a broken denominator, not a franchise. The gross-margin series in fundamentals.json is null for every year (no COGS/gross-profit tag captured), so no five-year margin level or trend can be quoted from the computed outputs; the Q2 2026 earnings release (research-cited) prints gross profit of $246.5 million on revenue of $382.5 million for that single quarter.

- There is no method range to hide behind: corrected median base -$3.93/share, latest-FY base -$2.09/share, TTM base -$2.50/share, uncorrected engine base -$3,932.44/share. Every defensible variant is Tier F. Anyone quoting a DCF value for TEM today is projecting the inflection, not measuring owner's earnings.

- What would change the tier: a filed period in which OCF − capex − SBC is positive, sustained enough to move the 3-year median. On current TTM legs that requires roughly the whole -$449,365,000 TTM OE gap to close — the FY2026 10-K (expected February 2027) is the first filing that could start that, and the Personalis close (more shares, more SBC history) cuts the other way on a per-share basis.
- Quality note for the file: the data asset and the +30.9% data-segment growth are genuine franchise evidence, and Diagnostics gross economics are improving with FDA-cleared pricing. The screen's verdict is narrower than a quality verdict: at $64.72 (capitalization approaching $12 billion) the price pays today for owner's earnings that have never been positive in a single filed year.
