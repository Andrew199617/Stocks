## What it is
Austin, Minnesota branded protein foods company (SIC 2011, meat packing): SPAM, Skippy peanut butter, Planters snack nuts, Jennie-O turkey, Applegate natural/organic meats, Columbus charcuterie, Hormel chili and pepperoni. Three segments — Retail, Foodservice, International. A Dividend King: the fiscal 2025 release noted a 1% quarterly dividend increase marking 60 years of uninterrupted dividend growth. Portfolio is being reshaped: the whole-bird turkey business was slated for sale to Life-Science Innovations, the Brazil operation (Ceratti) was sold / classified held for sale in fiscal 2026, and on 2026-09-30 the company agreed to acquire chicken processor Brakebush Brothers for $1.055B (expected close Q1 FY2027, funded with cash and new debt, ~$1.2B of trailing sales, accretive to adjusted EPS only from FY2028).

## Moat: 5/10 — moderate / narrow
Justification: genuine brands and foodservice relationships, but incomplete commodity pass-through, eroding margins, and ROIC that has fallen to around the cost of capital — no score above the moderate band is justified.
- FOR: Durable branded portfolio with real shelf presence: SPAM, Skippy, Planters, Jennie-O, Applegate, Columbus, Hormel chili and pepperoni; Foodservice channel strength — 12th consecutive quarter of organic net sales growth in Q3 FY2026, premium prepared proteins and branded pepperoni; scale in value-added protein processing and long-standing retail/foodservice relationships, with 60 consecutive years of dividend increases; pricing actions are sticking on an adjusted basis — FY2026 YTD adjusted operating margin +30bp and adjusted EPS +6% through Q3.
- AGAINST: Commodity input costs (pork bellies, beef, turkey, nuts) cannot be fully or quickly passed through — FY2025 gross margin fell to 15.6% from 17.0%; the ROIC proxy fell from 17–21% (FY2016–19) to 8–10% (FY2021–24) and 5.1% in impairment-hit FY2025 (fundamentals.json roic_proxy series), so returns no longer clear the cost of capital by a wide margin; private label and cautious consumers — Retail volume fell ~9% in Q3 FY2026 and organic net sales fell 2% in the quarter; capital-allocation scars — the $3.35B Planters acquisition (2021) was followed by Retail intangible impairments in FY2025, and International minority investments were impaired in both FY2025 and FY2026; exposure to shifting protein tastes with no structural cost advantage over larger packaged-food peers.
- Evidence (fundamentals.json): gross margin over the last five fiscal years ran 16.9% / 17.4% / 16.5% / 17.0% / 15.6% (FY2021–FY2025), down from 19.0–22.7% in FY2016–20; the roic_proxy series over the same five years ran 9.4% / 10.4% / 8.1% / 8.2% / 5.1%. A moat of 7+ requires ROIC well above the cost of capital through a cycle — Hormel no longer shows that.
- A 5 gives CAP = 8.5 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 8.5 years, 15-year projection.
