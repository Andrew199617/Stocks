## What it is
Guangzhou-based Chinese smart-EV maker (SIC 3711, 20-F filer, NYSE: XPEV): battery-electric cars (MONA M03, P7+, P7, G6/G7/G9, X9 including an EREV version) built around an in-house smart-driving stack (XNGP / VLA), the Turing AI chip, and a self-operated fast-charging network, plus high-margin services and other revenue that includes technical R&D services for Volkswagen. Newer bets (humanoid robot IRON, robotics financing in Q2 2026 per search results) are optionality, not yet owner's earnings. Each ADS represents two Class A ordinary shares — see ADS_RATIO_APPLIED below.

## Moat: 3/10 — weak
Justification: Real smart-driving technology differentiation (in-house ADAS/VLA and Turing chip, validated by the Volkswagen partnership) and a large self-operated charging network, but no durable pricing power in a price-war market: vehicle margin was only 12.8% in FY2025, gross margin recovery to 18.9% leans on Volkswagen services revenue, and the ROIC proxy was negative in all of the last 5 fiscal years (-15.8%, -22.9%, -33.0%, -21.2%, -8.5%). A moat of 7+ cannot sit on that ROIC history, so 3/10 (weak).
- Evidence (fundamentals.json, annual): ROIC proxy -15.8% (FY2021), -22.9% (FY2022), -33.0% (FY2023), -21.2% (FY2024), -8.5% (FY2025) — improving, but negative in every year. Gross margin 12.5%, 11.5%, 1.5%, 14.3%, 18.9% over the same years — the FY2023 collapse to 1.5% is what a price war does to a maker without pricing power, and the FY2025 recovery is helped by services mix.
- FOR: In-house ADAS stack (XNGP / VLA) and Turing AI chip — among the leaders in China smart-driving, validated by Volkswagen's technology partnership and R&D-services revenue; Self-operated ultra-fast charging network (3,159 stations at FY2025 per the annual report) supports the brand and ownership experience; Broadened model range (MONA M03, P7+, G7, X9 EREV) drove deliveries +125.9% in FY2025 and overseas expansion is scaling; Technology licensing / services revenue is high-margin and diversifies away from pure vehicle gross profit.
- AGAINST: No pricing power in China's EV price war: vehicle margin only 12.8% in FY2025 despite scale, and buyers switch brands with near-zero cost; ROIC proxy negative in all of the last 5 fiscal years (-33.0% in FY2023 to -8.5% in FY2025, fundamentals.json) — the franchise has never earned above its cost of capital; Scale disadvantage vs BYD and Tesla China in procurement, manufacturing and charging economics; Gross margin is flattered by non-vehicle services (Volkswagen technical R&D services); core vehicle economics remain thin; China consumer / regulatory and VIE/ADS tail risks, plus heavy ongoing AI/robotics R&D spend that must be funded from the car business.
- A 3 gives CAP = 5.9 years (key_numbers.md) — academic here, since there is no positive normalized OE stream to fade.

## My thoughts / overlay
- The FY2025 inflection is real — first positive conservative OE year, first quarterly net profit in Q4 2025, gross margin up to 18.9% — but on this sweep's method it is one year against a negative 3-year median, negative ROIC in all five years, and H1 2026 interim net losses. That is an inflection to verify, not a base to capitalize. Even taking the latest year alone at face value, its OE/share is $0.65 per ADS against a $9.53 price — a thin earnings yield that would need the FY2025 growth rate to persist for years to clear a 15% hurdle, and Q2 2026 revenue growth of 8% YoY says it is not persisting.
- The balance sheet is not the problem: the engine reads net cash of -$660.0M net debt, and the company's reported total liquidity (including short-term investments and time deposits) is materially larger (RMB40.48 billion cash position at June 30, 2026, search results). XPeng can fund several more loss years; whether the car business ever earns its cost of capital is the open question, and the ROIC series says it has not yet.
- Robotics / physical-AI optionality (IRON humanoid, the Q2 2026 robotics financing at a >$6 billion post-money valuation, search results) may be worth something as a sum-of-the-parts matter, but it produces no owner's earnings today and this screen does not capitalize it.
- Hurdle-clearing price: not computable from this model — with no positive normalized OE base there is no IV@15% at any price. No tier override: the numbers tier F is retained.
