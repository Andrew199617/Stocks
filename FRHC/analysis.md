## What it is
Nevada-incorporated, Nasdaq-listed holding company built on a Kazakh brokerage, now a diversified financial ecosystem operating in 24 countries: brokerage (~858,000 customer accounts at March 31, 2026, +26% YoY, more than 56% funded — reported), Freedom Bank Kazakhstan (banking combined assets $5,359.8M, loan portfolio $2,045.3M, deposits $2,522.8M at FY2026 — reported), life and general insurance (Freedom Life, Freedom Insurance), payments, telecom and lifestyle services (ticketing, travel, e-commerce), all bundled into the Freedom SuperApp (~2.6M monthly active users in March 2026 per S&P's rating release — reported). Total ecosystem customers 8,743,000 at June 30, 2026 (reported). Founder/CEO Timur Turlov controls the company. SIC 6211 (security brokers/dealers) in fundamentals.json, whose profile also records the former names BMB MUNAI INC and INTERUNION FINANCIAL CORP — the listed vehicle began as an oil-company shell, which is where the early-history breaks come from. No dividend (fundamentals.json dividends: frequency "none").

Latest prints (reported): fiscal Q1 2027 (ended June 30, 2026) revenue $732.5M, +40% YoY, with brokerage revenue +60% and banking +54% — but net income fell to $31.7M ($0.52 diluted EPS) from $37.4M a year earlier as expenses rose to $691.7M from $476.5M. In July 2026 the company sold 2,374,356 new shares for ~$300M gross at ~$126.35/share and completed the acquisition of 99.32% of Turkish Bank A.Ş. (both reported).

## BASE OVERRIDE — measured impact
- **The decision** is recorded in fields: judgment.json `base_override = {value: 2.5087236673636744, kind: "filing", source: median construction quoted verbatim above from financials_history.csv}` and in the central registry (_system/overrides_registry.json, event "base_override", 2026-10-18). score.json carries base_source "agent_supplied_ttm_override" (the engine's label for a --base-oe-ps pin).
- **Measured impact:** the override replaces a base that cannot support any tier at all — on the engine-native normalized base (-$1.33/share) the preliminary run printed Tier F with no scenarios, no IV and no IRR (implied_irr_weighted null). On the NI-median base the same model produces the full scenario set below and Tier D (weighted IRR 5.16%). The override moves the verdict from "unmeasurable / spurious F" to a measured D; it does not flatter the stock — every scenario on the corrected base still values it far below the $181.57 price.

## Moat: 4/10 — weak-to-moderate; real ecosystem, unproven franchise economics
Justification: Kazakhstan's #3 financial group and a genuine SuperApp ecosystem, but it trails Kaspi.kz and Halyk Bank at home, switching costs are low, it buys deposits on price, and the return history does not prove a franchise — ROE (fundamentals.json) fell to 6.2% in FY2025 and 10.3% in FY2026 from 26.4% (FY2023) and 32.6% (FY2024), i.e. at or below the cost of equity in the two most recent years, after early-scale years (54.0% FY2021, 41.1% FY2022) that were flattered by a tiny equity base. fundamentals.json carries no roic_proxy for this filer (null in every year — a finance presentation with no usable operating-income/debt split for the proxy), and its gross-margin series (~96–99%) is meaningless for a net-revenue financial, so the moat evidence is the ROE series above plus market position. A 7+ moat needs ROIC well above the cost of capital through a cycle; Freedom shows the opposite pattern lately — scale first, returns later, maybe.
- FOR: Integrated SuperApp ecosystem bundling brokerage, banking, insurance, payments and lifestyle — ~2.6M monthly active users in March 2026 (S&P, reported), cross-sell across 8,743,000 ecosystem customers at Q1 FY2027 (reported)
- FOR: Leading Kazakhstan brokerage: ~858,000 customer accounts at FY2026 (+26% YoY), >56% funded (reported)
- FOR: Real insurance niches per National Bank of Kazakhstan data in the FY2026 reporting: Freedom Life ~19.3% of pension annuity premiums and ~7.4% of voluntary accident insurance; Freedom Insurance ~14.53% of car-owner liability premiums, ~6.55% of the general insurance market by assets (reported)
- FOR: Top-3 Kazakhstan financial group by market capitalization, behind only Kaspi.kz and Halyk Bank (reported); S&P upgraded core subsidiaries to BB- and revised the outlook to positive in 2026 (reported)
- FOR: Loyalty program converts cashback into fractional FRHC shares — customers are literally invested in the platform (reported)
- AGAINST: Only #3 in its home market: Kaspi.kz and Halyk Bank run larger ecosystems with bigger MAU and lending volumes (reported); Freedom is the challenger, not the incumbent standard
- AGAINST: Returns do not prove a moat: ROE 6.2% (FY2025) and 10.3% (FY2026) — at/below cost of equity — and net income ranged $76,287,000–$379,107,000 over FY2024–26 (financials_history.csv / fundamentals.json)
- AGAINST: Regulatory hostage in its core market: a Kazakhstan legislation change on borrowers' life insurance cut net insurance revenue 29% in FY2026 (reported); every segment is a licensed, policy-sensitive business
- AGAINST: Governance/regulatory overhang: SEC Wells Notice to the company and CEO Timur Turlov in March 2026 (reported); the 2023 Hindenburg allegations (sanctions evasion, related-party FST Belize) were never fully put to rest and a Russian prosecutor lawsuit naming the company and Turlov was filed in October 2026 (reported); CFO Evgeny Ler resigned June 25, 2026 (reported)
- AGAINST: Low switching costs and price competition: digital brokerage and deposits are rate-shopped — Freedom Bank's top tenge deposit pays 19.5% AER (reported); founder control concentrates key-man and related-party risk
- A 4 gives CAP = clamp(2 + 1.3 × 4) = **7.2 years**: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 7.2 years, 15-year projection.

## FLAGS — resolving every fragility flag
The final dcf_numbers.json carries exactly seven fragility flags; each is resolved here:
- **BASIS_BREAK_SUSPECT** (revenue jumps >60% in the last 5 years): real, and it is segment construction, not organic compounding. Revenue went $83,393,000 (FY2020) → $346,883,000 (FY2021) → $689,790,000 (FY2022) → $1,647,256,000 (FY2024) → $2,191,291,000 (FY2026) in financials_history.csv as the banking and insurance segments were built/licensed from near zero, brokerage scaled, and the lifestyle businesses (Aviata/ChocoTravel, Ticketon, Arbuz.kz — acquired 2023, reported) were bolted on; the pre-2018 history is a different company entirely (BMB Munai shell; FY2017 revenue $0). Consequence: the 44.59%/39.96% revenue CAGRs describe construction, not a steady-state growth rate — scenario starts were recalibrated off earnings prints instead (see DCF), and no CAGR is trusted as a growth input.
- **NI_OCF_DIVERGENCE** (2024, 2025): structural, not a one-off — it *is* the sector defect. FY2024 net income $379,107,000 vs OCF -$1,064,362,000; FY2025 net income $76,287,000 vs OCF +$1,681,058,000 (financials_history.csv). For a broker-bank the OCF statement carries deposit growth, customer payables, margin/securities balances and trading-book purchases/sales; those balance-sheet flows dwarf earnings in both directions. There is no tax deposit or impairment to excuse — the divergence is why the base is net income (see BASE OVERRIDE). The earnings-side story inside the window is separately real: FY2025 net income fell to $76,287,000 from $379,107,000 as insurance economics deteriorated ahead of the Kazakhstan borrowers'-life-insurance legislation change that cut net insurance revenue 29% in FY2026 (reported).
- **SHARES_FALLBACK** (no diluted count filed for 2017): noted — FY2017 is the shell era (11,213,926 shares outstanding on the fallback tag in financials_history.csv) and its per-share history is approximate; it touches nothing in the last-3-FY base, whose legs are all filed diluted counts. Checked further: the FY2021/FY2022 rows of financials_history.csv print diluted counts of 58,442,921,000 / 59,378,207,000 — three orders of magnitude off the ~59–61M counts filed for FY2023–FY2026 — so per-share history for those two years (OE/share $0.0090 / -$0.0071 as printed) is not usable either; the totals are. The base construction uses only FY2024–FY2026 legs, which are clean and consistent with the Q1 FY2027 YTD count of 61,269,787 (ttm.json) and the 63,787,666 shares outstanding at 2026-08-07 (dcf_numbers.json, post-offering).
- **DEBT_TAGS_BLANK** (no debt tag for the latest FY — net debt unknown, not zero): stated from the filing instead. FY2026 balance sheet (financials_history.csv): total assets $13,155,239,000; total liabilities $11,665,960,000; equity $1,489,279,000; cash $966,115,000 and restricted cash $1,246,312,000. The liabilities are predominantly customer deposits (banking deposits $2,522.8M at FY2026, reported), amounts due to customers/brokers, and debt securities issued — the filer does not report them under the engine's standard debt tags, so the engine's net debt is null and no leverage adjustment is made in either direction. For a regulated financial group the meaningful leverage statement is capital, not net debt: equity is 11.3% of assets on the filed totals, and S&P rates the core subsidiaries BB- (positive outlook, reported). No leverage verdict beyond that is claimed.
- **SECTOR_MODEL_MISMATCH** (Finance): accepted in full — it is the controlling flag. Confidence is **low** by rule, score.json records model_fit "misfit" and alert suppression for sector misfit, the OCF base was replaced with the sector's honest proxy (BASE OVERRIDE), and the tier is a screen result, not a bank valuation. A financial's distributable earnings are additionally bounded by regulatory capital, which this model does not compute.
- **LOW_OE_YIELD** (base OE yield 1.4%): fires and is the verdict in miniature — at $181.57 against a $2.51 base, essentially all of the price is growth expectation; the scenario set prices that growth honestly and still lands at $34.56 weighted. The tier is fragile to the growth assumption in the *upward* direction only in degree, not in kind: even the super-bull case (31% start, 1.25x) yields an 8.20% IRR.
- **WIDE_SCENARIO_SPREAD** (5.24x): real — earnings ranged $76M–$379M across the last three fiscal years and the regulatory outcomes are genuinely binary-ish. Confidence is low (also mandated by the sector flag), and the verdict is sized to the conservative end, which here changes nothing: bear and bull tiers are both D.

## My thoughts / overlay
- The honest headline is the *gap between two true statements*: Freedom is a real, GAAP-profitable, fast-growing financial ecosystem (revenue $2,191,291,000 in FY2026, TTM net income $154,592,000), AND at $181.57 the market price capitalizes it as if the FY2024 peak earnings ($6.3863/share) were the base and ~30% compounding were assured. On the median earnings the company actually printed over the last three years, the weighted IV@15% is $34.56 — the price is more than 5x that figure, and it is also a large multiple of the $24.37 book value per share for a group earning a 10.3% ROE.
- Method range: the engine-native OCF bases (latest FY, 3-yr median, TTM — all negative) print a spurious F, which says something true about the model's fit and nothing true about the company; the NI-median base prints D at 5.16% weighted IRR. Pinning the FY2024 peak NI/share instead would print higher, but FY2024 was the pre-legislation insurance peak and is not a defensible base. Every defensible variant agrees on the investable point: at $181.57 this does not clear a 15% hurdle; it clears 15% only at ~$35 on the weighted path.
- What the screen cannot price, stated plainly: the Wells Notice outcome (March 2026, company and CEO — reported), the October 2026 Russian prosecutor lawsuit (reported; the company says it holds no Russian assets), and whether the Turkey/Europe expansion converts the ecosystem playbook abroad. Those are reasons the left tail got 15% super-bear weight, not reasons to override the arithmetic.
- SBC trend, for the record: $59,592,000 (FY2025) → $68,047,000 (FY2026) filed, $68,228,000 TTM (ttm.json) — a real and growing cost, already inside the NI base.
