## What it is
South Africa's largest gold producer by volume: deep underground mines in South Africa (Mponeng, Moab Khotsong, Tshepong, Doornkop, Kusasalethu and others), surface/tailings retreatment (Mine Waste Solutions), the Hidden Valley mine in Papua New Guinea, and a new copper leg in Australia — the CSA mine (acquired with MAC Copper, effective 24 October 2025) plus the approved Eva Copper project. FY2025 gold production was 1,479,671 oz, down 5% YoY; FY2026 (reported, year ended 30 June 2026) was 1,429,551 oz with 18,207 t of copper from CSA — the 11th consecutive year of meeting production guidance.

## Moat: 3/10 — weak; a commodity franchise, not a moat
Justification: a gold miner has no pricing power and Harmony's returns prove it: roic_proxy was negative in four of the last nine years and only exceeds the cost of capital when the gold price rescues it (22.7% FY2024, 37.4% FY2025 at record prices); gross margin swung −15% to +33% on price alone. The 3 rests on scale, the high-grade Mponeng/Hidden Valley cash engines, a net-cash balance sheet, and the CSA/Eva copper pivot — real assets, but a commodity franchise, not a moat.
- ROIC evidence (fundamentals.json, ZAR filings, roic_proxy): FY2017 −2.8%, FY2018 −12.2%, FY2019 −7.3%, FY2020 −1.1%, FY2021 16.3%, FY2022 −1.9%, FY2023 13.9%, FY2024 22.7%, FY2025 37.4%. Gross margin over the same years: −1.9%, −15.4%, −7.3%, 11.4%, 15.0%, 1.7%, 19.8%, 23.0%, 32.8%. Both series are the gold price, not a franchise: a moat ≥7 requires ROIC held well above the cost of capital through a cycle, and Harmony fails that test in every down-leg.
- FOR: scale — South Africa's largest gold producer by volume, ~1.4–1.5 Moz/yr, guidance met for the 11th consecutive year in FY2026; high-grade cornerstone assets (Mponeng, Hidden Valley) generate a disproportionate share of free cash flow; net-cash balance sheet at FY2025 funds the copper pivot without dilution; copper diversification — CSA producing (18,207 t in FY2026) plus Eva Copper approved ($1.55B, first production H2 2028) toward ~100 kt/yr combined.
- AGAINST: pure price taker — earnings are the gold price minus AISC; through-cycle ROIC is mediocre (see above); depleting, deep, old South African underground mines — production fell 5% in FY2025 and was flat in FY2026; South Africa operating risk (power, labour, safety stoppages — fatal incidents still reported in FY2026 6-Ks); growth is bought, not organic (Mponeng 2020, Eva 2022, MAC Copper/CSA 2025).
- A 3 gives CAP = 5.9 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 5.9 years, 15-year projection.

## Flags — every fragility flag resolved
- **STALE_FY** (latest FY ended 16 months ago): real and unresolved by construction. ttm.py cannot run on this IFRS filer (see Owner's earnings), so the base is the filed FY2023–25 median. The newer information exists outside EDGAR XBRL: FY2026 results were furnished on Form 6-K on 27 August 2026 (revenue R99.2B, +34%; HEPS R43.63, +87%; gold 1,429,551 oz; CSA copper 18,207 t) — but the FY2026 20-F is not yet filed, so no filing-direct OE base can be built from it in this pass. This name is a prime refresh candidate the moment the FY2026 20-F lands; confidence is capped at low partly for this reason.
- **BASIS_BREAK_SUSPECT**: the >60% revenue jump is FY2021 (revenue $1,684.5M → $2,915.5M in the USD history), caused by the Mponeng Acquisition — with effect 1 October 2020 Harmony acquired the remainder of AngloGold's South African business, including the Mponeng mine and Mine Waste Solutions (stated in the FY2025 20-F). Pre/post-FY2021 CAGRs straddle a bigger company, which is one more reason the blended growth was recalibrated rather than taken at face value. The MAC Copper acquisition (effective 24 October 2025) is a second, forward break: FY2026 reported figures consolidate CSA copper for the first time.
- **OE_VOLATILE**: real cyclicality, not an inflection — OE/share ran −$0.08 (FY2019) to $0.44 (FY2021) to $0.06 (FY2022) to $0.90 (FY2025) in the USD history, tracking the gold price. Handled with the normalized median base plus bear-tilted probabilities (45% bear-or-worse).
- **NI_OCF_DIVERGENCE** (2023, 2024): operating cash flow exceeds net income in both years (FY2023 NI $257.8M vs OCF $525.3M; FY2024 NI $477.1M vs OCF $859.3M, financials_history.csv) — the normal miner pattern of large non-cash charges in net income (D&A $182.4M in FY2023 and $254.9M in FY2024 on the same history, plus impairment/derivative-type non-cash items), not missing cash. The conservative OE is OCF-based throughout, so the divergence does not inflate the base; if anything the NI-based maintenance leg understates cash generation in those years.
- **FX_CONVERTED**: reporting currency is ZAR; the engine converted at period-end ECB reference rates (recorded in dcf_numbers.json — e.g. 0.05624 USD/ZAR at 2025-06-30). Flow facts use the period-end rate, not a period average; noted as a translation caveat on every USD figure here.
- **USD_TRANSLATIONS_USED**: the issuer files its own USD convenience translations alongside ZAR; the engine used the filed USD figures where present. Noted; no separate adjustment.
- **ADS_RATIO_APPLIED**: resolved via judgment.json `ads_ratio` (kind: filer's own 20-F) — the FY2025 Form 20-F cover states the ADSs are "each representing one ordinary share" (HMY, NYSE), so ratio 1.0 and per-share figures are directly comparable to the $17.12 ADS price with no scaling.

The median is the through-cycle figure and is used; the cost of that choice is stated plainly in Thoughts: it makes this score a normalized-earnings verdict, not a spot-gold verdict.

## My thoughts / overlay
- The honest headline is the method range, and it is unusually wide because the base year is doing all the work. Three computed views: (1) this final run — median base, recalibrated growth — weighted IRR 7.01%, Tier D; (2) the preliminary engine run — same median base but the engine's unadjusted 20.80% momentum blend — weighted IRR 11.26%, IV@15% $11.30, Tier C; (3) any spot-gold view (FY2026 reported HEPS of R43.63, ~$2.73 as reported in USD coverage) implies earnings power far above both — but it capitalizes a +35% realized gold price as if permanent, which is exactly the assumption a normalized DCF exists to refuse. Even view (2) does not clear 15% at $17.12.
- What the market is actually pricing at $17.12 is the gold price staying near record highs plus the copper growth story (CSA + Eva toward ~100 kt/yr). That may happen — but it is a commodity bet, not an owner's-earnings discount: on through-cycle earnings the stock clears Andrew's 15% hurdle only below $5.99.
- Balance sheet is not the problem: net cash at FY2025 (−$627.0M net debt), and FY2026 reported results show the MAC Copper acquisition absorbed with only modest net debt (R852M as reported). The problem is purely that normalized OE/share of $0.61 against a $17.12 price is a 3.57% OE yield for a depleting, price-taking asset.
- Watch item: the FY2026 20-F (due around 31 October 2026 on the filer's pattern) will force a refresh — the median base will roll to FY2024–26 and mechanically rise. That refresh, not this score, is where a spot-gold bull case would have to make its stand with filed numbers.
