(2) Recruiting engine and culture — #1 in J.D. Power employee advisor satisfaction four straight years (company release); 181 advisors added in 2025 including experienced hires from B. Riley. (2) Exogenous gears — asset-based fees ride equity-market levels; bank/sweep net interest income rides policy rates and deposit mix. (4) Supervision/legal overhang — the Roberts structured-products cases forced a $180.0M provision in Q1 2025 (−$1.16/share pre-split EPS impact, company) and related claims remain open; that is a franchise-controls risk, not just a cost line.
