## What it is
Pre-revenue, single-asset biotechnology company (London HQ, Nasdaq-listed ADSs) developing COMP360, a proprietary synthetic psilocybin formulation, as a supervised, in-clinic treatment for treatment-resistant depression (TRD). Two Phase 3 TRD trials (COMP005, COMP006) met their primary endpoints — 39% of COMP006 participants achieved a ≥25% MADRS reduction at Week 6, with benefit maintained on average through Week 26. A rolling NDA submission is underway with final modules expected Q4 2026 and a targeted U.S. launch in H1 2027 — subject to FDA approval and DEA/state rescheduling of a Schedule I substance. A late-stage PTSD program is the only other material pipeline leg. FDA Breakthrough Therapy designation and a Commissioner's National Priority Review voucher shorten the review clock but do not guarantee approval. There has never been any product revenue: revenue is $0 in every fiscal year in the EDGAR history.

- FOR: First-mover / only late-stage psilocybin program — two positive Phase 3 TRD trials
- FOR: Breakthrough Therapy designation + Commissioner's National Priority Review voucher; rolling NDA already in review
- FOR: Patent estate on synthetic COMP360 formulation, manufacture and use, plus regulatory exclusivity if approved
- FOR: Trained-site / delivery-network know-how overlapping interventional-psychiatry infrastructure
- AGAINST: Active molecule is a known natural compound — moat covers formulation/protocol, not the molecule
- AGAINST: Differentiated rivals can route around it (shorter-session compounds, e.g. Lilly/AtaiBeckley and AbbVie/Gilgamesh programs)
- AGAINST: No pricing power demonstrated — no approved product, no payer contracts; supervised multi-hour session reimbursement unproven
- AGAINST: Delivery model is a structural cost disadvantage vs daily oral drugs — certified site + monitored session per dose
- AGAINST: Schedule I status — DEA and state rescheduling sit between FDA approval and revenue

## My thoughts / overlay
- This is the screen working as intended: CMPS is not an owner's-earnings investment at any price — with negative OE in every period, there is **no price at which the OE DCF clears a 15% hurdle**, and no margin-of-safety price to quote. Anyone buying at $11.42 is buying the probability-weighted pipeline: FDA decision on a rolling NDA completing Q4 2026, DEA rescheduling, payer coverage for a supervised multi-hour session, and uptake against Spravato's installed base and incoming Lilly/AbbVie-backed rivals.
- What would change the tier: approval plus 2–3 years of post-launch filings showing revenue covering the ~$179M/yr operating spend (FY2025 operating income -$179,037,000, financials_history.csv). Until the first positive-OE year exists, CMPS stays F in this sweep regardless of trial headlines.
- Method range: unusually, there is none — latest-FY, 3-yr-median, and filing-derived TTM bases all give Tier F. The spread in this stock's value lives entirely outside the OE method (approval probability × peak sales), which is the finding.
- The one judgment call in this run is the capex override, and it is the weakest kind of judgment call there is: a $0 the filing's own cash-flow reconciliation proves, with zero measured impact on the base. It exists so the v5 gate's paperwork matches the filing's presentation, not to move the verdict — the verdict is F with or without it on any zero-fill math.
