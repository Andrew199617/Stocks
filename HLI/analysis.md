## What it is
Global independent investment bank built on the mid-market: Corporate Finance (M&A and capital solutions — the majority of engagements are mid-cap transactions the bulge brackets underserve), Financial Restructuring (the No. 1 global restructuring franchise by deal count, eleven years running), and Financial & Valuation Advisory. No. 1 global M&A advisor by transaction count. The three-segment mix is the story: restructuring and valuation work is countercyclical ballast when M&A fees fall. Pure people business — no lending book, no balance-sheet risk, essentially no funded debt.

Revenue by fiscal year (financials_history.csv): FY2022 $2,269,958,000 → FY2023 $1,809,447,000 (deal freeze) → FY2024 $1,914,404,000 → FY2025 $2,389,416,000 → FY2026 $2,617,516,000 (record). Latest quarter, Q1 FY2027 (ended 2026-06-30, per the company's Q1 release): revenue $511 million vs $605 million a year earlier, diluted EPS $1.15 vs $1.42 — Corporate Finance fell to $303 million from $398 million on delayed larger-fee closings (Middle East instability, software-sector disruption as AI repriced tech deals); management calls the headwind temporary, says delayed deals remain in the pipeline, and guides the FY2027 adjusted compensation ratio to the 61.5% long-term target.

## Moat: 6/10 — moderate, the top of the band
Justification: No. 1 global M&A advisor by deal count and No. 1 global restructuring franchise, with a three-segment mix (Corporate Finance, countercyclical Financial Restructuring, Financial & Valuation Advisory) that kept ROIC at 24% or better in every one of the last five fiscal years including the FY2023–24 deal trough (24.0–34.5% across FY2023–26; FY2022 printed 72.9% on a small net invested-capital base) — but it is a people business: no contracts or switching costs per mandate, managing directors can walk, and a ~61.5% compensation ratio means the bankers capture most of the economics.
- FOR: No. 1 global M&A advisor by transaction count and No. 1 global restructuring franchise (11 years) — scale and reputation in the mid-market segment bulge brackets underserve; countercyclical mix: restructuring and valuation work offsets M&A downturns (FY2023 revenue fell 20% while M&A froze, and the firm stayed solidly profitable); ROIC proxy 24.0–34.5% across FY2024–26 and 29.8% even in trough FY2023 — returns stayed well above cost of capital through the cycle; asset-light, debt-free balance sheet: $1,189,454,000 cash at FY2026 against no filed funded debt — no balance-sheet risk to the franchise; gross margin held in a 29.8–35.0% band over FY2022–25 despite the cycle — fee pricing power in its niches.
- AGAINST: People business: clients hire individuals, not the platform — managing-director departures take revenue with them and there are no contracts or switching costs per mandate; bankers capture the economics: adjusted compensation ratio guided to the 61.5% long-term target for FY2027, capping shareholder margin leverage; deal fees are cyclical and closing-dependent: Q1 FY2027 Corporate Finance revenue fell to $303M from $398M on delayed larger-fee closings (Middle East instability, software-sector disruption); intense competition from bulge brackets moving downmarket and boutiques (Evercore, Moelis, Lazard) moving into the mid-market; stock compensation is large and rising ($201,304,000 in FY2026) — a real, recurring cost of retaining the talent that is the moat.
- Evidence (fundamentals.json `roic_proxy`, after-tax operating income over equity + debt − cash): FY2022 72.9% (peak operating income on a small net invested-capital base), FY2023 29.8%, FY2024 24.0%, FY2025 31.4%, FY2026 34.5% — never below 24% in any year, trough included; that through-cycle return record is what carries the score to 6 rather than the 5 the closest scored peer (Evercore) carries. Gross margin (fundamentals.json): FY2022 35.04%, FY2023 30.79%, FY2024 29.83%, FY2025 32.32%; FY2026 gross profit is not tagged in the filing feed (fundamentals.json carries no FY2026 gross margin), so the margin evidence stops at FY2025 — operating margin FY2026 was 20.13% vs 21.03% in FY2025.
- A 6 gives CAP = 9.8 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 9.8 years, 15-year projection.

## Flags — every fragility flag resolved
- **OE_VOLATILE** (OE/share spans >3x over 5 years — in fact $−1.06 to $9.33 across FY2022–26): real and structural — this is a deal-cycle business with a fixed people cost base. Handled as the flag directs: the normalized median base (not the FY2025 peak, not a single year) plus bear-end weighting — 45% of probability sits on bear-or-worse, super-bear/bear multipliers cut to 0.65/0.85.
- **NI_OCF_DIVERGENCE** (FY2025: net income $399,711,000 vs OCF $848,609,000): the one-off is compensation timing on a fee surge, not earnings quality. statements.json's FY2025 cash-flow lines identify non-cash stock compensation of $168,443,000 and depreciation & amortization of $41,270,000 added back to net income, plus a $55,698,000 inflow from receivables collection; the balance of the gap is the accrued-compensation build on FY2025's +24.81% revenue year — bonuses accrued at March 31, 2025 and cash-paid in Q1 FY2026, a build the standardized statements do not break out as a separate line. Consequence: FY2025's $9.33 OE/share is flattered by timing, FY2026 partly reverses it (OCF $704,131,000 on net income $425,697,000), and the 3-year median base is the right antidote — one more reason the base is the median, not FY2025.
- **DEBT_TAGS_BLANK** (no debt tag for the latest fiscal year — net debt unknown to the engine): resolved from the filing (statements.json / fundamentals.json). At FY2026 the balance sheet carries cash of $1,189,454,000 (financials_history.csv), total assets of $4,309,000,000, total liabilities of $1,856,000,000 and equity of $2,342,377,000; long-term debt is filed as none for every year FY2023–26, no debt was issued or repaid in any of those years' cash flows, and the liabilities are operating items — accrued compensation, operating leases (right-of-use assets $407,000,000), deferred tax liabilities $175,401,000 and other long-term liabilities $151,000,000 — plus goodwill of $1,396,000,000 from the acquisition program. Verdict: no funded debt; the firm is in a large net-cash position and leverage is not a risk factor. The engine's blank is a tagging artifact (the small LongTermDebt balances of FY2017–21, last $55,864,000, disappear from the feed), not hidden borrowing.
- **SBC_TAG_RECONCILED** (FY2017 only: AllocatedShareBasedCompensationExpense $45,059,000 used over ShareBasedCompensation $39,357,000, +14.5%): one line, as the flag allows — FY2017 is far outside the base window, and the engine uses the Allocated tag consistently in the years that matter (FY2026 SBC $201,304,000); no effect on the base.
- **SECTOR_MODEL_MISMATCH** (Finance sector): confidence is low, as required. But the misfit here is the generic sector tag, not the insurer/bank failure mode: HLI takes no deposits, holds no policyholder float and runs no loan book, so OCF is simply collected fees minus cash compensation and tax — the OE construction is meaningful, and no float-softening tier notch is applied (the same treatment the sweep gave Evercore; the notch is reserved for filers whose OCF capitalizes other people's money, with the gap quantified). The genuine model limits are the ones handled elsewhere: OE volatility (above) and SBC as a real cost (already deducted in full).
- **WIDE_SCENARIO_SPREAD** (super-bull IV 4.29x super-bear IV): real — the outcome range for a cyclical fee business IS wide. Confidence capped at low; the verdict is sized to the bear end.
- **TIER_BAND** (bear tier D at 7.32% vs bull tier C at 12.04%): the verdict is sized to the bear end — Tier D stands, and even the bull case does not clear the 15% hurdle at this price.
