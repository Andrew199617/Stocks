## What it is
Life-science tools: instruments, consumables and software for single-cell and spatial biology — Chromium (single cell), Visium and Xenium (spatial), plus the new Atera spatial platform launching through H2 2026. Razor/blade model: Q2 2026 revenue was $151.0M, of which consumables were $130.8M (Single Cell $88.5M, Spatial $42.3M, +16.2% YoY), instruments $7.7M and services $10.7M (Q2 2026 release, via search). Customers are academic, translational and biopharma labs, so demand rides research budgets.

## Moat: 5/10 — moderate/narrow, real platform, unproven economics
Justification: integrated Chromium/Visium/Xenium installed base with recurring consumables, workflow switching costs and a court-validated patent estate, capped at 5 because returns on capital are negative in every measured year and gross margin compressed ~16pp from its peak before recovering.
- FOR: largest installed base in single cell and spatial — Chromium, Visium and Xenium as one integrated instrument + consumables + software ecosystem; razor/blade pull-through (Q2 2026 consumables $130.8M of $151.0M revenue, Spatial consumables +16.2% YoY); patent estate that wins in court (2026 Delaware jury: three Scale Biosciences single-cell patents valid and willfully infringed by Qiagen's Parse unit, 14% royalty); high gross-margin structure (69.1% FY2025, 70.0% TTM in fundamentals.json, ~74% in Q2 2026 per the Q2 release); workflow/data switching costs — lab protocols, pipelines and published datasets standardize on 10x formats.
- AGAINST: no returns on capital yet — the roic_proxy series in fundamentals.json is negative every year 2021–2025 (-17.9%, -22.6%, -54.9%, -42.0%, -14.9%) and operating income is negative in every filed year, so there is no ROIC-above-cost-of-capital evidence for a higher score; gross margin compressed from 84.9% (2021) to 66.2% (2023) before recovering to 69.1% (2025) / 70.0% TTM — pricing power is contested; real competition (Bruker CosMx ex-NanoString, Vizgen MERSCOPE, Akoya in spatial; Parse/Qiagen and Illumina in single cell); SBC consumes the cash ($98.7M TTM vs $127.1M TTM OCF) and the diluted count grows 3.1%/yr with no buyback; growth has stalled to low single digits ex-settlement and demand is academic/government-budget exposed.
- A 5 gives CAP = 8.5 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 8.5 years, 15-year projection.

## My thoughts / overlay
- The honest headline is the method range: Tier F on the normalized 3-yr median base (-$1.22/share) and Tier F on the TTM base ex-release (≈ -$0.33/share); only the as-filed TTM base (+$0.18/share, IV@15% $1.68) prints a D, and it is entirely a settlement-receivable collection. All variants agree on the only decision-relevant fact — at $71.95 the market is pricing in an OE trajectory (sustained double-digit growth with SBC falling toward zero as a share of OCF) that no filing period has yet printed.
- The cost inflection is real (SBC has fallen 41% from its FY2023 peak and OCF has improved for three straight periods), but the earnings inflection is a settlement collection, not run-rate OE — and even the as-filed TTM owner's earnings of $22,695,000 support a market cap of $8.975B only at ~395x TTM OE (a ratio of the two quoted figures, stated for scale). Q2 2026 also showed the fragility: instruments fell 47.2% YoY as Xenium customers paused ahead of Atera, and FY2026 guidance of $620M at the midpoint is a headline decline.
- Balance sheet is not the risk: no funded debt, $473,966,000 of combined cash at FY2025 ($502,512,000 cash + $49,521,000 marketable securities at Q2 2026 per the Q2 release), lease liabilities $84,361,000. The risk is purely the price versus the OE base, plus academic-budget exposure and spatial competition (Bruker, Vizgen, Akoya, Illumina's entry, Parse/Qiagen).
- What would change the verdict: scale, not direction. The model is linear in the base: IV@15% of $1.68 on the $0.18 as-filed base reaches the $71.95 price only at ≈ 43x the base (≈ $7.55/share, ≈ $974M of OE — OCF of ≈ $1.08B at the current SBC/capex run-rate). SBC falling below ~$50M/yr while OCF holds above ~$150M would roughly quadruple the as-filed base (≈ $0.73/share, IV@15% ≈ $7) — a genuine inflection, but still Tier D, not a verdict change. Only Atera driving a return to double-digit revenue growth with OE leverage compounds toward the 43x; neither is in the filings yet.
