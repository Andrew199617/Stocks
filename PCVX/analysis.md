## What it is
Clinical-stage vaccine company (San Carlos, California; founded 2013 as SutroVax, a Sutro Biopharma spin-out) with no product on the market and no revenue in any filed year. Everything rides on VAX-31, a 31-valent pneumococcal conjugate vaccine built on a cell-free protein synthesis platform — the broadest-spectrum PCV in the clinic, aimed at Pfizer's Prevnar 20 (20-valent) and Merck's Capvaxive (21-valent). On October 5, 2026, VAX-31 met all prespecified primary immunogenicity and safety endpoints in OPUS-1, its pivotal adult Phase 3 trial (4,047 adults) — non-inferior on the serotypes shared with Prevnar 20 and Capvaxive, superior on its unique serotypes, safety similar to comparators (company release, cited search results). OPUS-2 and OPUS-3 readouts are targeted for H1 2027, a manufacturing consistency study is still ahead, and the BLA is planned for H1 2028. VAX-24 is superseded as the lead; VAX-A1 (Group A Streptococcus, Phase 1, first participant dosed June 2026) and VAX-GI (Shigella, preclinical) are early optionality.

## My thoughts / overlay
- There is no method range to hide behind here: TTM base -$7.36/share, latest-year-only -$5.94/share, 3-yr median -$4.59/share. Every defensible variant is Tier F. Anyone quoting a DCF value for PCVX today is projecting approval and launch economics, not measuring owner's earnings.
- The bull case is clinical and real, and it strengthened after the period the financials describe: OPUS-1 met every primary endpoint on October 5, 2026 against both incumbents' vaccines, analyst probability-of-success estimates were raised on the print (Guggenheim to 95% for adults, cited search result), and the company immediately raised ~$1B (~$500M equity at $64 plus ~$500M of converts) to fund manufacturing scale-up with Lonza and commercial readiness (cited search results). None of that is owner's earnings: over the same twelve months the company burned $881,233,000 of operating cash and paid out $150,508,000 in stock compensation.
- The risk stack from here is execution, not discovery: OPUS-2 / OPUS-3 readouts in H1 2027, the lot-to-lot manufacturing consistency study (a delay there delays the BLA regardless of clinical success — cited search result), an H1 2028 BLA, and then a launch against Pfizer and Merck, who own the adult PCV recommendation and the sales infrastructure. Meanwhile the burn is accelerating (Q2 2026 net loss $284.3M, +71% year over year) and each financing round dilutes — share count +28.0%/yr.
- What would change the tier: first an approved product and filed revenue, then a filed period in which OCF − capex − SBC is positive. On the company's own timeline that cannot be a near-term event — BLA in H1 2028 at the earliest, launch economics after that. The FY2026 10-K and the OPUS-2/3 readouts are the refresh triggers.
- Quality note for the file: the broadest-spectrum PCV in the clinic, a positive pivotal readout against both incumbents, $2,507.7M of cash and investments at June 30, 2026, and a platform with follow-on candidates are genuine assets. The $63.62 price and $8.658B market cap pay for their conversion into approved, sold product — with no margin of safety measurable in OE terms, because there are no OE.
