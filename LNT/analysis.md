## What it is
Madison, Wisconsin-based regulated utility holding company providing electric and natural gas service in Iowa and Wisconsin through Interstate Power & Light (IPL) and Wisconsin Power & Light (WPL). Essentially all earnings are regulated. Management's plan is rate-base compounding: a $13.4B capital plan for 2026-2029 guided to grow rate base from $15.3B in 2025 to $26.2B in 2029, with 2026 ongoing EPS guidance of $3.36 to $3.46 per share, a long-term EPS growth target of 5-7%+ (7%+ for 2027-2029), and a 2026 dividend target of $2.14 per share (search results). Data-center load is the growth driver: 5 executed electric service agreements, 3 GW+ contracted, with a 2-4 GW pipeline and demand expected to rise ~60% by 2031 (search results).

## Moat: 5/10 — moderate regulated monopoly, capped economics
Justification: Exclusive regulated electric and gas franchises in Iowa and Wisconsin (Interstate Power & Light, Wisconsin Power & Light) with captive, essential-service demand and a rate base guided from $15.3B in 2025 to $26.2B in 2029 on a $13.4B 2026-2029 capex plan — a durable, visible compounding machine. Capped at 5 because regulators in Iowa and Wisconsin set the allowed ROE and recovery so there is no pricing power beyond rate cases, measured ROIC has stayed low at 4.0-6.0% over 2020-2025 (fundamentals.json roic_proxy), and the plan is funded with debt (net debt $11.560B) plus recurring equity issuance (share count +0.87%/yr).
- FOR: Exclusive regulated electric and gas franchises in Iowa and Wisconsin — captive, essential-service customers (IPL and WPL); $13.4B capex plan 2026-2029 guided to grow rate base from $15.3B (2025) to $26.2B (2029), largely recovered through rates; Contracted data-center load: 5 executed electric service agreements, 3 GW+ contracted, expected to lift demand ~60% by 2031; Long dividend history — 17 growth years in fundamentals.json, DPS TTM $2.0575, 2026 target $2.14/share.
