# BIDU — Baidu, Inc.
v5 run, 2026-10-14. All figures below are copied from key_numbers.md / dcf_numbers.json, financials_history.csv, ttm.py output, or the company's own FY2025 Form 20-F — none are recomputed.

## What it is
China's incumbent internet search company, in a forced transition to AI: Baidu General Business (search and AI search, online marketing, AI Cloud, AI applications, Apollo Go robotaxis) plus a controlling stake in the iQIYI streaming platform. It reports in CNY under US GAAP on Form 20-F; the Nasdaq instrument is an ADS, each representing eight Class A ordinary shares (FY2025 20-F cover and EPS table). In scope on the universe screener (preliminary engine run, before the shares override, put the estimated market cap at $29,033,759,392) — see the market-cap note under Flags.

## OVERRIDE — shares (filing) and ADS ratio
- **The gap:** the preliminary engine run exited 4 with DATA_GAP:shares — diluted shares missing for fiscal years 2023, 2024 and 2025 of the last 3. Recent share counts are tagged in the filing by share class, and the aggregated companyfacts drop dimensioned facts, so the engine filed no share value for any period in its 2016–2025 history.
- **The decision (bounded worker authority, kind "filing"):** judgment.json carries shares_override = 2,744,000,000 — the diluted weighted-average number of Class A and Class B ordinary shares for FY2025 printed in the FY2025 20-F's Consolidated Statements of Comprehensive Income (the same table gives 2,798 million for FY2024 and 2,837 million for FY2023). The engine applies this single value to every missing period (periods_filled: all of 2016–2025 in overrides_applied), so the 3-yr median base is the FY2024 OE total on this count — close to, and slightly more conservative than, the same year on its own filed count, because the count has been shrinking under buybacks.
- **Out-of-band, acknowledged:** the engine flags the override out of band because its band anchors on a stale filed value of 34,917,835 from an old share structure, which is not a comparable anchor for the current ordinary-share count; judgment.json carries an ack_reason stating exactly that. The value itself is a filing figure, not an estimate.
- **Measured impact (dcf_numbers.json override_impact):** binary — with no override there is no base and no tier at all (base_oe_per_share_zero_fill and weighted_irr_zero_fill are both null, unblocks_tier). With it, the base is $3.32 per ADS and the weighted IRR is 6.27%.
- **ADS ratio:** judgment.json carries ads_ratio = 8, sourced from the same 20-F (cover page, Introduction, and the EPS table all state each ADS represents eight Class A ordinary shares). Per-share figures were scaled x8 so they are comparable to the $81.86 ADS price (flag ADS_RATIO_APPLIED).

## Moat: 5/10 — moderate, eroding
Justification: China's incumbent search engine with a full AI stack (ERNIE models, AI Cloud/GPU cloud, Apollo Go robotaxis) and fortress liquidity, but the search-ad moat is eroding fast: advertisers and attention are shifting to short-video and super-app platforms, AI search itself cannibalizes ad monetization (management deliberately delayed it), cloud competes with Alibaba/Tencent/Huawei on price, and China/VIE/regulatory overhang is structural. AI-powered business reached 50% of Baidu General Business revenue in Q2 2026 and AI Cloud Infra grew 50% year over year, yet total revenue fell 4% and online marketing fell 19% in the same quarter — the new engines are not yet outrunning the legacy decline.
- FOR: Incumbent China search distribution and advertiser base, now being rebuilt as AI search (ERNIE Assistant daily users +83% YoY in Q2 2026)
- FOR: Full-stack AI position: ERNIE foundation models, AI Cloud Infra revenue +50% YoY in Q2 2026 with GPU Cloud +283% YoY, plus Kunlunxin chips
- FOR: Apollo Go robotaxi scale: 1 million fully driverless rides in Q2 2026, cumulative rides over 23 million, expanding internationally
- FOR: Fortress liquidity: cash and investments of RMB283.1 billion at June 30, 2026 (Q2 2026 results), funding AI capex, a $5B buyback through 2028 and a first dividend policy without balance-sheet stress
- FOR: Buyback tailwind: diluted weighted-average ordinary shares fell from 2,837M (FY2023) to 2,744M (FY2025) in the 20-F EPS table
- AGAINST: Legacy search advertising is in structural decline: online marketing services revenue -19% YoY in Q2 2026 (-22% in Q1 2026), as budgets shift to Douyin/short video, Xiaohongshu and super-apps
- AGAINST: AI search cannibalizes its own ad model: management deliberately delayed AI-search monetization, and total revenue fell in FY2025 (-3% in RMB) and again in Q1/Q2 2026
- AGAINST: Cloud and models are contested, not owned: Alibaba, Tencent, Huawei and ByteDance compete on price and distribution; ERNIE's model lead must be re-earned each generation
- AGAINST: China/VIE/regulatory overhang: VIE structure, PRC data and platform regulation, US ADS delisting tail risk, and a 2026 US securities class action over AI-revenue disclosures
- AGAINST: Controlled, cash-hungry subsidiaries and investments: iQIYI revenue -5% YoY in Q2 2026 with a working-capital deficit disclosed in the 20-F; FY2025 group operating cash flow turned negative
- A 5 gives CAP = 8.5 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 8.5 years, 15-year projection.

## Flags — every fragility flag resolved
- **DATA_GAP:shares (preliminary run)** — resolved by the filing-sourced shares_override and ads_ratio above; final run has data_gap_uncovered empty and overrides_applied covering the gap.
- **STALE_FY** (FY ended 9.4 months ago): ttm.py could not compute a TTM base (its exact failure message is quoted under Owner's earnings — it found no annual facts under its matching and refused to zero-fill), so the median base was used and the reason is stated there. The post-FY quarterly evidence (Q2 2026 revenue -4% YoY, online marketing -19%) points down, not up, so a fresher base would not rescue the verdict.
- **BASE_DIVERGENCE** (latest FY OE/share -$7.80 vs 3-yr median $3.32): the latest year is the distorted one — FY2025 operating cash flow was -$431,000,000 after a working-capital collapse (next flag), while FY2023–24 conservative OE was positive. The median is the defensible normalized base; using the latest year alone would be a trough base, and even the median fails the hurdle by a wide margin.
- **NI_OCF_DIVERGENCE** (2023, 2025): 2025 — net income was positive ($799,000,000) while operating cash flow was -$431,000,000; the 20-F's own liquidity discussion attributes the operating-cash-flow drop primarily to a large decrease in working-capital changes year over year (summarized, in the filing's MD&A). 2023 — net income ($2,861,000,000) sat well below operating cash flow ($5,157,000,000), consistent with the filing's non-cash charges that year, including share-based compensation ($894,000,000 in the conservative OE bridge) and equity-method investment losses reported in other income. Resolution: the OCF-based conservative series is used throughout, so neither year's net-income figure contaminates the base — but the 2025 divergence is also a real warning that reported profit did not convert to cash.
- **OE_VOLATILE** (OE/share spans >3x over 5 years): real — the conservative OE total went from $2,687,000,000 (FY2023) to -$2,674,000,000 (FY2025). Handled by the normalized median base, a 45% combined weight on bear/super-bear, and confidence low.
- **WIDE_SCENARIO_SPREAD** (super-bull IV 4.4x super-bear): real, and it does not change the verdict — every scenario, including super bull at $53.00, sits below the $81.86 price. Confidence is low, not high.
- **FX_CONVERTED**: the reporting currency is CNY; the engine converted CNY-only periods to USD at period-end ECB reference rates (rates recorded in dcf_numbers.json fx_conversion) — flow facts use the period-end rate, not a period average, which is a documented simplification and one reason filed-USD revenue growth (+1.21%) differs from the RMB print (-3% for FY2025).
- **USD_TRANSLATIONS_USED**: where the issuer files its own USD convenience translations alongside CNY, the engine used the filed USD figures — the base rests partly on those translations.
- **ADS_RATIO_APPLIED / OVERRIDE_APPLIED:shares**: see OVERRIDE above — gap, decision, band acknowledgement, and measured (binary) impact are all recorded in judgment.json, dcf_numbers.json and the override registry.
- **Market-cap note (not an engine flag):** the final engine run's market_cap_est ($224,623,840,000) multiplies the ADS price by the ordinary-share count, overstating the economic cap by the 8x ADS ratio — the same convention seen on other ADS names in this sweep. The universe screener figure from the preliminary run ($29,033,759,392) is the economically meaningful cap and is under $40B; judgment.json's in_scope_under_40b is set to match the final engine figure, as make_score.py requires, and this note is the record of the discrepancy. Scope does not affect the tier.

## My thoughts / overlay
- This is not a close call, so there is no meaningful method range to report: the normalized median base gives Tier D (6.27%), the latest-FY base is negative (an F-style trough), and even the 5%-probability super-bull case — a 13.21% OE/share start at 1.25x the base — yields only 10.90% IRR with an IV@15% of $53.00, still well under the price. No defensible input set on this engine clears 15% at $81.86.
- The investment debate about Baidu is a sum-of-the-parts / cash debate (the RMB283.1 billion of cash and investments at June 30, 2026, Apollo Go, Kunlunxin), not an owner's-earnings debate: on the standardized OE screen, FY2025 cash earnings collapsed, and the Q2 2026 prints (revenue -4%, online marketing -19%, AI-powered business 50% of general-business revenue) show the transition still subtracting more than it adds.
- Red flags sized into the verdict: the FY2025 OCF turn negative; a 2026 US securities class action alleging the AI-revenue cushion was overstated (an allegation, not a finding — noted, not treated as fact); iQIYI's working-capital deficit in the 20-F; SBC still a real and material cost line every year.
- Price that clears 15%: the weighted IV@15% is $24.86 — Baidu clears Andrew's 15% hurdle only below about that price on these inputs, and a margin of safety would require a price well below it, since even the super-bull IV@15% is $53.00.
