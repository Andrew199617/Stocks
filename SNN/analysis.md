## What it is
Diversified medtech in 100+ countries across three franchises: Orthopaedics (hip/knee implants, trauma & extremities, CORI robotics), Sports Medicine & ENT (arthroscopy, joint repair) and Advanced Wound Management (ALLEVYN dressings, PICO single-use negative-pressure therapy, SANTYL bioactives). FY2025 revenue $6,164M (+6.1% reported YoY, key_numbers.md / financials_history.csv). Market cap: the engine's $25.37B estimate multiplies the per-ADS price by the ordinary share count; on the ADS basis (price ÷ 2 × 877,724,845 ordinary shares outstanding) the cap is roughly $12.8B — in scope under $40B either way.

## Moat: 5/10 — moderate / narrow
Justification: leading positions and real switching friction, but returns on capital have not cleared the cost of capital through the cycle and the margin trend is adverse.
- FOR: #2 global positions in sports medicine and advanced wound care; top 3–4 in orthopaedic reconstruction, sold in 100+ countries; proprietary platforms and materials (OXINIUM implants, CORI handheld robotics, PICO single-use NPWT, ALLEVYN dressings, REGENETEN bioinductive implant); surgeon training, clinical evidence and installed instrument sets create real switching friction; three-franchise diversification balances weakness in any one segment.
- AGAINST: the `roic_proxy` series in fundamentals.json ran 7.1% (2021), 5.6% (2022), 4.9% (2023), 7.0% (2024), 8.1% (2025) — below or near the cost of capital through the cycle; the company's own >10% post-tax ROIC is only a 2026 target and 12–13% a 2028 target. Gross margin (fundamentals.json) eroded from 74.0% (2019) and 70.4% (2021) to 68.0% (2025) on pricing pressure, mix and China volume-based procurement. Larger rivals' robotics ecosystems (Stryker Mako, Zimmer ROSA, DePuy VELYS) outscale CORI, and US knee implants lost ground in 2026 (Q2 2026 US knee revenue −7.2%, per the H1 2026 report coverage). Hospitals and GPOs have high bargaining power and demand proven cost-effectiveness.
- A 5 gives CAP = 8.5 years: growth holds 2 years, then converges linearly to the 2.5% terminal rate over 8.5 years, 15-year projection.

## My thoughts / overlay
- The honest headline is how far the price sits above every defensible path: even the super-bull case (13.15% growth start, 1.25x OE) values the ADS at $20.56 @15%, below the $29.06 price, and even re-running the stack on the latest-FY base ($1.8534/ADS instead of the $1.2922 median) would raise the weighted IV@15% materially but still leave it far below the $29.06 price — still a Tier D. The D is not a base-choice artifact.
- Quality is middling, not broken: OE positive 5/5 years, SBC trivial, gross margin still 68%, trading margin recovering (18.3% in H1 2026, FY2025 trading profit +15.5% per company reporting), and management is returning cash ($500M buyback). The problem is the price capitalises the RISE 2028 targets (6–7% revenue CAGR, 12–13% ROIC) as if delivered, while the filing history shows ROIC of 4.9–8.1% and a guidance cut two months ago.
- Risks to the bearish verdict: a takeout (SNN is a perennial rumoured target for larger medtech), faster CORI/LANDMARK adoption, or RISE margin delivery ahead of plan. Risks to the company: US knee share, China VBP pricing, tariff exposure, and the inventory-rationalisation programme slipping.
- Margin of safety: there is none at $29.06 on these numbers. The ADS clears a 15% hurdle only at or below the weighted IV@15% of $11.42; it clears 10% only below $19.30.
